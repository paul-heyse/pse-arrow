# Proto capabilities and integrated codebase improvements

## Combined judgment

**Proposed:** retain the owner-derived native/Proto/Arrow composition, but make its next
deliveries operation-shaped: executable scientific semantics, atomic worker admission,
complete preparation/evidence workflows, and useful independent authoring and result readers.
Generation should remove repeated interpretation and preparation rather than become the
organizing model for the simulator.

Plan65 already contains the essential distinctions. Its remaining opportunity is to make
them govern complete consumers and credible extensions. This review recommends concrete
amendments to that undertaking, including a source-established worker lifecycle hazard and
new alternatives for generation composition. It also challenges current functional and
deployment exclusions where a useful consumer warrants it. Broader deployment remains a
proposal, not a prerequisite to finish local scientific delivery.

Any breaking change is eligible. Scientific meaning, exact values, original effects, physical
settlement and independent evidence remain obligations. Neither existing implementation effort
nor a preferred library stack settles a target-design choice.

## Scientific meaning and extension

<a id="I01"></a>
### I01 — Make operation and product coordinates adequate for the next workflow

**Current approach.** Native `DeclaredExecution`, `PreparedStudyOperation` and
`RuntimeOperationRequest` already compose meaningful operations. `Completion` owns
scientific permission rather than letting exporters recompute it. This is a stronger foundation
than a universal wire operation enum. However, current product scope is deliberately narrower
than the future workflow breadth: `ProductScope::governing` refuses a nonempty member, and
task-derived permission is specific to fit/statistical families. A point/step label alone
cannot describe every experiment, trajectory interval, spatial member or selected accuracy attempt.

**Amendment.** Extend product and operation contracts at their scientific owners for a finite
new consumer population: a non-water mixture/phase regime, reacting transient operation, and
shared-parameter fit across experiments. Model the necessary source, material/phase convention,
initial/boundary/observation roles, derivative capability, validity, coordinate selection,
assessment and governing attempt. Generated observations expose those relationships; they
do not construct permission. A new physical phenomenon can require a new core concept;
an ordinary case/binding should reuse the existing operations.

Make a scheduled-input fit's target explicit. Current integrated fitting intentionally binds
the first interval while later schedule intervals retain authored values. Preserve that
interpretation for current requests. A whole-schedule offset, selected interval, experiment-local
initial state and shared physical parameter are different possible targets; add only the
selected useful targets with executable admission and derivative contracts. Do not silently
generalize first-interval behavior because all targets happen to be numeric.

Use the existing compiler/preparation dependency owners to bind phase/branch, data/datum,
provider and derivative contexts to the products or reusable artifacts they actually affect.
Semantic identity, descriptor change, physical representation and live handle invalidation
remain separate. A result that is readable or locally valid must not authorize a different
member or nonselected attempt.

**Mechanisms and sources.** Native owners:
[`declared.rs`](../../crates/pse-runtime/src/workflow/modeling/declared.rs),
[`operation_preparation.rs`](../../crates/pse-runtime/src/workflow/operation_preparation.rs),
[`completion.rs`](../../crates/pse-runtime/src/workflow/completion.rs),
[`integrated.rs`](../../crates/pse-runtime/src/workflow/integrated.rs) and
[`fitting/modeling.rs`](../../crates/pse-runtime/src/workflow/fitting/modeling.rs).
Authored property and conservation definitions remain the physics authority.
Buffa 0.9.2 typed options/reflection and generated adapters can expose new coordinates and
capabilities; `ReflectMessage::for_each_set` inspects generated values without a dynamic
encode/decode bridge. [Reflection characterization](https://github.com/anthropics/buffa/blob/v0.9.2/buffa-descriptor/src/reflect/message.rs).
Mathematical and native solver libraries retain differentiation, integration and numerical iteration.

**Alternatives and closure.** Retaining the current restricted product scopes is sound for
their supported population; explicitly refuse unsupported requests. Prefer operation-shaped
references over one universal simulator ontology or a covariance-specific permission rule
copied into each reader. Migrate the producer, completion assessment, storage projection and
independent reader together. Challenge with a usable endpoint but unusable intermediate sample,
two experiments sharing a parameter, a changed phase branch, and an unsupported spatial model
missing temporal closure. Independent physical expectations and receiving-side refusals are
needed; generated agreement cannot establish scientific adequacy.
This advances AP-03–AP-06, DP-02/DP-08/DP-09 and PS-01/PS-02/PS-07/PS-11/PS-12.

Two additional distinctions make this extension concrete. Provider shapes already carry axis,
coordinate-tuple and scalar-cell identities in
[`pse-kernels::shape`](../../crates/pse-kernels/src/shape.rs). Their structural checks cannot
establish that those axes denote the receiving operation's selected components/phases.
Bind that correspondence at receiving scientific adoption. Reordered species require an
explicit correct mapping or refusal; adding a species may require new pair records and data
closure. Scalar providers should not acquire irrelevant material-system metadata.

Also expose phase/branch selection provenance separately from local regularity and established
thermodynamic stability. Existing
[`nested-equilibrium.pse`](../../packages/reference/thermodynamics/models/nested-equilibrium.pse)
and [`phase-stability.pse`](../../packages/reference/thermodynamics/models/phase-stability.pse)
are distinct authored capabilities. Preserve provider `RegimeCrossing` and linearization-token
context; a selected regime does not authorize differentiation through its boundary. A phase-aware
study reader can show regime changes and unavailable stability evidence without a new flash
algorithm. An intentionally metastable operation is the countercase to a blanket stable-phase
requirement. New phase-envelope/global-stability algorithms remain separately selected science.

## Runtime and developer lifecycle

<a id="I02"></a>
### I02 — Commit queue admission with the job, not with the observing RPC

**Observed source hazard.** In
[`scientific_worker/transport.rs`](../../crates/pse-runtime/src/scientific_worker/transport.rs),
`Server::exchange` mutates `HandleControls::admit` before awaiting `jobs.send`.
Prepare inserts a handle before submission; cancellation or a failed send can leave control
state without a submitted preparation. The native loop also breaks when the response oneshot
receiver is gone. That branch retires the shared lane and its sessions, even though loss of
one response waiter is not an explicit Shutdown command. Exact deadline/reset reachability
has not been fault-probed; the ordering and branch consequences are source-established.
Installed ConnectRPC 0.9.1 `service::with_request_deadline` uses Tokio `timeout_at`
around gRPC unary dispatch, supplying a method-future cancellation route.
[Deadline wrapper](https://github.com/connectrpc/connect-rust/blob/v0.9.1/src/service.rs).

**Amendment.** Reserve queue capacity before committing preparation admission, then submit
the job without an intervening cancellable wait. Roll back any unpublished provisional state
on definite non-submission. Preserve immediate Stop/Shutdown cancellation where it must
interrupt active FFI; don't delay control signaling behind the native job queue.
After submission, native execution and bookkeeping own settlement. Distinguish a departed
response observer from explicit session/process shutdown. An abandoned Prepare must either
remain recoverable by its original identity or be closed by its performing owner after
settlement; merely ignoring a failed acknowledgement would leak an unreachable session.

Use Tokio 1.53.3 `mpsc::Sender::reserve_owned`/`OwnedPermit::send` for capacity ownership,
with the existing native `Job` permit retained through actual computation. Oneshot delivery
reports observation delivery, not a command to terminate unrelated science.
[Queue API](https://docs.rs/tokio/1.53.3/tokio/sync/mpsc/struct.Sender.html#method.reserve_owned),
[owned permit](https://docs.rs/tokio/1.53.3/tokio/sync/mpsc/struct.OwnedPermit.html).
The existing connection-wide parent-loss/indivisible-FFI containment policy remains distinct
and may intentionally retire every session.

**Closure.** Exercise cancellation while waiting for queue capacity, definite send failure,
lost Prepare acknowledgement, and a lost evaluation waiter while another handle on the same
live connection remains usable. Also verify actual connection/parent loss still drains or
contains native work and retains charges until reap/I/O join. Keep all independent cleanup
errors. Replace the two incidental lifecycle branches with explicit native outcomes and
delete their old expectations only after the replacement controls pass.
This corrects an AP-05/DP-19/DP-20 risk within the existing E04/D05 footprint.

<a id="I03"></a>
### I03 — Make prerequisite preparation independently usable and complete

**Current approach.** The native runner first discovers the actual test population, matches
authored fixture declarations, and calls `PreparedExecution::prepare`. That function returns
on the first missing original environment input. `NativeLane` is an environment-selected
executable; scientific inputs are a separately prepared capsule. The interrupted 87-test
selection exposed these prerequisites sequentially. This is truthful refusal, but an explicit
preparation workflow can provide a better complete operation without implicit startup.

**Amendment.** Extend existing fixture requirement/readiness owners to return all missing
prerequisites, their owning preparation operation and required retained outputs in one
structured account. Before discovery, narrower test-scoped requirements remain conditional;
after the original inventory is available, report exact requirements. Do not reject a pure
selection merely because another test in the target may need a database or solver.

Provide an explicitly invoked preparation action composing the existing source-build,
scientific capsule, native-lane and module owners. Preserve target-owned, runner-borrowed
and preparation-only distinctions. Select native-lane package/target/features through its
declared owner, not guessed paths. Reuse immutable captures only when source, compiler,
feature/native profile and necessary input identities remain applicable; each test execution
still owns its original inventory, performer and outcome.

**Mechanisms and sources.** Reuse
[`fixtures.rs`](../../crates/pse-host/src/fixtures.rs),
[`fixtures/execution.rs`](../../crates/pse-host/src/fixtures/execution.rs),
[`runner.rs`](../../crates/pse-host/src/test_workflow/execute/runner.rs) and
[`discovery.rs`](../../crates/pse-host/src/discovery.rs).
Use existing `BuildCapture`/`Executable` custody rather than introducing another artifact
store. Generated passive contracts expose readiness and preparation requirements; they
cannot manufacture a completed build, current fixture authority or test verdict.

**Alternatives and closure.** Keep explicit environment selection as an eligible low-level
route. Avoid automatic build/start during help, inspection or client connection. A new
universal build/fixture scheduler would duplicate existing owners. Demonstrate one exact
selection with several missing inputs, conditional versus exact readiness, explicit preparation,
execution, setup failure, interruption, retained read and exact rerun after source change.
Delete repeated handwritten preparation recipes only where the owner-composed action covers
their full contract. AP-06/AP-07, DP-10/DP-16/DP-18/DP-21 and test-assessment §6/§8 apply.

Coalesce equivalent immutable helper preparation within the invocation. The current preparation
loop groups worker needs by compiler group and can call its correctness/build/capture operation
again for equivalent helper contexts. Reuse one captured executable only when the complete
relevant compiler/native/feature/source context agrees; keep distinct group associations and
original execution evidence. Feature-name equality alone is insufficient, and mutable fixtures
must remain independently owned. This is a local preparation reuse opportunity, not a new
persistent artifact cache.

<a id="I07"></a>
### I07 — Delegate lifecycle mechanics without discarding task results

Preserve the existing engineering server's ordering: native `engine.shutdown` precedes finite
transport drain; inner operation errors and outer task failures remain distinct. Consolidate
repeated shutdown code only where the same ownership and failure contract applies.

Tokio 1.53.3 `JoinSet` retains task results; `abort_all` must be followed by joining.
Tokio-util 0.7.19 `CancellationToken` signals cancellation and `TaskTracker` observes tracked
task destruction. Tracker `close` does not reject new spawns and it does not retain outcomes;
it cannot replace admission closure or result-bearing joins.
Tokio-rayon 2.1.0 submits CPU work eagerly; dropping its observation handle does not stop the
closure. Acquire capacity before submission and retain its lease inside the computation.
Use collect-all settlement for independent teardown; fail-fast joining can erase later
cleanup obligations. Sources:
[JoinSet](https://docs.rs/tokio/1.53.3/tokio/task/struct.JoinSet.html),
[TaskTracker](https://docs.rs/tokio-util/0.7.19/tokio_util/task/struct.TaskTracker.html),
[tokio-rayon](https://docs.rs/tokio-rayon/2.1.0/tokio_rayon/).

Keep separate owners for request, native attempt, session, process, durable effect and reader
protection. A shutdown hierarchy library is conditional on a real hierarchy whose failure
propagation it can express; a new framework is not justified by repeated loops alone.
Check lost waiter, worker panic, simultaneous cleanup failures, late native completion and
admission closing concurrently with submission. Affected code is D/E/J and
[`engineering/rpc.rs`](../../crates/pse-runtime/src/host_operations/engineering/rpc.rs).
This advances AP-01/AP-03/AP-06 and DP-19/DP-20 while preserving PS-11 resource lifetimes.

There is a specific release gap to correct: scientific `IoOwner::drop` ignores its thread
join result, `Remote::drop` logs child-drain failure, and
`ProcessCustody::settle_failure` stringifies cleanup alongside the original cause. Add an
explicit result-bearing finish at the existing placement owner: close admission, settle the
appropriate session/process, reap, join I/O and aggregate original typed failures after every
independent obligation has been attempted. Keep Drop as best-effort containment when a caller
abandons the owner. The explicit success path must establish settlement rather than infer it
from Drop having run. If reap or I/O settlement cannot be established, attempted cleanup
does not authorize admission release. Verify original scientific failure combined with reap failure, I/O panic
and delayed destruction; admission must remain held through the last physical owner.
Sources: [transport](../../crates/pse-runtime/src/scientific_worker/transport.rs),
[proxy](../../crates/pse-runtime/src/scientific_worker/proxy.rs),
[process](../../crates/pse-runtime/src/scientific_worker/process.rs).

## Contract infrastructure and independent consumption

### I04 — Compare typed supplier composition with the actual plugin pipeline

**New evidence.** A04's separate-tree rationale correctly identifies metadata lost through
name/content-only plugin responses. It does not settle the public Rust API alternative.
Installed connectrpc-codegen 0.9.0 `codegen::generate_services` returns
`Vec<buffa_codegen::GeneratedFile>`, retaining package/kind. The maintained validator's
`scan` and `emit` are public but admission remains private in its binary.
Current `xtask::codegen::proto_projection` runs preflight and then the validator plugin:
preflight admission gathers descriptors, plugin admission gathers them again, and the plugin
gathers them again for rendering. This is demonstrable repeated structural preparation, not a measured
generation bottleneck.

**Conditional amendment.** Compare one admitted validator preparation result consumed by
both preflight and emission, and typed in-process Connect output. Reuse Buffa 0.9.2
`generate_with_diagnostics`, compatible `GeneratedFile` outputs and immutable corpus
preparation where it actually fits. Preserve each supplier's valid layout, imports,
`extern_path` mappings and independent target closure. `apply_companions` is optional:
it is not deduplication, package collision validation or proof an orphan output is included.

Compare retaining subprocess isolation with moving preparation into the tooling dependency
closure. Select in-process composition only if it removes complete preparation/stitching
responsibilities without excessive build coupling or weakening generator failure isolation.
Keep a thin plugin entrypoint for external plugin consumers when needed; it must call the
same maintained admission/emission implementation. Do not create a second validator.

**Sources and closure.**
[`proto_projection.rs`](../../xtask/src/codegen/proto_projection.rs),
[validator main](../../vendor/protoc-gen-protovalidate-buffa/src/main.rs),
[validator library](../../vendor/protoc-gen-protovalidate-buffa/src/lib.rs),
[Connect codegen 0.9.0](https://docs.rs/connectrpc-codegen/0.9.0/connectrpc_codegen/codegen/fn.generate_services.html),
[Buffa codegen 0.9.2](https://github.com/anthropics/buffa/blob/v0.9.2/buffa-codegen/src/lib.rs).
Compare independently specified descriptor/option cases, package collisions, imported/WKT
types, owned/view validation, warnings and complete generated consumers. Remove replaced
process/stitching paths only after equivalent generation and admission are established.
AP-02/AP-07 and DP-10/DP-13/DP-14/DP-16 support the investigation, not unconditional adoption.

### I05 — Make a boundary's supported policy discoverable and executable

Extend A/B's existing semantic checker into complete boundary consumers rather than adding
parallel policy tables. Distinguish schema/edition features, wire fidelity, local rule coverage,
semantic interpretation, contextual admission and observation-only construction. Bind each
retained artifact to the originals actually required for that family.

Buffa 0.9.2 `DescriptorPool` and generated vtable reflection support immutable linked worlds
and typed options; generated-to-dynamic conversion encodes/decodes and is not a free field
walker. Use checked dynamic mutation where needed. Prost 0.14.4 `Message::merge` can mutate
before a later error: adopt a fresh successfully decoded value before replacing authoritative
state. PBJSON/ProtoJSON is a declared format, not an exact archive or ordinary Serde substitute.
[Buffa reflection](https://github.com/anthropics/buffa/blob/v0.9.2/buffa-descriptor/src/reflect/dynamic.rs),
[Prost merge](https://docs.rs/prost/0.14.4/src/prost/message.rs.html).

Keep the maintained 0.7.2 validator's bounded CEL admission, exact arithmetic/error distinctions
and explicit invocation. Generated `Validate` and runtime-selected CEL validators serve different
needs. Runtime `MessageDescriptors`, `AdditionalDescriptorSetBytes`, `DisableLazy` and
`NowFn` are conditional mechanisms for a real closed dynamic-schema consumer; `Filter`
and `FailFast` narrow the reported evidence. An unsupported rule must not silently become
unchecked success. [Maintained contract](../../vendor/protoc-gen-protovalidate-buffa/README.md),
[runtime validator options](https://docs.rs/prost-protovalidate/0.6.0/prost_protovalidate/enum.ValidatorOption.html).

Reopen strict JSON only against a released complete mechanism. Buffa-main
`deny_unknown_json_fields` is not established for pinned 0.9.2; unknown-key rejection alone
does not replace duplicate literal/logical aliases, trailing-input checks, WKT/external
policy and pre-allocation admission. Edition compatibility also needs the complete chain:
prost-protovalidate 0.6.0's `normalize_edition_descriptor_set` is an adaptation, not proof
that every compiler/generator/runtime supports every Edition feature.

Delete intermediate trees or repeated checks only for guarantees preserved across the exact
trust transition. Challenge unknown fields/enums, absent/default/null, large exact integers,
malformed tails, changed semantic options with wire-compatible fields, missing historical
policy and observation decoding that attempts to mint permission. This completes and sharpens
November 3 I05/I07/I08/I10; it does not report those obligations as newly discovered defects.
AP-04/AP-05, DP-03/DP-08/DP-15/DP-24 apply.

### I06 — Expose useful selections without duplicating result or storage authority

Complete F04 and C's native-query/projection contracts across producers and independent readers.
Carry selected columns/coordinates, governing attempt, semantic coverage, returned extent and
terminal completeness as distinct intent/facts. A small output can still need global scalar
checks; preserve F04's explicit narrower readable-root coverage and full scientific admission.
Do not label a selected Arrow view a full validated scientific result.

For SurrealDB, source-declared mapping should generate only mechanical conversion and queryable

`SurrealValue::{kind_of,into_value,from_value}` and `Kind` supply structural primitives,

[Native codec](https://docs.rs/crate/surrealdb-types/3.3.4/source/src/proto/mod.rs),
[canonical codec](../../crates/pse-operations/src/canonical_codec.rs),
[protected results](../../crates/pse-operations/src/canonical_results.rs).

A concrete graph inspection consumer can justify reaction/equipment/provenance path queries
with typed roles, direction, multiplicity and occurrence identity. Compare current selected
queries with derived views; related-record and absent-lookup dependencies must be complete.
Live notifications do not provide retained replay continuity. An exporter needing continuity

not a generic outbox adopted merely because it is available. Current server patches and

[Capability/source account](../library_leverage/libraries/proto/briefings/surrealdb-storage-and-projections.md).

For buffers, compare producer selection plus native Arrow with Buffa `OwnedView`,

[Buffa encoding](https://github.com/anthropics/buffa/blob/v0.9.2/buffa/src/encode_sink.rs),

Migrate selected producers, actual CLI/service readers, mapping/history readers and release

### I08 — Turn contract metadata into independent engineering functionality

Deliver a usable descriptor-aware native client for discovery, constraint explanation,
exact-parent edit preview, scientific operation submission and selected protected result
inspection. Reuse `pse-host::schema::payload` and native edit/operation owners. A generic
inspector must show unavailable/unknown interpretation and scope rather than infer scientific
readiness. Descriptor metadata supplies forms and explanations; source owners supply actual
edit, parameter, unit and operation meaning.

ConnectRPC 0.9.1 generated service/client contracts, `Router::try_merge_in_place`,
`Router::methods`, generated `Spec` and route limits can derive actual registration
and avoid another method catalogue. `ConnectError::into_http_response` supplies protocol-aware
early refusal before typed interceptor work. Reassess custom Hyper hosting against released
`Server` only with authenticated UDS peer/image custody, pre-poll admission, native shutdown,
connection settlement and typed multi-error preservation.
[Router](https://docs.rs/connectrpc/0.9.1/connectrpc/router/struct.Router.html),
[server](https://docs.rs/connectrpc/0.9.1/connectrpc/struct.Server.html).

Typed rich details should carry original codes/field paths and explicitly incomplete public
summaries, referencing protected full evidence where available. ConnectRPC
`ErrorDetail::from_message`/`with_detail` serialize details; `with_source` preserves a
local cause separately. For external Tonic clients, `tonic-types::StatusExt::check_error_details`
can preserve malformed-versus-absent extraction. Neither detail encoding nor health/reflection
is scientific outcome authority.
[Connect errors](https://github.com/connectrpc/connect-rust/blob/v0.9.1/src/error.rs),
[Tonic details](https://docs.rs/tonic-types/0.14.6/tonic_types/trait.StatusExt.html).

An optional browser client can use unary submission and server-streamed progress/results;
ordinary browser gRPC-Web support does not imply native bidirectional provider sessions.
Keep this a client projection of the same native operation, with response admission,
descriptor disclosure and reconnection to original identity. Delete client-specific field
walkers and success classifiers when the actual consumers migrate. Check unknown capability,
replaced peer, lost ACK, non-UTF-8 source provenance and partial result followed by error.
This is an AP-03/AP-06 and DP-13/DP-21/DP-24 enhancement within D/F/G's changed footprint.

### I09 — Give three different scientific transfer operations separate contracts

Keep portable reconstructible source/configuration, live admitted-body delegation, and durable
historical replay distinct. E02's current JSON interpretation 3 remains active; a passive
generated Recipe family is not a production protobuf cutover.
Proposed [ADR-0246](../adr/0246-live-admitted-body-delegation.md) is a narrow optimization for
one already-admitted closed private child. It is not a generic portable scientific capability.

For independent receiving or durable reconstruction, retain original scientific context,
implementation eligibility, source/receipt evidence, descriptor interpretation and native
receiving admission. For live delegation, the parent retains admission/evidence through
actual child reap and independent proposal screening; transfer only the validated native
mathematical/execution closure needed by that child. Neither route serializes live closures,
solver workspaces or arbitrary authority.

Compare the existing source reconstruction, generated portable contract, and checked
library-native packing over preparation, transfer, restore, derivative/evaluator preparation,
execution and settlement. The existing E02 supplier investigation is a prerequisite:
non-rational/MPFR values, all consumed settings/guards, numeric symbol relocation and packed
term ordering must survive; equal packed widths do not prove equal symbol order.
Use the active native-solver-libraries research route before a numerical-library/API change.
[E02 exact mechanism and source account](../plans/65e-scientific-portability-and-execution.md).

Do not implement both as competing production paths for the same consumer. Select per operation
and remove the replaced route only after complete reconstruction/custody controls.
New independently compiled provider plugins may justify a versioned C ABI with opaque handles
or a process boundary. Proto metadata does not provide a Rust ABI, callback quiescence or safe
unload. This is a conditional extension decision, not an implicit requirement for E02.
AP-02/AP-07, DP-08/DP-10/DP-15 and PS-07/PS-09 apply.

## Shared inspection and large evidence

<a id="I10"></a>
### I10 — Separate descriptor acquisition from optional whole-corpus presentation

**Observed amplification:** `schema::Bundle::from_bytes` unconditionally invokes `index`,
materializing declarations, comments, relationships and option details for the entire linked
world. `Bundle::options` converts linked options to JSON and parses that JSON again.
Payload-only inspection and exact declaration lookup therefore pay for an unrelated full
presentation population; search collects matches before applying bounded/Exists demand.
[Current owner](../../crates/pse-host/src/schema/mod.rs).

Keep immutable acquired bytes/digest, closure validation and one `Arc<DescriptorPool>`.
Use Buffa 0.9.2 `message_by_name`, `file_containing_symbol` and linked descriptors for
exact requests; render selected details/options on demand. A broad-search consumer may own a
reusable lazy index. Required semantic-option admission remains enforced independently of
whether a declaration is displayed. No second schema graph or process-global mutable pool
is needed. [Pool API/source](https://github.com/anthropics/buffa/blob/v0.9.2/buffa-descriptor/src/pool.rs).

Migrate payload, search and comparison consumers together, removing unconditional presentation
and unneeded JSON intermediates. Exact counts still require exhaustive relevant traversal;
retain deterministic order, cursor identity and complete comparison semantics. Verify a
payload-only command avoids full presentation, an exact declaration retains its output,
and full counts/bounded pages agree with independent cases. Unrenderable optional presentation
must not silently become a semantic-admission bypass. This is an AP-07/DP-10/DP-14 improvement;
no latency or allocation reduction has been measured.

<a id="I11"></a>
### I11 — Bind generated validation to the effective world consistently

**Observed inconsistency:** `Bundle::compiled_world` recognizes only raw bytes equal to
two embedded runtime artifacts. General payload inspection consequently does not select the
compiled decoder/validator for a source-aware artifact differing only in genuine SourceCodeInfo.
The developer reader already implements `same_runtime_world`, removing only source information
before complete descriptor comparison. A03 intentionally publishes both artifact forms.
Sources: [schema owner](../../crates/pse-host/src/schema/mod.rs),
[payload](../../crates/pse-host/src/schema/payload.rs),
[developer reader](../../crates/pse-host/src/developer_evidence.rs).

Share that narrow existing relation through the appropriate host contract-world owner.
Return a typed matched-world identity selecting its generated dispatch, rather than an
undifferentiated Boolean. Preserve each original artifact's checksum and provenance.
Recognition grants neither semantic compatibility nor native scientific/performer authority.

Do not normalize imports, file ordering, validation options, effective features, unknown fields
or arbitrary wire-compatible schemas merely to obtain a match. Keeping byte-only recognition is
a credible simpler alternative, but would deliberately limit the usefulness of source-aware
delivery and retain inconsistent readers. Prefer the existing strictly scoped effective-world
relation, with the dispatch population integrated into A04's consumer composition.

Compare the same independently malformed payload under published runtime and source-aware
worlds: generated validation meaning should agree while artifact digests differ. Alter an
option, feature, import or unknown field and require refusal of compiled binding. Remove the
duplicated byte-only recognition only when these controls and developer observation boundaries
remain intact. This corrects remaining A03/G02 integration under AP-02, DP-01/DP-15/DP-24.

<a id="I12"></a>
### I12 — Keep large original artifacts outside a single message-sized evidence allocation

**Observed opportunity:** `developer_evidence::export` acquires original dependency/report
bytes, converts them into generated messages and encodes another complete vector.
The current portable input/output profile is 64 MiB; `CapturedFile::original` embeds raw artifacts.
That bound refuses honestly. It need not become a general limit on valid large compiler,
JUnit or scientific test evidence. [Export](../../crates/pse-host/src/developer_evidence.rs),
[evidence owner](../../crates/pse-host/src/test_workflow/execute/evidence.rs).

Retain one original commitment interpretation: ordinal, role, extent, digest and declared
omissions. Offer a bounded manifest with incremental protected file/chunk acquisition for a
real large-report consumer. For offline export, stage required members and publish the final
manifest only when its required closure is complete. The compact message remains a suitable
physical profile for small inputs; both profiles must use the same native commitments and
adoption, not independent verdicts or separately editable manifests.

Use the existing invocation publisher's staging/sync/no-replace semantics. Tokio-util
`ReaderStream`/`StreamReader`, bounded channel reservations and Bytes 1.12.1
`Bytes::from_owner` can supply transfer mechanics. They do not establish original custody,
safe path selection, coherent bundle publication or reader protection.
[I/O adapters](https://docs.rs/tokio-util/0.7.19/tokio_util/io/index.html),
[publisher](../../crates/pse-host/src/invocation.rs).

J04 must first identify the actual pin/acquisition/release owner; current invocation publication
does not itself provide a general prune/dispose contract. Preserve omitted-at-capture evidence
even if a file appears later. Challenge a complete bundle exceeding 64 MiB, slow/escaped reader,
cancellation, corrupt/missing later member, destination collision, failed final publication
and retirement with an active reader. Avoid arbitrary extraction paths from messages.
Remove repeated whole-population copies only for the consumers fully migrated.
This is an AP-07/DP-19/DP-21 enhancement within J/F/G, not a second evidence store.

## Integration, broader opportunities and decisions

### Coherent delivery order

1. Correct I02's native lifecycle hazard and establish I03's original preparation contract.
   These make current scientific and developer verification more reliable; neither requires
   broad codegen completion.
2. Specify I01 through concrete extension consumers, and I05's exact boundary/interpretation
   obligations. Those meanings determine generated records, not the reverse.
3. Settle I04 with a bounded whole-generation comparison and integrate I10/I11's shared
   inspection corrections, while completing I06/I08/I12 through
   one scientific and one developer producer-to-independent-reader operation.
4. Select I09's transfer route per actual consumer. Extend populations and delete replacements
   as each complete route qualifies; H reconciles assembled coverage at the end.

This is prerequisite order, not a ranking that makes developer preparation more scientifically
important than model adequacy. I01 is the highest leverage target-design amendment; I02 is the
most immediate correctness concern. I04/I09 are conditional simplifications whose costs can
justify retaining current routes. Scheduling remains a subsequent plan-revision decision.

Preserve November 3 I01–I11 at their existing owners. This review sharpens their implementation
and adds concrete new issues/functional routes; it does not reset them to unresolved or completed.
In particular, I01 extends the older scientific-operation/product/dependency group; I03
extends developer lifecycle obligations; I04 reopens a specific A04 premise; I05 preserves
the strict-JSON release gate; I06/I08 complete selected readers rather than introducing another
result store or workflow engine.

Broader deployment is eligible on value, not required for uniformity.

### Rule and migration implications

Changes to scientific product/operation/kernel contracts, construction authority, metadata,
hashing, crate placement or durable interpretations require the existing applicable decision
routes before implementation. ADR-0241–0246 retain their actual statuses. No accepted ADR,
architecture section, plan package or scope disposition is amended by this review.

Design-phase disposable state can be rebuilt deliberately. Protected/user-retained artifacts
need their original supported interpretation, explicit migration or explicit incompatibility.
Compatibility readers exist for that promise, not to retain a replaced production path.
No proposal weakens physical constraints, numerical tolerances, source fidelity, the zero
failure baseline or the independence of scientific expectations.
