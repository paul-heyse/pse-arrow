# Integrated simulation target and library leverage

## Combined judgment

**Proposed:** replace the science-versus-Proto organization of the remaining undertaking
with one scientific capability architecture and outcome-oriented delivery. Preserve authored
physical meaning, library-owned mathematics, native execution custody and original evidence.
Use generated contracts, runtime libraries and analytical engines to make those operations
usable and extensible, rather than making completion of a universal metadata catalogue the
first milestone. Reconsider implemented mechanisms when a complete alternative is better.

The most consequential improvement is stronger composition across the existing owners:
scientific definition → selected physical case and analysis purpose → demanded preparation
and numerical products → execution and independent assessment → protected products → useful
independent reading. Development workflows need the corresponding explicit selection,
prerequisite, performing operation, original evidence and rerun lifecycle. These flows share
mechanics where their contracts coincide; their scientific, operational and test verdicts
remain different meanings.

This is a substantial amendment to delivery organization and several interface boundaries,
not a proposal to replace every working mechanism. The pure contract foundation, explicit
physical semantics, original-coordinate checks and protected readers are strengths. A new
universal simulator IR, workflow engine, metadata registry, permission service or changefeed
is not justified merely by combining the plans. Useful library capabilities and future
scientific extensions remain eligible without an existing caller; their integration needs
a credible operation and a reasoned account of the complete resulting system.

## Recommended amendments

<a id="I01"></a>
### I01 — Make the scientific operation the composition boundary

**Alignment improvement; accepted remaining scope.**
[`DeclaredExecution`](../../crates/pse-runtime/src/workflow/modeling/declared.rs),
[`PreparedOperation`](../../crates/pse-runtime/src/workflow/operation_preparation.rs) and
[`Completion`](../../crates/pse-runtime/src/workflow/completion.rs) already separate useful
meanings. Yet the current roadmap scatters their extension across science, service, worker,
artifact, result and tooling packages. Completing one packet does not deliver an operation.

Select an operation by its physical definition/context, case, analysis purpose, actual input
roles, requested products/evidence and method constraints. Its native preparation resolves
the supported realization; generated records describe the request or observed result in the
appropriate direction. Keep physical models reusable across analyses while statistical,
optimization and control policies remain at their existing operation owners. Use small
family-specific compositions, not one universal operation enum with every optional field.

The alternative of leaving every adapter to reconstruct these relationships repeats meaning;
making Proto the executable scientific authority cannot discharge formulation or assessment.
An immutable admitted operation and a separately editable request/observation are preferable
where public mutable views currently make original versus effective input ambiguous. Preserve
legitimate late bindings and recheck only their affected premises. Delete duplicate mode and
client permission interpretation when producers and actual selectors move.

Challenge with one source definition used for steady simulation, design and transient fitting;
change a first-interval fit target to a later interval and require explicit semantics rather
than numeric-path substitution. Existing `IntegratedExperiment::Binding` intentionally binds
the initial interval. Preserve `DeclaredExecution::prepared_model`'s current admitted
numeric-value overlays and structural/source/context checks: making admission immutable
must not turn every compatible value change into respecialization. Structural Analyze may
publish subject/operation evidence with zero numerical members; physical-state permission
is inapplicable there, while point-derived products retain their exact permission.
AP-01–AP-06, DP-01/DP-09, PS-01/PS-11/PS-12 govern this amendment.

<a id="I02"></a>
### I02 — Grow physical capability through explicit conservation and coordinates

**Missing accepted obligations plus extension-locality improvement.** The authored
[`ControlVolume1D`](../../packages/reference/process/models/control-volume-1d.pse) explicitly
refuses dynamics because spatial flux balance is not temporal inventory. Preserve that
scientific distinction. Add required stock/allocation, temporal accumulation and original
boundary-flux assessment at their physical owners before extending supported routes.

Separate material/phase/site/time/experiment coordinate identity from numerical storage
position. Distinguish initial physical conditions, numerical starts, prescribed schedules,
physical operating transitions and independent Study occurrences. Use existing authored
contributions and temporal/spatial specialization, extending a generic semantic contract only
when a required family exposes the gap. Shared projection infrastructure must not force all
scalar providers to acquire chemistry metadata.

This supports the remaining steam/power, heterogeneous beds, MEA/membranes, TSA/SOC and network
populations with their actual constitutive/data dependencies. A constant-density aqueous
finite-volume case can challenge shared accumulation; it cannot qualify compressible linepack,
reacting multiphase beds or a continuum approximation. Require separate energy/charge/momentum
closure where modeled, route-specific derivative behavior, conservation and refinement evidence.
No new universal physical ontology or equipment-name dispatch table follows. AP-04, DP-02/DP-07,
PS-01–PS-08/PS-13; detailed alternatives are in the scientific analysis.

<a id="I03"></a>
### I03 — Let demanded mathematics select complete library operations

**Library leverage and general enhancement.** Do not extend the successful exchanger accuracy
repair into a universal arithmetic mechanism. Choose symbolic, coefficient, factorable,
sparse numerical or action-based representation from the required block and product. Preserve
authored guards, exact constants, physical acceptance and source correspondence through every
transformation. Point estimates, certified enclosures, local regularity and independent
reference agreement remain different evidence.

Use the scientific analysis's exact Symbolica/Numerica, faer and native-solver contracts to
evaluate stronger algebraic preparation, shared multi-output programs, derivative actions,
factor reuse and precision-aware diagnostics. A library routine that supplies a whole solve
or factor operation should own its iteration and workspace where qualified; application code
supplies only the remaining scientific contract. Do not substitute a plain Newton method for
a bounded, constrained or certified operation merely because it is readily callable.

Prioritize actual representation/conversion removal and derivative-demand propagation before
more pools or caches. Preserve genuinely necessary native original-coordinate assessment.
Precision/tolerance changes are eligible with dimensional rationale and countercontrols;
loosening an allowance until a case passes is not scientific justification. Close through a
changed-coefficient case, ill-conditioned/rank-deficient case, rejected domain and an original
independent physical comparison. AP-07, DP-10/DP-13/DP-15, PS-06–PS-10.

<a id="I04"></a>
### I04 — Generate at useful boundary scope with a reproducible bootstrap

**Observed integration friction and structural improvement.** The paused result adapter
needs ordinary default-feature access while broad engineering adapters are service-gated.
The inspection source changes also need source-to-generation bootstrap without compiling
stale generated mappings. This is evidence against equating one descriptor world with one
mandatory compilation, service or presentation unit.

Keep one authoritative declaration per meaning and one effective semantic interpretation,
but allow purpose-selected adapter roots, disclosed descriptor sets and validator preparation.
Compare the candidate same-world root-scoped emission with complete typed in-process compiler
and companion composition. Preserve source positions, imports/options, edition/features,
diagnostics, original unknowns and deterministic generated provenance. A split earns its place
when it removes dependency cycles or unrelated product preparation, not simply by adding files.

The contract analysis characterizes Buffa, maintained validation, ConnectRPC, Prost/Tonic,
protox/Buf and Rust-first alternatives. Their selected functionality must remain explicit in
future plan bodies. Use generated local rules for their actual shape guarantees; native
contextual admission retains scientific membership, authority and effects. Remove replaced
hand maps, subprocess/preflight duplication and default-to-compatible interpretations only
with all selected consumers. Verify a changed original field through generation and default,
service and independent-client consumers, including a stripped annotation and invalid input.
AP-01/AP-02/AP-05, DP-01/DP-03/DP-15/DP-24.

<a id="I05"></a>
### I05 — Share lifecycle mechanics at their actual ownership boundaries

**Alignment improvement; remaining operational delivery.** Current generic supervision and
unique surrogate retirement are useful concrete corrections, not a mandate to wrap every
operation in another actor. Compose library cancellation/tracking/streams and native performing
owners according to the work's actual lifetime. An observer, reply channel, async task,
CPU closure, native session and OS process can settle at different times.

Prefer finite completion-driven work and library batching where they discharge the operation;
retain joined/thread-affine ownership where foreign callbacks or TLS require it. Carry all
independent operation and retirement failures. Once closed, admission and cleanup need one
authoritative owner; task-tracker closure alone is not submission exclusion. Reuse tested
mechanics without inventing a universal runtime state machine or generic durable scheduler.

Keep live admitted-body delegation, current receiving reconstruction and historical evidence
distinct as 65k already requires. Validate any simpler placement against actual preparation,
transfer, native demand, original verification and physical drain. Additional transport codecs,
deployment profiles or ABI plugins need their own complete useful operation; broad capability
coverage is not a reason to add all of them. Preserve configured host width, incremental builds
and caching. AP-02/AP-05/AP-07, DP-19/DP-20, PS-09/PS-10.

<a id="I06"></a>
### I06 — Make preparation validity local and environment selection compositional

**Observed execution friction and reuse improvement.** The completed bridge repair removes a
preparing-worktree file dependency, but a later worktree generation still refused compiler
bridge context. The review has not established that cause. Do not weaken identity checks or
call this solved from the earlier component pass.

Treat compiler/native provider selection as a composed immutable preparation with explicit
construction inputs, installed selections and dependent artifacts. Reentry should validate
the same meaning without reinterpreting installed values as fresh caller overrides. Separate
checkout build targets from shared supplier artifacts. Challenge independent worktrees,
selected capability subsets, cache-mode changes and removal of the preparing checkout.

Apply the same consumed-dependency discipline to Salsa-derived science, descriptor worlds,
symbolic programs, numerical factors and result interpretation. Use existing query backdating
and value/structure separation where appropriate; remove overlapping cache keys and repeated
verification only when equivalent premises really survive. Never retain mutable native work
inside a tracked semantic query. Complete-path benefit remains unmeasured. AP-01/AP-07,
DP-04/DP-09/DP-10, PS-11; source: native preparation and compiler owners plus supporting analyses.

<a id="I07"></a>
### I07 — Make product queries and publication one coherent data path

**Library leverage and remaining migration.** Exact selected-result context and duplicate-key
preflight are useful foundations. ADR-0250's candidate metadata encoding is one export
interpretation, not a new scientific authority or a requirement that all analytical products
be serialized as Proto. Preserve Arrow/native bulk forms where they serve the operation.

Let selection, field demand, aggregation and required evidence reach DataFusion/Arrow and
the canonical query producer. Keep original global keys, predicates and scientific-use inputs
when narrowing hydration. Use database-native complete operations for transactional effects
and original protection, not per-row client orchestration. Retain the existing witness capture
unless an exact patched-engine alternative covers absent/future membership and every writer.

Separate small typed metadata, bulk numerical arrays and opaque retained evidence by access
and lifecycle needs. Compare IPC random access, sequential exchange, analytical files and
object-backed artifacts over complete publication/read/release obligations. Do not infer local
inode custody from an ETag or durable authority from a successful upload. Remove old writers
as each consumer population migrates; historical readers survive only their actual promise.
Challenge empty/partial selections, nonselected attempts, uncertain ACK, changed mapping and
failed release. AP-04/AP-07, DP-08/DP-15/DP-21, PS-12; exact APIs in the data analysis.

<a id="I08"></a>
### I08 — Give developer and production evidence complete native lifecycles

**Functional improvement and streamlining.** The new aggregate fixture-prerequisite account
correctly moves beyond first-refusal reporting. Extend its real workflow: exact selected
population → independent missing/refused inputs → explicit preparation → performing execution
→ original full evidence → bounded independent reading → valid rerun or incompatibility.

Use the existing report, inventory, original-file directory delivery and performing terminal
owners. Historical success grants no present fixture readiness; source/build compatibility
does not grant scientific permission. Scientific failures, missing prerequisites, incomplete
capture, native cleanup and unattempted work remain distinguishable without making clients
reconstruct the verdict from strings or combine many loosely related status flags.

Share bounded acquisition, selected inspection and error composition where they mean the same
thing. Keep scientific assessment and test outcome native and domain-specific. Prefer a small
local semantic test for a local policy, reserving full solver/database setup for actual
integration uncertainty. A real failed original report/export/read/rerun and one interrupted
cleanup journey are stronger than more paired round trips. AP-02/AP-06/AP-07, DP-21/DP-23.

<a id="I09"></a>
### I09 — Extend analyses as typed scientific campaigns, not copied workflows

**Functional enhancement within the remaining64g footprint.** Joint inference, uncertainty,
adaptive experimentation, surrogate training, discrete design, control and assessment need
distinct scientific products but can compose the same model/evaluation operations.

Use the scientific analysis's faer, ndarray, EGObox and random-stream mechanisms for explicit
data/coordinate layouts, observation noise/dependence, adaptive sampling and demand-shaped
linear algebra. Distinguish GP predictive variance from physical uncertainty, pending points
from observations, failure imputation from measured response, and a seed from a complete
replay contract. Retain realized inputs and failed members when required for interpretation.

For fitting, explicitly select shared versus experiment-local parameters, initial states and
schedule targets; add a new target only with native admission and derivative/initialization
support. For control/cycles, carry actual physical state, not Study-node succession. Separate
surrogate evaluation, optimization embedding and trainer reproduction. New modules are useful
only for genuine policy variation, not each library's nouns. Test two transient experiments
with different schedules plus a steady experiment, confounding, failed samples and a deliberately
wrong member association. AP-03/AP-04, DP-02/DP-06, PS-07/PS-10–PS-13.

<a id="I09-G06"></a>
#### Engineering assessment and useful native views

G06/G07 require a distinct complete delivery alongside inference/control. Existing
[authored costing](../../packages/reference/process/models/costing.pse) and the
[costed exchanger](../../packages/reference/campaign/models/exchanger-costing.pse)
already compose solved physical quantities with empirical correlations, currency/base
period, applicability permissions and accounting contributions. The exchanger control
explicitly checks arithmetic at extrapolated/unknown support; it does not establish a
qualified purchase price. Preserve this useful separation rather than treating a generic
successful solve or report value as an admitted economic conclusion.

Extend that composition for the selected environmental and utility consumers: declare
physical contributors, system boundary, utilization/time basis, allocation, factor source
and version, and the demanded assessment product. Missing required factors remain unavailable.
Inventory/conservation, cost allocation, environmental characterization and utility targeting
have different rules even when all use sums. A pinch/utility target additionally requires
its chosen temperature approach, shifted-temperature conventions and feasible heat levels;
a duty total alone is not that target. The exact required method and reference population
remain a G06 investigation, not a new implementation claim.

Compare authored assessment contributions over existing result/quantity owners with a new
general assessment engine or an external postprocessing database. Prefer extending the
authored composition and native product/admission owner for current consumers; a genuinely
different assessment method can supply a complete bounded operation. Reuse research/publication
for factors and original provenance, and DataFusion's selected factual joins/aggregation for
reporting. Keep scientific interpretation at the native owner; SQL aggregation cannot infer
allocation or qualification. No new library is required merely to produce these products.

Native stream tables, trajectory/uncertainty views and flowsheet/result visualization should
consume the existing [protected result inspection](../../crates/pse-runtime/src/workflow/result_inspection.rs),
source identities and typed edit/projection contracts. Local sensitivity/uncertainty views
also retain the scoped availability in
[`local_analysis`](../../crates/pse-runtime/src/workflow/local_analysis.rs).
Rendering is observational; it must not rerun science, issue permission, silently interpolate
unsupported regions or route edits around admission. Select a rendering library only after
the actual table/plot/graph interaction and output requirements are clear. This review does
not source-qualify a graphical stack or claim that dedicated environmental/utility workflows
already exist.

Deliver a costed physical case and a contrasting environmental or utility case through
assessment, exact protected reading, native view and independent export. Change only currency
period, utilization, factor provenance or allocation and require the appropriate derived
product invalidation without an unrelated physical rerun. Challenge double-counted contributors,
unknown empirical support, missing factors, unavailable siblings, wrong units/time basis and
reordered stream/species identities. G07 retains joined consumer migration and enduring
publication, including native visualization/reporting. Delete replaced ad-hoc aggregation and
presentation interpretation after their callers move; do not add another life-cycle database.

actual qualified output. Unavailable required data remains a blocker, not an excluded row.

Carry the existing population into the new plans with explicit source→configuration→physical

Keep local adequacy tests, projection fidelity, protocol interoperability, physical conservation,
reference comparison, failure settlement and measured benefit as separate assurance roles.
Challenge comparators with wrong output, missing required output, reordered species and a
different datum. Review isolation and oracle independence without rebuilding a universal test
framework. Required broad qualification remains at functional scope end; this review runs none.
AP-04/AP-06/AP-07, DP-22/DP-23, PS-13.
