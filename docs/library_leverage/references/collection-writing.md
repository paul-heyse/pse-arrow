# Writing the living collections

Use coherent explanations, representative evidence and stable references. The following content
leads make accounts useful to their consumers; they are not mandatory headings, tables or fields.
Depth follows the decision. All examples below are hypothetical and establish no current finding
or actual library capability.

## Collection entry pages

Each collection's README holds its purpose, current synthesis, inspected baseline and coverage
limits, routes into substantive topic pages and the next consequential research questions. A
focused refresh states what it reconsidered without resetting the dates of inherited material.
Keep one current account, avoiding a new folder per pass and repeated findings in the index.

The codebase entry page explains relevant responsibilities, intended direction, constraints and
where opportunities cluster. The library entry page routes to researched libraries and focused
types with their version coverage; it should make retained types without current matches easy to
find. The review entry page explains recommendations, their interactions and material unresolved
choices, and links to the downstream decision or execution owner.

All three entry pages route to the shared additional-pattern output when it exists. Read that
account alongside the base framework; its relevant patterns have the same role in identification,
applicability and comparative reasoning. Keep definitions in that one output rather than copying
them into each collection or a skill document.

Topic segmentation follows coherent responsibilities or questions. Repeatedly reconstructing one
argument across many short files is a reason to combine them. A substantial explanation with its
own readers can become a supporting document. Do not create empty category scaffolds.

## Additional patterns discovered during assessment

The initial codebase inquiry assesses whether the working patterns adequately frame what it
finds. Library applicability work reassesses that sufficiency through the relationship between
the identified opportunity and the library's actual functionality. Comparative review can expose
another missing framing. Consider whether an apparent difficulty is a local fact to record, an
existing pattern needing clarification or a useful additional pattern; no novelty quota applies.

Record useful additions or refinements in the shared output at the framework-bound location.
Create the account with its first real pattern. Use a stable ID such as `LOP-X001` and a meaningful
title. Explain its articulation, recognition shapes, useful investigation questions, counterconditions
and scope. Include what existing patterns missed or obscured and the source observations that
prompted it, with their dates and evidence limits. A single revealing case can support a reusable
framing without proving universal applicability.

Keep the reusable definition understandable apart from its originating opportunity. Supporting
occurrences can link to that definition and explain its actual application. Use the new framing
to reconsider affected opportunity descriptions, research questions or candidates within the pass;
broaden investigation where the insight warrants it. Do not imply retrospective coverage of work
that was not reconsidered.

All skills use relevant output patterns immediately, within their stated scope. Retain a useful
pattern after its initial opportunity or current matches retire. Refine or merge overlapping
framings and preserve ID lineage. The operator can later select helpful patterns for manual
inclusion in the base framework and skill guidance; record the mapping then. Discovering a pattern
does not authorize changing the base set, and promotion is not required for its current use.

## A codebase opportunity

Explain the stable ID and title; the intended operation and consumer; observed mechanism or
missing support; why improvement matters; relevant patterns; representative evidence and known
footprint; preservation constraints; and material contrary evidence or uncertainty. Distinguish
observed current behavior, promised behavior, accepted planned use and a proposed functional gain.
Candidate mechanisms can be research leads without deciding the remedy.

For example, `CBO-001 — Selection reaches the reader after full hydration` might explain that a
consumer asks for a narrow subset but the current reader constructs every record before applying
selection. It would cite the inspected call path, state whether filtering can alter errors or
effects, and distinguish the established mechanism from an unmeasured expected benefit. LOP-05
and LOP-06 could guide the investigation. Another consumer needing all records is important
context, rather than a reason to assume either universal narrowing or universal eager loading.

An opportunity is ready for applicability research when its need, relevant footprint and decisive
constraints are understandable. It need not contain an exhaustive affected-file list, a proven
defect, a selected library or an accepted new product requirement.

## A reusable library opportunity type

Explain a namespaced handle and focused need; recognition shapes; concrete APIs, configuration
or extension mechanisms; how the pieces compose; obligations discharged and those left to callers;
important semantic differences; availability/version/build conditions; limits and failure behavior;
and precise research sources with dates and evidence strength. Preserve distinctions that could
change applicability instead of accumulating generic benefit claims.

For example, `example-library/selective-access` could describe a researched family that expresses
selection and projection before execution. Its account would distinguish source pruning from
post-read filtering, specify the conditions for each and state whether incremental consumption
is available. Those distinctions remain useful after the original caller changes. They cannot
be inferred solely from a feature being called "filter" or "streaming."

Keep the reusable explanation independent of repository paths, pins, local recommendations and
current opportunity IDs. Contextual applicability belongs in a separate portion of the library
account. A type can reference precise primary sources without copying a general manual. Retain
prior version coverage only where it remains useful, with explicit limits and original dates.

## A contextual applicability assessment

Connect a codebase opportunity at an identified revision to the relevant type handles and the
actual library realization considered. Explain the fit, semantic mismatches, configuration,
extension/adaptation, remaining work and any premise still unsettled. Reference the authoritative
opportunity and reusable types, while summarizing enough to understand the connection.

For the hypothetical `CBO-001`, an assessment might find that selection can reach an existing
reader, but some predicates require a callback that the reader cannot execute. Explain which
part can be delegated and what remains. Preserve the distinction between documented support and
an application not yet exercised. "Fits with a residual predicate" is more useful than a generic
confidence score. A missing answer about callback semantics stays unsettled.

For a rejected route, state its actual incompatibility and inspected search scope. Generalize a
limitation into the reusable type only when its evidence supports that generalization. One local
configuration or failed probe does not establish a universal limitation.

## A comparative review

Keep the source opportunity IDs. Explain the current responsibility and target intent, credible
complete options, the comparative argument, recommended bounded target and material uncertainty.
Preserve concrete enabling mechanisms and why they matter, including composed library features
that subsequent design or planning should not have to rediscover.

Show what the codebase retains authority over, what each mechanism owns, what existing machinery
can disappear and what new integration or lifecycle is required. Consider consumer impact,
error/effect semantics, execution fit, ordinary extension, transition feasibility and operation
where they affect the choice. Use relevant governing principles and a revealing legitimate case;
no exhaustive compliance matrix or numerical score is implied.

For `CBO-001`, options could include using a native reader's selection, moving the operation into
an existing engine or retaining the current reader for a justified contract. A useful judgment
would explain why one option best fits the real operation, how residual predicates are handled,
which guarantees survive and what could change the choice. It can remain Proposed even when the
enabling interfaces are Interface-checked.

Recommendations can group opportunities sharing a coherent solution. Distinguish reinforcing
choices, alternatives, prerequisites and conflicts. Keep consequence-based priority separate
from implementation order. Identify rule impacts through existing decision routes; a proposal
does not silently change those rules. Detailed execution packages belong to later planning.

## Refreshes, correction and retirement

Use changes in contracts, consumers, intended work, library versions and environment to direct
attention. A diff is a lead rather than the complete scope; unchanged code can acquire different
significance. Challenge prior conclusions and inspect decisive contrary evidence. Evidence from
an old context remains attributed to that context.

When a codebase opportunity retires, record why and on what evidence in the removal commit.
Reconcile current applicability and review links; carry reusable lessons into the library type.
Preserve an earlier published reference for a still-current downstream consumer, or carry its
surviving premises forward before deleting its live home. Never reuse the opportunity ID.

Keep useful library types even when unmatched. Remove obsolete current matches while retaining
the general recognition and semantic analysis. Correct, merge or retire a type for substantive
reasons such as a false claim, duplicate meaning or removed capability with no remaining reader.
Bias toward accumulating useful knowledge rather than accumulating historical status.

Probes and raw artifacts use the host project's evidence convention. A retained evidence README
explains its question, source/library realization, commands, outcomes and limits. Link to the
original immutable outputs and report failed or incomplete work honestly. A probe is useful when
it settles an important premise, not merely because an assessment deserves an evidence artifact.
