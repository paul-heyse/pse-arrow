## Cross-cutting ownership and open evidence

Scientific meaning and derivative/precision duties belong in the science briefing; schema,
wire, mapping and semantic identity distinctions in generation and storage; local trust and
endpoint custody in runtime; source acquisition/offline delivery/fixtures in generation and
clients; diagnostic truncation and honest coverage in diagnostics. The comparative review
reconciles their interactions rather than assigning each concern a new mechanism.

Material uncertainties include external generator/backend fidelity, complete unsupported-rule
coverage, demand-shaped copy elimination, historical projection recovery and measured worker
overhead. The target supplies explicit conditions and discriminating controls. No universal
compatibility, zero-copy, exactly-once, scientific-equivalence or performance claim follows.
