[Plan 37a](../37a-scientific-artifacts-history-and-policy.md) owns the dependent scientific design; the coordinator owns package status, decisions and finding dispositions. Other workers may change their assigned scopes after this inspection. OP01 selects a fresh target store and regenerated evidence: no legacy record conversion, historical reader adapter, old rollout import or preservation snapshot is a prerequisite here.

## Selected target

Use a small owned content capsule for seeds and artifact-owned bounded IPC blocks for selected scientific rows. Preserve one artifact/reference vocabulary, with kind-specific closure decided by the actual scientific producer. Keep exact selected source revisions under artifact-owned roots independently of producer-run roots. Existing persisted graph analysis remains a graph-owned product and acquires narrow artifact inputs rather than retaining a whole producer attempt.

The initial target copies selected immutable content into its artifact owner; it does not share producer-owned block lifetimes. Whole-block copies are valid when their granularity is explicit. A scientifically admitted selected-row copy may reduce mixed blocks, retaining every selected original column and its exact value bits. Its new encoding digest and original block/row provenance are different facts. Do not claim byte-identical IPC encoding after re-encoding or row-level physical recovery from a whole-block copy.

This choice avoids cross-artifact chunk ownership and prevents producer-keyed collection from deleting promoted content. It permits seed/final-state/analysis preservation while unrelated producer trajectory content drains. Revisit shared immutable blocks only when repeated promotion of materially overlapping large content makes duplication a demonstrated concern; the replacement must then supply shared ownership, bounded reachability and restartable collection. Files, deduplication, generic chunk GC and a new service are not prerequisites.

A common artifact admission carries: issuing store/authority and interpretation; artifact kind and closure interpretation; original run/attempt/generation and scientific occurrence coordinates; original manifest/digests and exact selected membership; independently retained source revisions; the selected operation/case/provider/numerical context needed to interpret that kind; original assessment and permitted use; requested versus observed scope; and bounded content membership/digests. These are conceptual fields for the registry owner to declare once, not a prescribed table per field. Attribution to an unavailable producer is valid; a provenance identifier is not a live read permission or a promise that the whole producer history remains reconstructible.

Retained exact inputs establish downstream input reproducibility. They do not promise reconstruction of every producer operation or bit-identical numerical output across a changed execution environment. Preserve transitive input values only when a selected scientific use or explicit audit obligation needs them. Never infer scientific permission from the presence of bytes, a terminal class or a reference count.

## Concrete sufficient and insufficient selections

### Seed and optional prediction

**Sufficient case:** an original qualified KINSOL root start from `workflow/durable_tests.rs::LINEAR`, with the exact original solution ID, producer run/attempt/generation/step, seed inventory membership, typed `SeedPayload::Root` primal bits, layout/preparation/profile/data/backend, original candidate assessment permitting seed reuse, and the necessary rooted scientific interpretation. For NLP/HiGHS use the actual corresponding payload: optional bound/row multipliers/barrier or primal/dual/basis are preserved as present or absent, never reconstructed from a successful status. Include `SeedDocument.prediction` only with its original semantic product key, coordinate/primal bits and permission; preserve its distinction from an ordinary warm start.

**Insufficient case:** a `SolutionId` plus a copied primal vector with no original reuse assessment/inventory witness, or a prediction copied from a point whose assessment does not permit result use. It cannot be promoted as a qualified seed/prediction even when its dimensions fit. A seed-only assessment remains seed-only; promotion cannot grant result permission.

Decisive source: `pse-runtime/src/workflow/durable.rs::{SeedPayload,SeedDocument,Operations::admit_seed,AdmittedSeed}`, `pse-operations/src/canonical_execution.rs::{result_seed,result_seed_candidates}`, and `pse-codegen/src/codegen/surreal_execution.rs::{seed_eligible,seed,seal}`. Current acquisition verifies original manifest membership, step assessment, chunk coverage/digest and compatibility before returning owned immutable values. The new admission witness must preserve those verified original premises without requiring future reads of the retired producer.

**Producer work scheduled with A1/A2:** construct the bounded selected assessment/inventory witness while the original protection is live; persist the owned seed payload and source roots; route pending/retry acquisition to the artifact authority; keep per-occurrence provenance separate from deduplicated study input obligations. Existing `AdmittedSeed` alone is an in-process owner, not that durable witness. No new numerical metadata or solver algorithm is required for the existing supported seed kinds.

### Final physical state

**Sufficient case:** the actual terminal `trajectory_endpoints` row from a simulation, including physical time, stable state/input/output IDs and values, live input segment columns, outputs/integrals, endpoint requirement/event, endpoint satisfaction, prefix completeness and missing observations. Preserve the original completion/assessment, the selected model/case and quantity/unit/basis/reference interpretation, and a manifest mapping endpoint mode and integral/input coordinates to their original authored/prepared meanings. This is an independently useful observed final state, including a truthfully partial endpoint when that is what occurred.

**Insufficient case:** the last requested `simulation_samples` row presented as the final state, or an endpoint vector without coordinate/unit/mode/integral interpretation. Requested samples need not include the actual termination time; a partial prefix cannot become a completed requested endpoint. A final state is not a durable integrator checkpoint.

Decisive source: `pse-schema/src/catalog/native_math.rs::{simulation_samples,trajectory_endpoints,simulation_events}` declarations, `workflow/modeling/dynamics.rs::ModelingTrajectory`, and `workflow/modeling/trajectory.rs` endpoint export. The exporter converts state and inputs through original coordinate scale/offset and retains actual endpoint coverage. Endpoint fields include a native mode index and integral/input arrays; the row alone is not their complete standalone interpretation.

**Producer work scheduled with A2 before final-state promotion:** materialize the endpoint mode name/identity mapping, state/input/output/integral coordinate ordering and units, and selected operation/binding/profile context from `ModelingSimulation`/the original source selection while preparation exists. Preserve a no-endpoint outcome as such; do not synthesize one. This is interpretation metadata, not numerical restart state. Rooted source revisions can retain the definitions, but the artifact must identify the exact mapping rather than ask a future reader to guess it from array position.

### Selected trajectory

**Sufficient case:** one declared symbol's original completed sample rows over an explicitly named original sample selection, with exact times, sample IDs, quantity/unit IDs, selected source/model/case context, relevant event transitions and authored mode mapping, original endpoint/coverage and candidate assessment. The manifest states both the requested trajectory domain and the selected observed subset, including missing requested tail/observations. If response sensitivities are selected, include their own parameter/output/time coordinate and permission context. A projection need not retain unrelated symbols' numerical payloads.

**Insufficient case:** a selected prefix labelled the complete requested trajectory; selected samples stripped of an event/mode transition affecting their interpretation; or a selector that chooses a symbol while omitting the original assessment/coverage. An empty selected result, an unrequested relation and a missing demanded relation remain different outcomes.

Decisive source: `workflow/modeling/trajectory.rs::relation_ids` and export paths; `workflow/study_demand.rs::{StudyOutputDemand,StudyDetailDemand,StudyMemberDemand}`; native `simulation_samples`, `simulation_events`, `trajectory_endpoints`, `response_sensitivities`; `pse-schema/src/catalog/modeling_analysis.rs::modeling_trajectory_modes`. Existing demand types help select rows but do not certify closure. Existing sample mode export uses authored names; endpoint mode has the separate mapping requirement above.

**Producer work scheduled with A2:** a trajectory closure constructor binds selection and coverage, includes required transitions/mode interpretation and preserves original row identities. The initial supported selection can use existing full-symbol/sample membership; a new time-window selector needs an explicit event-boundary rule before it is exposed. Do not silently invent interpolated samples, event states or extrapolated tails. Missing context is a producer/manifest task, not a reason to defer required interpretation to Q1.

### Local scientific analysis

**Sufficient case:** selected original `parametric_sensitivities` rows for a specified run/step/parameter/target, accompanied by the matching `local_validity` for that derived quantity, original candidate/completion permission, parameter/target units and dual/objective conventions, exact original case/binding and assessment context, and explicit subset scope. Preserve conditioning, discrete-assignment conditionality and certified/withheld reasons. For a reduced Hessian retain parameter order, coordinate/objective scales, units and declared objective sense with the selected matrix. For propagated covariance retain the ordered inputs and their assumptions/validity conjunction required to interpret it.

**Insufficient case:** a derivative scalar or covariance matrix with only a solver-success flag; a selected matrix without its parameter/output ordering; or a withheld quantity replaced by zero/an empty certified array. A certified subset cannot be reported as the full requested analysis. An independently reported analysis artifact does not promise rebuilding opaque numerical factors or reproducing the whole original solve.

Decisive source: `workflow/local_analysis.rs::{reason,record,Step,Rows}` and `pse-schema/src/catalog/native_math.rs` local-analysis declarations. `local_validity` explicitly separates certified rows from withholding, and `propagated_covariances` names the input covariance run and ordered coordinates. Native KKT objects and rebuilt factors stay with their numerical owners.

**Producer work scheduled with A2:** persist a bounded analysis context identifying the original operating point/case bindings, relevant numerical/statistical assumptions and input artifact identities, including upstream covariance where consumed. The existing `covariance_run_id` is attribution, not independent protection. For use of an already reported quantity, copy its original validity/assessment and required context; for a promised auditable/recomputable composition, retain its actual input artifacts and state that stronger scope. No fresh KKT calculation is part of promotion. A missing upstream context must refuse that stronger promotion until its producer emits it.

### Persisted graph analysis

**Sufficient case:** a selected incidence or result-evidence graph with original method/configuration/input digest, complete selected node and edge membership, endpoint identities, source revision roots and explicit selection controls. A result-evidence graph also owns every selected quantitative evidence row through independently retained input artifacts, with an exact artifact block/row/field locator. The original producer locator remains provenance; the active locator uses the artifact authority. Pure incidence graphs can legitimately have no numerical result inputs.

**Insufficient case:** graph nodes/edges plus an `ipc:<relation>:<producer-batch>:<row>:<field>` string after the producer blocks are collected; an edge whose endpoint is absent from the selected graph; or graph reachability presented as a numerical derivative. Copying the graph's strings is not quantitative closure.

Decisive source: `workflow/analyses.rs::{result_analysis,store_analysis_graph,AnalysisHandle}`; `canonical_analyses.rs::{seal_analysis_request,persist_analysis}`; `surreal_analyses.rs::{begin,append,activate,read}`. Current result analysis constructs original IPC locator strings and protects whole run/attempt/manifest selections through `canonical_analysis_inputs`. Its bounded source-to-dependent reachability does not invent quantitative values.

**Producer work scheduled with A2:** replace the emitter's producer-only evidence reference with a registry-declared typed artifact locator or an equivalently exact artifact manifest mapping; change input admission, request sealing and readers together. Do not create a historical producer-reader fallback. Keep original coordinates and assessments as provenance. Existing graph staging, endpoint-before-edge insertion and complete-membership activation remain useful foundations; this work does not require a graph algorithm rewrite.

## Atomic admission, obligations and collection

Scientific closure construction runs outside a short visibility transaction while exact source/result protection remains live. Stage immutable capsule/IPC content under an immutable creation intent. Final admission registers the producer retirement conflict authority, checks current protection and original membership, validates staged closure and installs artifact source roots plus the consumer obligation atomically. Activation must not precede that transfer. Retirement-first refuses admission; admission-first completes the transfer before producer protection is released. Uncertain submitted admission retains the exact intent/staging/protection until settlement establishes which effect occurred.

Subsequent study retry reads the artifact, not a new producer lookup. Artifact obligations identify their owning study, user/project reference, analysis, live reader or audit use. Releasing one cannot release another. Acquired copied bytes retain their allocation owner; future pages still require fresh artifact read permission. Terminal execution, caller release, scientific history release and physical transport/service drain are separate operations.

Artifact retirement first fences new acquisition/obligation creation under the same exact authority, then waits for or refuses live obligations and unknown creation/read grants. Collection removes artifact-owned indexes and blocks in bounded restartable pages; independent source roots release only when no retained interpretation/content needs them. Producer result collection does not traverse artifact-owned copies. Releasing graph history removes edges before nodes, then its input obligations and roots, preserving existing creation-window settlement. Do not delete detailed effect state needed for an unresolved acknowledgment to make collection appear complete.

## Reduced replay and compact fences

The target distinguishes payload-retention release from scientific-history release. Current `forget_study_results` deliberately keeps occurrence/outcome receipts; its retirement marker is not automatically a new history-release authority. A terminal state alone authorizes neither history deletion nor destruction of deliberate audit data.

Choose owner-bound target intents/handles for run/study/attempt/point/operation requests: the exact issuing authority and immutable parent run/study identity accompany every operation-only or attempt-only path. Preserve the original operation and scientific identities and their existing hash meaning; add checked ownership context rather than minting a different identity for replay. The adapter/server validates that context against the immutable issued intent/authority before any receipt replay or absence interpretation. Caller-asserted context alone is insufficient. D0 records the affected handle/interface contract, and a changed public boundary receives its required ADR/review before A3 implementation. OP01 permits direct replacement of old context-free paths with no compatibility adapter.

This is consequential: current `canonical_execution.rs::settle_operation(operation,kind,request)` returns `None` when a receipt is absent; `resume_closed_attempt` begins from an operation-only lookup, and `canonical_studies.rs::point_key` is a hash rather than a decodable study locator. Missing records after collection cannot be interpreted as evidence that an old operation never happened. Before removing an operation-to-owner association, A3 must establish how its original immutable binding can still be validated independently of the deleted receipt. If that cannot be established for a supported opaque identity, retain a compact operation-to-owner locator fence; do not infer ownership from a new caller assertion. Such locators retain identity/ownership, not complete scientific requests, and their possible growth with append count is an explicit consequence for A3 to eliminate only with a sound binding contract.

The minimal permanent run/study fence records its exact owner identity/issuing authority/interpretation, irreversible payload/history retirement disposition, terminal attribution where established, and the settled authority boundary. Retain the last terminal attempt identity/class and cancellation attribution needed by the scoped receipt contract, not every scientific diagnostic. Unknown outcomes remain unknown and block history completion. Retained grant-authority incarnation and monotone closed-through floors must not be collected while an original delayed acquisition can still address them. Analysis creation already has an authority/nonce/expiry-bound intent and a closed-through floor; new artifact creation needs equivalent exact settlement semantics.

Before history release, exact immutable request comparisons and full receipts continue serving active/uncertain effects. After completed history retirement, every old mutation/replay returns explicit retired/history-unavailable or the defined minimal terminal attribution; no full original response is fabricated. Reusing the same retired study or operation identity with a different run refuses. A request with the same retired owner but altered bytes also refuses as retired, without retaining its old full request solely to compare it. Fresh work receives a fresh operation identity under its admitted owner. A compact identity is not proof that a scientific solve succeeded.

Permanent compact state can still grow with retained owner identities; this design removes detailed occurrence/request payload growth rather than claiming every trace disappears.

## I1: structural references and exact guards

Select existing typed graph endpoints/ENFORCED relation structure where endpoints exist before insertion; retain explicit guards as the governing lifecycle mechanism. Do not add `REFERENCE` deletion actions or custom cascades for the initial artifact/history target. The scientific relationship is an explicit attributed obligation with owner, kind and settlement—not a bare link that can be removed by deleting its target.

The [record-reference documentation](https://surrealdb.com/docs/reference/query-language/language-primitives/record-references) and reviewed [3.3.0 deletion implementation](https://github.com/surrealdb/surrealdb/blob/v3.3.0/surrealdb/core/src/doc/purge.rs) distinguish typed marked references from strings/opaque payloads, and target deletion from deletion of the referencing object. CASCADE's direction and permission behavior cannot supply scientific release or bounded page collection. REJECT could add a structural check on a durable obligation, but it does not replace exact registration, expiring protection, unknown admission settlement or source closure. With the selected copied-content realization, explicit indexed obligations are already needed for inspectable bounded release; native backlinks would add another representation without removing that machinery.

`substrate.rs::ENFORCED_RELATIONS` already includes memberships, analysis edges/inputs, problem-run and run-source links. Study dependencies intentionally allow a predecessor on a later ingestion page. Preserve that distinction rather than enabling immediate enforcement everywhere. New artifact-input graph endpoints may be enforced after both staged owners exist; no deletion cascade may bypass the operation owner. Revisit REFERENCE only for a concrete relationship where it removes existing machinery and owned RocksDB tests establish both insertion/deletion orders and permission behavior. No such adoption is necessary to settle A0.

## I4: payload, forensic history and physical reclaim

Select existing native bytes and independently owned bounded blocks. Keep engine VERSION disabled and deliberate scientific/audit history explicit. Current `scripts/surreal_server.py::database_endpoint` selects `sync=every&versioned=false`; `substrate.rs` bounds result blocks/messages and stores payload bytes. The [bucket interface](https://surrealdb.com/docs/reference/query-language/statements/define/bucket) introduces experimental files and external storage lifetimes. No inspected scientific consumer requires that additional publication/backup/reclaim boundary for these products. Current moving-main documentation is not evidence that a different backend/version profile is enabled here.

`scripts/test_resources.py::remove_database` checks exact logical REMOVE acknowledgment. The reviewed 3.3.0 engine separately queues key reclamation; RocksDB file-space return is a further physical outcome. Keep these names distinct. There is no current capacity consumer in this scientific inquiry that requires a reclaimed-byte promise, so no physical churn benchmark or compaction adoption is needed for this decision. If a capacity admission policy later consumes disk recovery, its owner must measure an authenticated owned RocksDB fixture's logical disposal and subsequent physical outcome separately. A timer, TTL or routine per-run compaction does not authorize deletion.

## I5: access paths for the selected target

Current source establishes declared indexes and query shapes, not measured plans for the future schema. Seed candidates use layout/preparation/backend, recorded descending run/attempt/step order, eligibility before the limit and retirement exclusion; exact admission follows discovery. Current result readers use indexed result-set/output/partition/range/block membership. Retention looks up live result protections and analysis inputs by run and collects owned blocks/indexes in bounded pages. Existing graph read pages select analysis/key; study readiness uses eligible/ordinal and bounded outgoing dependency notification. Preserve those meanings rather than substituting graph syntax for exact selection.

Required target paths are: exact artifact identity; owner-scoped input obligation uniqueness; obligations/read grants by artifact and owning consumer; staged/active/retiring artifact membership; artifact block order and original selected row locator; artifact source roots by owner; owner-bound retirement/fence lookup; and graph evidence locator resolution through its artifact. Provide ordered cursor indexes for collection and incoming borrower inspection. Preserve seed eligibility/order before any cutoff when independent seed discovery is added. These are proposed declarations/access contracts, not claims that current indexes already serve the new schema.

Do not select LIGHTWEIGHT edges for obligations or evidence carrying owner, role, provenance or settlement, and do not select INLINE caches to hide arbitrary string joins. The chosen graph has attributed records and bounded input/read lifetimes. A native relation can implement its actual structural edges without erasing those attributes. Revisit only against a named query that loses meaningful repeated work and retains the complete contract.

The decisive future probe uses the target generated declarations and a small mixed fixture: several artifacts, one shared borrower, unrelated producer payload and multiple candidate eligibility classes. Inspect plain EXPLAIN for intended access paths; compare exact selection and bounded cursor continuation to an independent expected set. `canonical_capability_tests.rs` records that trailing EXPLAIN FULL at 3.3 invokes execution/AnalyzePlan, so FULL also belongs in an owned fixture. This inquiry makes no current latency, scan-count or capacity claim and adds no permanent instrumentation.

## I7: retained local retry scope

Keep local-first deployment and truthful loss/retry. `Operations::recover` records original worker loss with no scientific completion and unavailable seed eligibility; the study owner projects that actual outcome into its shared retry policy. `resume_closed_attempt` resumes publication/settlement authority, not an integrator's numerical state. Existing root/NLP/HiGHS seeds, partial trajectories, final states, backups and VERSION history are not durable numerical continuation checkpoints.

A future checkpoint requirement would need the workload owner to choose sufficient state: physical time and mode, state and required rates, event/schedule position, input segment, quadratures/sensitivity state and any horizon/window/accepted-role state actually consumed, with source/provider/numerical compatibility and an explicit equivalence claim. Relevant source is `workflow/modeling/dynamics.rs` and `workflow/study_operations.rs`; current study seed vocabulary treats simulation/fit/horizon separately from an explicit algebraic seed. Capturing those new checkpoints, remote binding and search/GQL/WASM/SurrealML/cloud features are not selected by A0 or RC07. Revisit under a concrete supported consumer and product decision; no numerical probe is needed to prove a capability that this target does not claim.

## Revealing implementation controls and remaining boundary

The decisions above are sufficient to write the dependent A1/A2/A3 production contracts; they do not establish implementation acceptance. Required producer metadata/emitter work is named with each product and belongs with its A2 consumer, not postponed to Q1. D0 must record the new artifact relation/operation and owner-bound reduced-replay contracts before coding them. A4 is independent and its concurrent execution does not change these scientific meanings.

Source-only evidence settles the initial bytes/copy/explicit-guard/local-retry choices. No decisive service probe is required before recording them. When A1/A2/A3 implementation is authorized, the smallest revealing controls are:

- Seed promotion from the existing tiny KINSOL LINEAR case; admission/retirement forced in both commit orders; restart before consumer dispatch; retry after worker loss; independently released studies; exact original bits/permission preserved; expired producer read refused while artifact acquisition succeeds.
- Final endpoint at a non-requested termination time and a missing requested tail; verify exact coordinate/unit/mode/integral interpretation and partiality after producer collection. Refuse a last-sample-only promotion.
- Selected trajectory spanning an event/mode transition; unrelated symbol payload drains while required transition/coverage survives. Refuse a scope that omits interpretation needed at its boundary.
- Certified and withheld local quantities, a selected matrix with coordinate ordering, and propagated covariance with an independently retained upstream input. Refuse missing context and never convert withholding to zero.
- Persisted result-evidence graph whose artifact-owned row/field locator still resolves after the producer is collected; reject absent selected endpoints/evidence. Structural reachability remains distinct from quantitative permission.
- Explicit exploratory history release versus retained audit/unknown effect; restart between collection pages; replay every affected mutation/recovery family; old study identity with a different run; missing old receipt and delayed old creator cannot lead to native redispatch or content resurrection.

Adapt the existing `canonical_result_retention.rs::{result_read_and_retirement_conflict_on_exact_selection,multipage_result_read_hands_protection_to_analysis_before_retirement}`, `workflow/durable_tests.rs::canonical_admitted_seed_shares_payload_and_refuses_fresh_retired_acquisition`, canonical study lost-ACK/creation-cancellation tests and analysis race tests. Their bodies are source leads, not fresh passes. Use fixture ownership and explicit force-validation routes; root coordinates native/service execution and assembled qualification. The assigned investigation makes no numerical model adequacy, whole-simulator, physical-space recovery or assembled lifetime acceptance claim.
