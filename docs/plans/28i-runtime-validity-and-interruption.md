# 28i: Receiving-runtime validity and interruption

## Responsibility, decisions and baseline

This companion of [Plan 28](28-surrealdb-unified-substrate.md) develops receiving-runtime
protection, reusable validity and interruption for mathematical preparation. It supplies
the runtime premise contract to [28j](28j-pure-preparation-and-publication.md), selected
preparation in [28b](28b-selected-compilation-and-reuse.md), and actual worker/Python roots.
[28h](28h-native-setup-and-artifact-identity.md) continues to own native installation,
artifact association and setup. Native generation pins are useful inputs, not protection of
Python's entire executable closure. [28c](28c-durable-execution-and-studies.md) owns study
dispatch and native drain; [28e](28e-rebuild-retirement-and-qualification.md) owns assembled
evidence. The coordinator alone owns PA01/PA04 and U01 dispositions.

The maintainer accepted the preparation review's RC01 and RC04 on **2026-10-08**, together
with RC02/RC03. The [coordinator's decision record](28-surrealdb-unified-substrate.md#preparation-rule-decisions-2026-10-08)
owns that acceptance and the decision route. Accepting the direction does not establish the
premises of a broader lifetime. I0 must settle them before I1 extends any guarantee.

The authoring baseline is dirty `main` at `2410880efc5ab20e623006fde78e624044d3358d`.
The [review](../design_review/reviews/design_review_preparation-assurance-and-reuse_2026-10-08.md)
and [incident evidence](../design_review/evidence/preparation-assurance-and-reuse-2026-10-08.md)
distinguish inspected source from the frozen executing extension. The sampled SHA dominance
establishes present relevance, not every preparation cost or the benefit of this proposal.

Current source has exact role/artifact/configuration observations, opaque replay admission,
strict portable envelopes, source protection and a bounded overlapping namespace cohort.
`FileObservation::capture` hashes full files; `LocalRuntimeObservation::capture` also
verifies the observed file set; `LocalReplay::with_current` verifies around guarded use.
`validated_namespace` can share an overlapping observation, but sealing removes the cohort:
sequential bodies and points repeat observation. Capture and condition-variable waiting
have no caller stop input. Current job cancellation still joins work and retains its charges.

Blueprint §14.3–§14.4 and ADR-0164 describe the current receiving profile. Upstream glibc
2.39 list exclusion does not freeze all symbol/provider/configuration state, authenticate
mapped memory, or qualify distribution patches and interposition. No wrong result or actual
deadlock follows merely from those limitations. I0 must connect a consumed premise to its
protection rather than treating a platform limitation as proof of a consumer defect.

## Target validity contract

The receiving root establishes eligibility at a real trust transition and retains an
opaque owner only while the premises of that eligibility remain protected. Preparation
consumers receive that owner explicitly; they do not independently recapture the host
merely because another semantic body is used. This is a responsibility, not a requirement
for a new public framework or serializable authority token.

| Boundary | Required behavior |
|---|---|
| Receiving qualification | Observe actual artifact/role, consumed runtime interpretation and effective configuration; qualify exact persisted payload/dependencies separately. A new process/deployment cannot inherit an in-memory success. |
| Protected reuse | Use the same owned validity for compatible immutable construction within its supported extent. No timestamp, unchanged loader counter or cached boolean substitutes for actual protection. |
| Relevant change | Renew affected admission, refuse replay, or take fresh scientific preparation before using an incompatible product. Do not reinterpret historical identity frames. |
| Uncontrolled context | Retain fresh preparation and historical result reads without claiming deployment-local replay eligibility. No silent promotion to source-qualified producer status. |
| Numerical execution | Keep provider handles, mutable evaluators, factors and solver state under their existing owners. Runtime validity authorizes neither a solver success nor reuse of value-dependent numerical facts. |
| Final release | Release validity and its resources at the actual end of owned use; surviving children/consumers retain their real owners. |

Qualified-source replay and exact deployment-local replay remain distinct guarantees.
Both preserve current receiving interpretation and payload checks. Original per-case
domains, bounds, residuals, closure and outcome checks remain independent of replay validity.

Do not hold a process-wide loader lock across a study, asynchronous store request, native
solve, provider callback or user code. A short reconstruction exclusion and an operation's
validity owner have different purposes. Reusing a validity owner must not expand the set of
operations safe inside the existing callback, acquire the GIL under the loader lock, or
convert a file observation into an atomic filesystem/mapped-memory snapshot claim.

## I0 — Settle receiving premises and the supported lifetime

Inspect actual worker and imported-Python roots, immutable reconstruction, configuration
capture, loader use, native generation owners and provider entry. Start with
`pse-buildinfo::{identity,loader_scope}`, runtime `math/portable`, Python/worker replay mints,
and 28h's actual operation/generation owners. Follow only dependencies needed to answer a
consequential consumed-premise question.

For each supported root, explain what reconstruction and subsequent product use read, who can change it, what excludes
or detects that change before use, and when protection ends. Distinguish code-list membership,
symbol scope, actual file bytes, configuration and later native/provider use. Determine which
of the broad observed inputs are consumed assurance premises and which are incidental to an
existing conservative profile; changing that profile requires its decision route, not omission.
Use the existing exact upstream source examination. Research a distribution-specific detail
or library API only if it changes the chosen protection argument; current-version snippets do
not qualify another pinned release.

Compare complete alternatives:

| Alternative | Selection condition and consequence |
|---|---|
| Trust-transition qualification, then independent immutable products | Examine first whether sealed specifications, receipts and prepared mathematics carry complete consumed meaning without later ambient runtime reads. If established, protect construction/qualification and preserve already admitted validity without a whole-host freeze. Check actual global mathematical state/library use; immutability alone is not proof. |
| Protected preparation operation | Preferred where remaining mutable consumed premises require an authentic owner over the actual immutable preparation extent. Avoids introducing whole-deployment management solely for one request. |
| Immutable runtime/deployment generation | Select where existing roots can actually own the consumed closure and configuration across operations. Native installation pins alone are insufficient; name additional obligations and their costs. |
| Scoped fresh preparation | Use where neither lifetime can be established, including uncontrolled embedding/plugin cases. Preserve execution and history; state the restart-reuse support limit honestly. |

I0 completes with a supported-root decision, its protection/release and mutation behavior,
specific refusal/fresh-route cases, and any still-unqualified profile. A plausible interface
is insufficient. If U01 remains unresolved for a root, record the exact missing premise and
block broader reuse for that root; other settled roots and pure effect work need not wait.
No arbitrary-host guarantee, permanent monitoring system or new proof artifact is required.

## I1 — Owned validity through actual preparation

Consume I0's supported lifetime and the coordinator's required decision/design amendment.
Consume J0's settled receiving/effect interface and integrate alongside the applicable
J1/B7 entry slice; completed B7 migration is not a prerequisite for I1.
Move receiving admission and compatible sequential body use to the actual preparation owner.
Preserve full receiving checks at new trust transitions, strict payload qualification and
changed-context refusal. Bind validity explicitly into 28j's effectful receiving step and
28b's preparation entry; pure mathematical products retain no ambient host lookup authority.

Root migration includes direct Rust runtime, worker and imported Python consumers in the
supported profile. An explicitly unsupported root takes the honest fresh route. Existing
ordinary replay and strict-producer controls must continue to distinguish guarantees.
The target does not add an alternate compatibility API or retain the old body-granularity
assurance protocol after its applicable callers move.

Acceptance distinguishes initial full receiving observation from unchanged sequential body
uses, concurrent independent preparations and renewed admission after changed premises.
An unchanged thousand-point value sweep must not multiply whole-host observation by its
body/point count within a legitimately protected extent. This states work placement, not
a promise of a particular latency or a universal single observation across uncontrolled work.

## I2 — Interruptible observation and shared waits

Make remaining capture/reconstruction boundaries and observer waits consume the operation's
stop and original clock. Check between file-read chunks and before/after consequential stages.
Do not promise forced interruption inside an opaque foreign call; its owner remains charged
and joins it. Observation refusal, caller cancellation and deadline remain typed distinctions,
not mathematical incompatibility or implicit solver fallback.
Cancellation or expiry must not publish a usable namespace or trigger fresh expensive
preparation. Waiting to acquire glibc's internal loader lock is itself not made interruptible
by checks inside the callback; avoid long assurance under that lock or state the remaining
foreign wait limitation honestly. Polling does not establish a maximum stop latency.

Use existing completion-owned job/flight and resource mechanisms. A follower may stop waiting
without cancelling work still required by other consumers. Shared work must not remain owned
only by the first departing caller. Name the leader's finite operation clock, consumer
attachment/detachment, last-consumer settlement and actual drain. No retry resets the caller's
clock. A poisoned or panicking leader settles all followers; cache clear/retirement fences
late insertion. Avoid retaining a scarce compute admission merely to wait for unrelated
shared observation where the existing owners can place that wait before compute entry.

I0 fixes the intended lifetime; I2 can design interruption before I1 completes, but its
integrated implementation must target the selected owner rather than extending a displaced
cohort as a second protocol. Existing one-shot cohort tests may be replaced with controls of
the new lifetime; tests specific to a deleted mechanism are retired with that mechanism.

## Outcome

### What was built

**Implemented:** I0 settled the controlled worker/imported-Python profile. I1 qualifies
actual receiving trust transitions and consumes independent immutable mathematics thereafter;
I2 supplies stop/original-clock-aware observation, follower waits and completion-owned
startup/reconstruction. The namespace cohort and repeated body-level host observation
protocol are deleted. Native allocation ownership lasts through actual thread/TLS join,
and private receiving construction drains before its protected source is released.
Blueprint §14.3–§14.4 and §5.3 own the enduring explanation; proposed ADR-0164 records
the amendment without changing its decision status.

**Tested:** the named startup, deadline, follower, private-drain, callback/attribute and
receiving controls are recorded with commands, native/force-validation conditions and
zero-baseline results in [28e's correction outcome](28e-rebuild-retirement-and-qualification.md#outcome-recorded-after-implementation).
That owner also records affected scientific qualification and its repaired composite boundary.

### A mistake made and corrected

Independent inspection found that an asynchronous timeout could detach private receiving
work before releasing its source protection, and that loader children could acquire a fresh
clock instead of consuming the original driver deadline. Both paths now retain their owners
through completion and use the original absolute clock. A generic SDK request deadline was
also incorrectly used as the whole scientific preparation budget; actual RPC deadlines and
finite preparation lifetimes are now distinct typed refusals. Solver stopping and physical
acceptance policies were preserved.

### Deviations from the plan, deliberate

The selected profile protects the consumed immutable mathematical products rather than
claiming a freeze of an arbitrary Python host. Callback-bearing symbol state, live reset,
uncontrolled mapping mutation, interposition and arbitrary embedding remain unqualified.
Foreign initialization/configuration and loader-lock acquisition are checked before/after;
they are not forcibly interrupted. Qualification follows the maintainer's selected failed
identities and necessary composition controls, without another full campaign or a speedup claim.
