# Finite topology exploration

## Outcome and baseline

**Proposed.** This companion of [Plan73](73-symbolica-ecosystem-improvements.md) delivers O09: a researcher generates a finite circulation family, inspects structurally distinct candidates and requests ordinary physical admission and scientific evaluation. Enumeration completeness, physical admission and scientific feasibility are distinct results. Own conversion, operation/result delivery and independent reading here, without waiting for unfinished Plan66 packages.

The new capability is a finite topology-candidate operation with two selected process families and an ordinary scientific evaluation consumer. It adds a family request/admission contract, native generation adapter, role-preserving conversion to authored source, and candidate-set result/reader. It also extends existing native operation, schema and publication boundaries for those products. Typed authoring, compiler specialization, physical flow admission, structural analysis, initialization, native solver selection and protected result custody remain the foundations. Graphica supplies generation and canonicalization; it does not replace those scientific owners or authorize reuse of results between isomorphic physical models. The inspected flowsheet/compiler/recycle owners contain physical graph projection and science rather than an incumbent topology-candidate operation. Deletions below concern replaced integration paths or provisional delivery code if introduced; they do not remove the current suitable graph/solver owners.

**Interface-checked.** Baseline `baadc7e32` includes typed flowsheet declarations, physical ports, parallel connection occurrences and grouped tears in `pse-structural/src/flowsheet.rs`. Existing authored units and recycle examples provide science, not topology enumeration. Retain suitable native graph and solver owners; no universal synthesizer, graph service or replacement tear algorithm is selected.

## Finite first family and scientific consumer

The open family contains exactly one mixer M, splitter S, heater H and cooler C, one fresh boundary and one purge boundary. Fresh enters M; purge leaves S. Other connections form one directed circulation visiting each internal instance once. Named fresh/recycle and purge/continuation roles are fixed. With M as anchor, permutations of S/H/C give six role-distinct candidates. H/C are distinct scientific roles; occurrence spelling is renameable. No extra equipment, self-loop, disconnected component or degree-two subdivision is admitted.

The closed control contains exactly H/C with two opposite directed material connections and no external legs. Ordinary science fixes circulation flow, pressure and a thermal datum with declared heat/cool targets; do not add an artificial feed for a generator convention. Use ordinary authored mixer/splitter and heater implementations (negative duty/declared cooling realization for C), constant-cp material, explicit pressure treatment, feed state and positive purge fraction. [G03](#G03) distinguishes imposed case equations from independently assessed targets so a redundant loop-energy equation is not mistaken for a well-posed closed control. Incompatible targets remain a scientific rejection rather than changing the structural population.

Each candidate passes ordinary physical typing, degrees-of-freedom/structural checks, initialization and original-coordinate conservation/duty assessment. Include a physical port-contract mismatch and a scientifically incompatible candidate. A generated graph never certifies feasibility.

## Native generation and scientific interpretation

Use Graphica3.0.1 `Graph::generate`, typed directed half-edge signatures, native filters and canonicalization. The [generation contract](../library_leverage/libraries/graphica/canonical-generation.md) and [mathematical correspondence account](../library_leverage/libraries/graphica/mathematical-template-reuse.md) establish the selected source conditions.

Signatures are reusable kinds, not exact counts; generated internal payloads are `Default`, not typed equipment instances, and signature order is not named-port order. Generate neutral signatures with fresh/purge edge kinds and explicit total vertex/equipment bounds. Decorate the two degree-two positions with H/C using a finite permutation operation, then canonicalize the role-labeled interpreted candidates. Validate exact inventory and single circulation on completed candidates. This avoids claiming the generator assigned named internal science.

Prune only monotonic impossibilities in partial `filter_fn` checks. Missing final motifs and exact final counts cannot be rejected before their completion is impossible. `max_loops` alone does not exclude arbitrarily many degree-two subdivisions; explicit vertex bounds and the inventory predicate establish finiteness. G02 must show the native generation/filter configuration actually enforces those bounds. If the selected supplier API cannot bound recursive exploration before materialization, own a checked supplier extension under B's control contract before widening the family; do not replace native enumeration with a second bespoke search.

Role-colored incidence vertices carry units, ports and connection occurrences where mapping needs them: Graphica supplies a vertex map but no canonical edge map. Preserve parallel edges and named roles; physical identity is not a dummy index. A owns mapped mathematical reuse, E native indexed mathematics. Neither graph equality nor symmetry grants provider/mathematical equivalence.

Configure closed-network bridge/root options deliberately. Their topology conventions are not conservation or physical flow. Generation emits connected candidates; canonicalization also accepts empty/disconnected inputs, which are contract controls but outside this family. Directed/undirected self-loop factors and exposed vertex generators do not imply complete edge actions or statistical weights. `HiddenData` excludes metadata from comparison, not display or canonical provenance ordering.

## Lifecycle, operations and publication

The request declares family/version, equipment templates, physical role interpretation, explicit bounds/predicates, selected science and demand. Candidate identity includes application interpretation and sorted canonical structure; do not use map iteration or a display string as identity. Preserve authored declarations/source and generation premises for each member.

Generation retains a result map; progress callbacks do not transfer ownership. Abort polling occurs at connected completed candidates, without guaranteed bounded latency through recursion/canonicalization. Report requested stop, actual settlement and incomplete enumeration honestly. The tiny selected family makes map retention appropriate. Do not promise streaming ownership or instantaneous cancellation.

Keep completed enumeration with scientific rejects distinct from interrupted enumeration and successful solves. Retain unsupported, physically refused and numerically failed members. Publish typed candidate sets through current operation/result custody and independent native readers/exports with role, source, units and partiality. Rendering consumes the original product without rerunning science. DOT/Mermaid helpers may project an actual result after escaping/metadata checks; do not migrate suitable existing graphs solely for formatting.

### G01 — Admit the finite family, its physical roles and its exact population

**Change and current boundary.** `crates/pse-structural/src/flowsheet.rs::Declaration` already describes explicitly identified nodes, scalar physical ports, parallel connection occurrences and grouped tear decisions. `crates/pse-compiler/src/workspace/modeling/flow.rs::SemanticModeling::flow_graph` obtains those facts from an admitted specialized model; it does not generate process alternatives. Add a runtime research request beside the existing modeling/study workflow owners that names the selected source revision, equipment definitions/templates, finite family/version, output demand and optional ordinary scientific evaluation settings. It produces an admitted family interpretation, not an admitted physical flowsheet. Keep generated graph indices out of the typed flow authority.

**Family contract.** For the open family, internal equipment is exactly one M, S, H and C, with two external boundary roles. Fresh enters M's named fresh inlet; M's named recycle inlet closes the circulation. S's named purge outlet goes to the boundary; its named continuation outlet remains on the circulation. M has two incoming and one outgoing material role, S one incoming and two outgoing, and H/C one incoming and one outgoing. The four internal instances form a single directed cycle. H and C are distinct equipment roles, while instance spelling is renameable. Fresh/recycle and purge/continuation roles cannot be exchanged by canonicalization. Disallow extra units, self-loops, internal branches, isolated units and degree-two subdivisions in this selected family.

The independent expected open population, written as directed cycles anchored at M, is `M-S-H-C-M`, `M-S-C-H-M`, `M-H-S-C-M`, `M-H-C-S-M`, `M-C-S-H-M`, and `M-C-H-S-M`. Each also contains fresh→M and S→purge. These six lists are an independent acceptance oracle, not a second production enumerator. The closed family is exactly H→C→H, with two internal units, two directed connection occurrences and no boundary roles. Canonicalizing its two H/C decorations yields one role-labeled candidate. It is a real scientific consumer of the operation, not an empty-external-list API smoke test.

**Identity and interfaces.** The admitted request retains neutral generator settings separately from physical template interpretation. Candidate identity includes family/version, role-labeled canonical structure, connection-occurrence/port interpretation and selected immutable template/source context. Scientific evaluation identity additionally includes case specification, demanded observations and numerical settings through existing operation admission. A rename changes presentation/source correspondence without creating a new structural candidate; changing H to C, changing a named port or changing the scientific template is meaningful. `HiddenData` may hold display/source annotation excluded from comparison only when its exclusion is justified; it still prints and requires controlled projection. No display string or hash-map iteration order is a persisted identity.

Declare these meanings and typed admission/refusal results through the series decision route before production depends on a new relation or operation contract. Add catalog declarations under `crates/pse-schema/src/catalog/modeling_analysis.rs` and `catalog/operations.rs` as their current responsibilities require. Reuse existing `OperationSource` and source-selection semantics from `workflow/study_operations.rs`; an operation cannot reopen an unversioned latest template during generation or evaluation. Keep enumeration completeness, candidate structural acceptance, physical admission and scientific use as separately represented facts.

**Ready and complete.** G01 needs the series source/product contract and current typed source/flow admission, not completion of A's mathematics or unfinished Plan66 work. Targeted request tests must accept both finite families and refuse contradictory inventories, unbounded settings, missing named ports, an unknown template/source, duplicate equipment/connection identities and an incompatible family version before generation. Independently authored six/one populations and the corresponding port-role maps become fixtures consumed by G02/G03. Delete any provisional family interpretation that derives physical roles solely from vertex degree, signature order or display spelling once this admitted contract takes over; preserve the existing typed flowsheet and tear APIs.

### G02 — Generate bounded neutral graphs, decorate roles and canonicalize their correspondence

**Selected library composition.** Use Graphica 3.0.1 `Graph::generate(external_edges, vertex_signatures, settings)` with `HalfEdge::{incoming,outgoing}` and explicit edge-kind payloads. Internal payloads are `N::default()` and the library sorts signatures, so signatures select reusable allowed incident multisets rather than exact equipment instances or port order. Use native generation for the search, then a finite equipment decoration step and `Graph::canonize` for role-sensitive deduplication. The [source-qualified generation account](../library_leverage/libraries/graphica/canonical-generation.md) and [selected implementation](https://docs.rs/crate/graphica/3.0.1/source/src/lib.rs) establish the API, filter and retained-map semantics. Supplier contract corrections belong to B's controlled-source route; do not create an application enumerator as a workaround.

**Concrete open generator.** Define edge kinds Fresh, Purge and Circulation. External Fresh is an outgoing Fresh half-edge; external Purge an incoming Purge half-edge. Allowed internal neutral signatures are M=`{incoming Fresh, incoming Circulation, outgoing Circulation}`, S=`{incoming Circulation, outgoing Circulation, outgoing Purge}`, and X=`{incoming Circulation, outgoing Circulation}`. The special edge kinds locate the M/S roles without requiring an internal node payload from the supplier. Set `max_vertices(6)`, counting the two boundary nodes as the inspected API does; `max_loops(1)`; `allow_self_loops(false)`; and `allow_zero_flow_edges(true)`, so a supplier zero-flow-bridge convention cannot silently remove a process candidate. A maximum bridge count of two is compatible with the fresh/purge legs; the final family predicate still owns the actual boundary interpretation.

For `filter_fn(graph, completed_vertices)`, use the supplied completed-prefix boundary to prune only irreversible violations: excessive vertex/equipment counts, an already completed forbidden signature, or a bound proving that the remaining exact inventory can no longer fit. A missing M, S or circulation in a partial graph is not yet a refusal. At completed graphs require exactly six total vertices, the two supplied boundaries, one M, one S and two X positions, exactly one internal directed circulation visiting all four internal instances, the two prescribed boundary legs and no other edges. These final predicates establish the accepted family; `max_loops` alone would admit arbitrarily many degree-two X subdivisions and cannot establish finiteness. The native `max_vertices` check occurs at recursive entry, before further generation from an over-bound graph; qualify that contract and its total-vertex meaning on the selected source.

Decorate the two X positions with the finite permutations of `[H,C]`, using Numerica `unique_permutations` or its current re-export rather than a second search. That function returns an owned finite permutation vector with multiplicity; the two-slot population makes this materialization appropriate. Canonicalize the fully role-labeled interpreted graphs after decoration: the neutral generator itself cannot distinguish H and C. Native search should yield the admissible neutral cycle placements, and interpretation/deduplication yields the six independently listed role orders. Do not multiply their scientific counts by Graphica automorphism sizes or permutation multiplicities.

**Concrete closed generator.** Pass an empty external list, the single neutral X signature, `max_vertices(2)`, `max_loops(1)`, `max_bridges(0)`, `allow_self_loops(false)` and `allow_zero_flow_edges(true)`. The inspected API seeds one default internal vertex when external edges are empty; it does not require a fabricated boundary. Require exactly two vertices and two opposite directed Circulation edges in the completed graph, decorate H/C, then canonicalize to one role-labeled result. Generation's weak connectivity check is adequate as a supplier prerequisite but does not replace the directed single-cycle predicate. Open and closed requests retain their actual settings so defaults cannot silently change this population.

**Correspondence and candidate construction.** Graphica returns an original-to-canonical vertex map, not a canonical edge map. For scientific correspondence, construct a role-colored incidence representation that explicitly contains unit roles, named port roles and connection-occurrence vertices; canonicalize that representation and transport its vertex mapping back to the interpreted candidate. Store this mapping with the original generated/decorated graph and source role table. This preserves parallel connection occurrences without endpoint-based coalescing and supplies an explicit target for every physical port/occurrence in G03. `canonize_edges` may normalize order with fixed vertex identities; it cannot recover omitted scientific role labels or identify an edge action. A owns any use of mapped correspondence for shared mathematics; G does not infer provider or model equality from this graph.

**Completion and interruption.** Own the returned map until conversion/publication has consumed or retained its actual graphs. `generate` returns `Ok(map)` for completed enumeration and `Err(map)` for abortion; both maps contain original accumulated candidates. `abort_check` is polled only after a connected completed candidate, before later validation/canonicalization, and `progress_fn` observes insertion of a new unique graph. Neither callback transfers map ownership nor guarantees bounded cancellation latency through recursion/canonicalization. Record the requested stop and the actual returned settlement separately. The selected tiny vertex/degree envelope permits retained-map materialization; widening it requires an explicit search/output/lifecycle assessment, not a promise that a callback makes generation streaming.

**Ready and complete.** Start after G01 and the selected supplier bounds/canonical-map contract are qualified. Tests compare the native result to the independently authored six/one lists, verify internal count and no subdivisions, and demonstrate the empty-external closed control survives the chosen bridge settings. Rename vertices/occurrences and reorder input declarations without changing the structural population; exchanging H/C or fresh/recycle roles must remain visible. Include port-colored parallel occurrences and directed/undirected self-loop canonicalization as contract controls outside the selected generated family. Interrupt at an actual callback and retain an honestly incomplete settled map. Remove provisional hand enumeration, signature-index-to-port assumptions and display-based identity when this path replaces them; retain the independent population fixtures as oracles.

### G03 — Turn each candidate into ordinary typed source and independently assessed science

**Conversion boundary and steps.** A generated graph is not a `pse-structural::flowsheet::Declaration`: that projection contains admitted physical ports and explicit tear decisions derived from a specialized model. Add the candidate-to-authoring adapter beside runtime modeling workflows. It consumes G01's frozen equipment templates and G02's unit/port/connection correspondence, and produces ordinary `pse_authoring::language::Declaration` instances for the selected flowsheet, material connections and case. Use the existing authoring renderer in `crates/pse-authoring/src/language/render.rs` when source files are needed. Admit the candidate against its exact parent through `workflow/modeling/canonical/delta.rs::with_delta` or the native parsed-file delta owner when its boundary requires files. Each candidate gets its own retained derived revision; candidates are not successive overwrites of one mutable source head. Persist original generated premises and source correspondence with that revision.

For each M/S/H/C occurrence, instantiate the selected ordinary authored definition with declared material/quantity conventions and bind the named material ports from the retained role table. Resolve material ports to their authoritative scalar coordinates through normal modeling admission. Do not create scalar ports from graph edge labels or inject equations directly into a prepared numerical plan. Assign an original semantic identity to every connection occurrence and an explicit tear group/cost/policy under the current vocabulary. Reuse the existing `FlowSelectionDocument`/`ModelingFlowSelection` and `SemanticModeling::flow_graph` in `pse-compiler/src/workspace/modeling/flow.rs`. The compiler refuses cut connections, unknown instances, conflicting group policy and physical mismatch; `pse-structural::flowsheet::FlowGraph` owns admitted connectivity and existing graph/tear algorithms after that boundary.

**Concrete ordinary science.** Author constant-cp material with `cp=30 J/(mol K)`, an explicit enthalpy datum and a validity interval covering the control states. Use the current ordinary mixer/splitter definitions and heater definition, with C an explicitly declared negative-duty cooling realization. The open default evaluation supplies fresh flow 1 mol/s at 300 K, a purge fraction of 1/2, pressure-preserving unit/connection treatment with a 100 kPa feed datum, heater duty +600 W and cooler duty −600 W. The mass balance then gives M outlet flow 2 mol/s, purge flow 1 mol/s and recycle-to-M flow 1 mol/s. H/C can lie before or after S, so their duty-to-temperature changes use their actual 2 or 1 mol/s stream, rather than assuming every unit sees the same flow. All six orderings must use their actual authored connections and original case equations.

The closed default evaluation fixes circulation flow at 1 mol/s, pressure at 100 kPa and H inlet temperature at 300 K. Impose +600 W at H; solve C's duty through the ordinary energy equations and assess its declared cooling target −600 W. Expected H outlet/C inlet temperature is 320 K, and C outlet/H inlet is 300 K. The target comparison is explicit scientific acceptance, not another fixed duty equation: prescribing both duties and then adding a thermal datum can leave a redundant periodic energy equation. The case owner must show its actual fixed/free and equation selections are well posed. Retain the distinction between an imposed specification and an independently checked target in request/result attribution. A changed cooling target of −500 W is a scientifically incompatible request whose original candidate remains in the complete structural population. It must not cause a hidden target change or a fabricated fresh feed.

**Preparation and execution.** Carry the generated source through `workflow/modeling/structural.rs::analyze_structure` and the ordinary `workflow/modeling/cases.rs` resolution/preparation owner. Structural incidence/DOF evidence does not establish numerical rank or feasibility. Apply an explicitly selected supported initialization/solve strategy, preserving current recycle semantics from blueprint §17.4 and actual `workflow/modeling/recycle_tests.rs` consumers. Existing native solver routing and `pse-backend-native/src/solve` execute the qualified class; a graph motif cannot select a solver or certify a conditional unit's locality. Share compatible immutable preparation through A where admitted; mutable native state, starts and result permission remain candidate-specific. Keep physical refusal, structural refusal, unsupported numerical route, solve failure, completed solve and independent target/conservation assessment separate.

For successful open candidates, independently assess original-coordinate component/material closure, enthalpy/duty closure and pressure treatment. With equal and opposite duties, global feed/purge energy gives purge temperature 300 K; per-unit temperature changes follow `Q/(n_dot×cp)` on the actual stream. For the closed control, check H/C duties sum to zero and both connection states agree in original units, using the actual fixed circulation and thermal datum. The independent checks must use retained original observations, not the same generated residual assembly as their sole oracle. Numerical residual tolerances, output units, sign convention, quantity basis and property validity remain the ordinary model contract.

**Ready and complete.** G02 must provide candidate/source correspondence and current ordinary source/case owners must be operational; no topology-specific replacement for them is permitted. Tests convert all six open candidates and the one closed candidate, round-trip their authored source/correspondence and evaluate the supported controls through ordinary native science. Independently constructed quantity-mismatched ports must refuse during physical admission before a solver starts. Include changed incompatible cooling targets, underdetermined closed data and out-of-validity states with their original typed causes. Confirm a failed scientific member remains enumerable/readable and does not reduce the six/one structural denominator. Remove provisional graph-to-solver injection, hand-coded heater/mixer evaluation and in-memory-only candidate source shortcuts once ordinary conversion passes. Existing compiler, petgraph/rustworkx, tear and native solve owners remain in use.

No test may infer complete enumeration from a nonempty map or successful science from canonical equality.

**Source leads and retained boundaries.** G01/G02 close inventory, signature, monotonic filter, explicit vertex bound, closed-root/bridge and complete/incomplete map semantics. G02/G03 close vertex-only canonical correspondence, port/occurrence coloring, parallel edges and ordinary typed conversion. Include empty/disconnected canonicalization as supplier contract controls while keeping connected generation's selected family unchanged. For automorphism controls, identical directed self-loops contribute `m!` and undirected self-loops `2^m m!`; exposed vertex generators omit complete edge actions. Symmetry sizes are descriptive mathematical output, not candidate probability or a thermodynamic multiplicity. Combinations/permutations are consumed at finite decoration, and display/edge helpers remain faithful projections in G04.

Whole-flowsheet caching is not selected by G; A's independent-motif comparison owns mathematical reuse and its scientific mapping qualifications. **Ready and handoff.** G05 starts after G01–G04 functional delivery, source migration and replaced-path deletion.
