## J04 — Decide and attribute benefit evidence

Separate removed structural work from measured benefit. The plan expects fewer duplicate
reads/row hydrations, repeated declarations, forwarding and process launches where the migration
actually removes them. Those are Interface-checked/Implemented claims once present; they do
not establish elapsed speed, peak memory, capacity, compile/startup savings or portability.

Choose a measurement only when it changes a consequential decision: registry startup/compile
tradeoff under the selected replacement, IPC live working set under the complete envelope,
observed fallback frequency before adding exact split sizing, or process/capture costs before
a deferred replacement. State the decision, baseline, modes and representative operation before
running it. Coordinate scientific timing/reference exclusions rather than partitioning the
workstation. Retain original immutable outputs and failed/incomplete attempts.

It is acceptable to decline a campaign that would only restate curiosity. Record that the
benefit remains unmeasured, with the meaningful future trigger. Measurements that are necessary
to select a remedy remain with that inquiry; optional comparative quantification is not an
automatic completion gate. The old registry timing sample and one-host determinism probes keep
their original attribution and do not become new-platform predictions.
