<a id="f02"></a>
## F02 — Assess observer and export fit with short controls

Use source/work facts first. For an adopted native observer or heavier recording/analysis route, compare off, ordinary and selected-detail operation on a small revealing case. Count actual callback/evaluation/factor/setup/step/replay work and original output qualification before describing time. Observer failure, sampling/limits and deadline effects remain visible. No identical deadline-limited trajectory, zero overhead or global performance winner is promised.

Begin with one complete observation per case. Repeat only when noise could alter a specific adoption decision. Unexpectedly long work is inspected with current diagnostics, cooperatively stopped/drained if appropriate, and reduced to the revealing dimension. A ten-minute run, precise statistical SLA or comparative benchmark campaign is not needed to finish this target.

For I07, exercise selected many-child/retry/context/result/profile growth and skew without assembling all payloads in memory. Inspect whether filters occur before decoding, source dependencies remain sufficient, queries avoid field-expanded count duplication, output streaming hides blocking state, and file/report consumers avoid repeated scans. Distinguish reservation/charged payload/native fill/RSS and source/output allowances; none alone describes complete private peak memory. Use a bounded actual measure only where it can change the selected implementation.

For I06, establish the selected profiler's real version/profile/sample/symbol/unwind/export scope only as needed for its answer. A tool name, converted file or empty loss query does not establish complete measurement. No-adoption decisions have a concrete reopening question and retained limitation. Do not add a mandatory platform, bypass guards or recreate provenance/environment proof.

**Acceptance:** adopted observations preserve scientific meaning and actual owner lifecycle; selected work/retention/copying is credible for its supported shape; unresolved overhead/capacity questions affecting a claim are settled or narrow that claim explicitly. Historical timings are not current measurements. Numerical accuracy remains the existing practical policy, including 0.001 where selected.
