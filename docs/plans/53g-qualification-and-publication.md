# Plan 53g: Qualification and publication

## Responsibility and evidence boundary

This companion owns assembled acceptance and enduring publication of the test architecture target. The [coordinator](53-test-architecture-target.md#package-state-and-execution-route) alone owns package/finding/inquiry progress. **Proposed:** qualify the composed contracts after functional work, keeping architectural fitness, scientific adequacy, implemented mechanisms and quantitative benefit separate. Plan authoring supplies no product test or benchmark evidence.

The remaining target incorporates [PI10](../plan_improvement_reviews/test-architecture-remaining-2026-10-24.md#PI10)
and the revised A04/D/E/F contracts. Completed 52k is the starting condition, including its actual
host-support decisions, loaded-revision semantics, derived references and authentic outcome handles.
This companion neither requalifies an obsolete interface nor transfers 52k delivery into Plan53.
