# Plan 44e: Short-run investigation and optimization rounds

## Purpose and campaign boundary

This is executable investigation and optimization scope, not merely the final validation of known findings. It covers the initial rounds of short testing needed to expose and action additional pipeline inefficiencies. [Plan44](44-pipeline-performance-optimization.md) owns finding/discovery disposition and the combined completion boundary. This document owns case questions/coverage, campaign progress and the analysis/retest loop. The local execution checkpoint below records completed short controls and their limits; remaining breadth, assembled acceptance and benefit claims retain their stated boundaries. Historical review observations remain historical.

Use the [review's compact cases](../design_review/evidence/pipeline-performance-optimization-2026-10-17/case-notes.md) as anchors, preserving their independent assertions and explicit limitations. Do not replay all old Plan39/43 campaigns, inherit long fixtures by name, or mistake source-only inspection for a newly measured route. Broaden the review's unexamined contexts through compact representatives rather than narrowing the scope to the nine findings.

### Observation protocol

Use ordinary correctness settings with explicit Arrow force-validation and the pinned toolchain/extension. Refresh only the necessary fast editable development build when source changes; no full release wheel. Physical horizon and wall duration are different. Do not tighten practical engineering accuracies or silently reinterpret rank thresholds as solve tolerances.

First collect existing invocation/attempt facts, prepared/evaluator work, native setup/evaluation/step/search counters, integration purposes, original checks, queue/wait/resource and result endpoints. Select complete detailed families before work only where sequence/order is decision-relevant; retain actual loss/availability. Use current/recent observations during an active short case to distinguish current phase, convergence, repeated setup, callback work and waiting. Inclusive stages overlap; do not sum them as exclusive total cost or infer wait from wall-minus-thread-CPU.

CPU/stack, allocation or timeline capture is selected for a concrete unexplained owner/phase. Establish its clock/process/operation scope and partiality before attributing cost. Use existing perf/Samply, trace or allocation capabilities where fitting; unavailable/partial tools stay explicit. A clone frame alone does not justify a representation redesign. A phase-aligned source/call-count control may settle the question more cheaply.

Target seconds per case. Around 60 seconds, request cooperative stop through an actual available handle/owner, inspect progress and choose a smaller faithful case or address an obvious cause. This is an observation policy, not a hard timeout, global runtime cap or permission to kill native work. Await real joins/cleanup; synchronous APIs lacking a timer-cancellable handle retain that limitation. Build/service startup is accounted separately and must not be confused with a ten-minute simulation. Larger runs require a named question that no short representative settles and belong to the later review.

## E02 — Breadth pass with immediate cause triage

Begin once the relevant A01/A02 recording slice and E01 specifications are available.
