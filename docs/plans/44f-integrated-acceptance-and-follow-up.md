## F01 — Integrated consumer and scientific acceptance

Confirm all actual producers/consumers of changed contracts are migrated: invocation/native dispatch and result clocks; integration current/raw terminal state; selected direction layouts and value/schedule/anchor mappings; fitting final products/rank; study views and accounting; sparse verification; selected native/provider/profile branches; Rust/Python/CLI/schema/generated projections as affected. Remove obsolete duplicate mechanisms and migrated assumptions. A source owner or capability flag alone is not assembled acceptance.

Select short integrated journeys from [44e's required matrix](44e-short-run-investigation-and-optimization.md#required-short-case-matrix), not full old campaign suites. At minimum cover:

- Edit/re-solve and repeated preparation with analytic physical answers; changed complete keys invalidate correctly.
- Coupled/scaled/scheduled selected transient responses on both supported backends, with ordinary full Forward and empty-selection controls.
- Final fitting success, rank preflight refusal/deficiency, derivative failure and cancellation without new fallback; independent original assessment and partial product permission.
- Shooting's consumed integral/terminal/path products and the actually selected forward/adjoint branch; existing hybrid refusal stays truthful.
- Pressure-sensitive independent/continuation studies with Full/Compact results, one/two lanes, managed/durable receiver coverage, cancellation and real drain.
- Eligible sparse IFT first/second derivatives and singular/nonfinite refusal; selected KLU/provider/profile changes with fresh numeric and stateful counterexamples.
- Dynamics with initialization, schedule/event/endpoint, short stiffness contrast and output-density checks; tiny durable publication/reopen and selected result reads.
- Nested/ephemeral/durable invocation coverage; completed/failed/cancelled terminal producer state; diagnostic loss/writer failure independent of scientific permission.

Use independent analytic/physical assertions, existing native/scientific reference controls and original conservation/domain/feasibility checks. If scientific model formulas are actually changed, select their conformance/parity obligations under PS-13 rather than assuming routing optimizations qualify every model. Source baseline and full IDAES parity are not silently promoted from compact numerical examples.

## F02 — Scope-end quality after functional work

During implementation each package compiles its actual affected closure and runs targeted tests. Once required functional scope is integrated, run the relevant integrated/component/solver/Python journeys once and `just hygiene`; repair failures and rerun the failing leaves. Include the full continuing Clippy default/no-default modes (`just clippy`) and linked-native closure (`just lint-solver-contracts`), Python `just typecheck` and `just lint-py`, and generated equivalence as applicable. Include required manual `just governance`, `just docs`, native contract/data lints and changed-feature powerset coverage through their existing recipes. Confirm actual Arrow force-validation for correctness closures.

Use the pinned toolchain and project tools through `scripts/pse-env --`; select the native environment and fast editable validated development extension as required. No full wheel/Maturin distribution installation is a goal. A warmed build is useful; none of the scientific conclusions requires a cold rebuild or a stable-environment proof. Builds and owned service setup are reported separately from simulation duration.

The target is zero failures/warnings; report command, actual mode/features, scope and result. A historical or composite pass is not a fresh whole-suite pass. A passing body with failed join/fixture cleanup remains unfinished. Preserve unrelated concurrent edits/resources; do not reset a shared registry, delete caches or change host guards to manufacture a pass.

The source review recorded two unrelated authoring-tool blockers: full documentation publication lacks a title/H1 on a supplied external report, and the shared resource registry requires explicit fenced migration recovery. Inspect current conditions when execution reaches them; do not assume they persist or schedule unrelated repairs without authority. Authored-scope checks can still be reported honestly. Required product journeys blocked by an environment condition remain not run/incomplete, rather than qualified by scoped document rendering.

Root runs `just turn-end` for changed-file handoff under AGENTS.md. Documentation-only closeout does not rerun product qualification. Fix generators and regenerate when declarations change; never hand-edit generated output.

## F03 — Final short-run conclusions and follow-up review input

Consume E05's integrated resweep, package outcomes and the coordinator's dispositions. State which work was removed, which numerical/profile choices were selected or retained, the actual cases/conditions and uncertainty. Keep ordinary work-count improvements separate from elapsed improvements; no universal solver ranking or global performance claim follows.

Produce a concise follow-up section **within this series**, linked to useful retained case outputs, not a new design review or another implementation plan. Each remaining question specifies:

- The observed cause/hypothesis and consequence, with scientific/diagnostic limits.
- Why the smallest faithful short case and available source/counters could not settle it.
- The required different experiment: longer physical horizon, larger sparse/stiff problem, adjoint/control crossover, sustained managed throughput/publication, failure recovery or another named mechanism.
- Required diagnostics, original assertions, discriminating result and prerequisite optimization/feature.
- Its decision/work owner and observable trigger for the subsequent review/planning step.

Do not put a cheaply settled short question here merely to close a package. Preserve unresolved confirmed F/N findings as deferred/open with their actual limitation; supported rejection/no-change outcomes for opportunities need their reasoning. Cases unavailable because of a fixable required setup are not automatically long-run questions. This handoff informs future review and planning; it neither executes those experiments nor authorizes their implementation.

No other existing plan file, plan index, status reconciliation or supersession is part of this series. The maintainer will scope those updates after the testing/optimization process.
