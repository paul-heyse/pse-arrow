# Plan 43g: Qualification and design publication

## Purpose and acceptance boundaries

[Plan43](43-solver-diagnostics-and-performance.md) owns the combined target, accepted rule decisions and finding dispositions. This companion owns integration/qualification/publication package progress. **Proposed:** all product checks and future publication work below. Document authoring itself does not execute them or establish scientific qualification.

Keep five claims separate: source/interface reasoning, focused mechanism verification, assembled functional/scientific qualification, diagnostic evidence sufficiency, and measured benefit. A pass at one level does not supply another. G9 revisits actual ownership/model/execution fit; passing numerical tests alone does not settle architecture. PS-G1–PS-G3 retain independent obligations for the numerical scope changed.

No new fixed observer overhead SLA, supported-size promise, repeat count, routine solver tournament or environment/provenance proof is introduced. One complete observation with assertions can establish avoided work; a percentage speedup requires appropriate comparative evidence only if that claim or decision is needed.

## Target packages

### G01 — Integrated contracts, decisions and consumer completeness

At the actual scope-end boundary, identify the stable integrated tree and selected implementations, recording ordinary commit/working changes as context rather than attestation. Confirm all RC targets and bounded decisions were respected; a concrete numerical/platform decision not covered by its original route returns to the operator before adoption. Accepted rule recommendations do not themselves change ADR text or qualify code.

Inspect shared checkpoint/producer/endpoint documents, math product/artifact identities, native reuse/mapping, dynamic effort, analysis/capture and resource boundaries across their actual consumers. Verify no duplicate schema/work/cache/scheduler authority, no legacy shim or second production path remains after a qualified replacement, and generators own every generated projection. Interfaces, stored representations, callbacks, report readers, Rust/Python/CLI and benchmark/export consumers migrate in their package, not a late inventory-only phase.

Local package progress remains at its companion, findings at the coordinator. Source facts already settled by the review are not subjected to a new mandatory proof manifest. Revisit only changed premises or exposed consequential gaps, under the binding's actual review cadence. Dependency/version moves remain ordinary judgment unless their changed contract requires a decision route.

<a id="g03"></a>

### G03 — Diagnostic sufficiency and proportionate benefit observations

Qualify explanation against controlled causes: changed work at unchanged scientific request; increased unit cost; failed attempts; displaced preparation/inner/analysis/publication work; actual waiting versus opaque intervals; mixed generations; and incomplete capture. A classifier yields a limitation/hypothesis when its phase/sequence/history assumptions are absent. Do not ask tests to establish a speculative cause from correlated timing.

Verify a valid scientific result survives optional telemetry failure, a failed scientific run can retain sufficient evidence, and a diagnostic study requesting complete coverage rejects insufficient evidence without changing science. Default bounded and selected complete products remain distinct. History-off current progress and absent optional collection remain interpretable.

For source-founded corrections, observe actual constructed/emitted/unique support, evaluations/projections, factor/setup/replay roles, copy/publication calls or native steps under an unchanged requirement. Such evidence can establish removal of unnecessary work without a timing claim. If observer cost is decision-relevant, compare absent/ordinary/selected detail while retaining mandatory local bookkeeping and original scientific work; name the baseline accurately.

Optional timing/kernel/instruction/PMU observations retain actual profile/features, requested products, start/reuse, family eligibility, execution mode, unknowns and original outcomes. Algorithmic counts and simulated instructions are not universally noiseless. Do not pair all-call work with only Criterion measured samples. Retain failure outcomes; family profiles do not rank incompatible requests.

Candidate stress contexts from the reports—cheap callbacks, costly properties, wide derivatives, difficult convergence, sparse structured systems, larger multicomponent columns, nested recycles, large exact-Hessian optimization and dense dynamic startup—are selected only where they distinguish a remaining decision. They are not mandatory scale promises or a repeat campaign. A long-running case gets meaningful current/recent/profile evidence, not a forced timeout interpreted as numerical failure.

<a id="g04"></a>

### G04 — Scope-end checks and zero-target reporting

Run static and broad integration checks once functional scope is complete, then fix failing scope-owned checks and rerun their relevant recipes. Root owns turn-end formatting/index work. During implementation use touched-package compile and focused functional tests, not repeated hygiene or integration runs at every package boundary.

Select the actual applicable closure: `just hygiene` for type/Ruff/import/repository/codegen checks; continuing full workspace/all-target Clippy for default and no-default through `just clippy`; and the linked-native closure through the existing native environment plus Arrow-validation Clippy `--keep-going`. Confirm modes/features rather than inventing a Cargo `--no-fail-fast` flag for a recipe that already provides continuation. Include applicable native data/contract lints, feature powerset, `just governance`, family checks, Python/native/component/integration journeys and `just docs`/offline links at their existing owners.

The baseline is zero findings/warnings/failures. Report command, actual mode/features, scope, result and not-run/unavailable boundaries. A passing body with failed enclosing cleanup is not an integrated pass. Test cleanup uses exact owned resources/registered routes and preserves other concurrent work. Do not delete unrelated data/cache or recover a shared registry merely to manufacture a passing report.

Documentation-only edits after qualified source do not require another native campaign. Document-only creation of this series uses metadata/scope/link/render checks and does not run product hygiene. If an unrelated input/registry blocks full-book/turn-end checks, preserve it, report the boundary and verify the authored scope directly where sound.

### G05 — Enduring design publication and standalone closeout

Route accepted target contract/rationale changes to their actual enduring owners through required ADR/design edits and a blueprint revision. Accepted ADRs are immutable; supersede rather than rewrite. Do this before changes that require a prior decision, not retroactively at closeout. G05 confirms completeness of those earlier routes and publishes remaining enduring implementation/operating detail.

Cover blueprint§14 selected products/compilation,§16 scaling/accuracy,§17–§18 native reuse/dynamics/precision,§23 diagnostic contracts, Rust/Python boundary docs and operational capture/analysis guidance as actually changed. RC11 corrects named diagnostic catalogue wording while preserving absence of full IDAES Toolbox parity. Do not publish new performance or complete-history claims beyond executed evidence.

Update this new series' checkpoint, Outcome and finding dispositions with Implemented/Tested/Measured distinctions, a mistake corrected and deliberate deviations. Remove replaced code/tests/fixtures immediately after their replacements qualify. Follow normal retention for this series/review when its dependencies permit; do not create a duplicate archive or remove the highest-numbered coordinator contrary to retention rules.

Existing-plan assessment, updates, reconciliation, supersession and status closure remain excluded. This series publishes its own enduring contracts and status; any later assessment of older plans needs its separately authorized scope. External source reports remain intact as consumed evidence until their legitimate lifecycle owner decides otherwise.
