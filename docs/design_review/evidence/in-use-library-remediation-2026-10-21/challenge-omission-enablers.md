## What is sound

- **Coverage.** All 32 findings have exactly one row. The 27/3/2 counts add up. Audit anchors resolve to the right pages, the PSE-S01–S07 anchors exist (`binding/pse-arrow.md:189-195`), and the relative paths from the intended `reviews/` location are correct.
- **R10 lane drain.** Making `drain` a child of `stop` preserves behaviour. Stop already cancels drain (`worker.rs:439`, `:446-448`), and drain cancels triggered by failure do not propagate upward.
- **R2 encoding.** `ErrorDetails::Thrown` carries no payload (`surrealdb-types-3.3.0/src/error.rs:171-178`, `:570`). Decoding a code prefix once, in the lowest owner, is the best route the SDK offers. The three messages at `worker.rs:1697` are project `THROW`s.
- **R4 preservation.** Append replay stays guarded by batch digest and block equality (surql:1025-1028). Exact bits, P3 and the single decoder are kept.
- **Probe consistency.** The draft respects the REFERENCE race result (guard rows kept), pruning effectiveness (key only), DataFusion ±0 (`normalize_float_zero`), the refuted whole-group Dualizer and `merge`, and the 79 LRU re-runs. The 19 s build figure is the total over 19 groups (`probe-worktree.md:46`, 19.2 s); the checkpoint's ≈10 s is `root.outlet` alone (`:40`). The Parquet ±0 row rests on the writer-statistics fact, not on the comparison claim the probe corrected.
- **Remedy-open items with a stated conflict.** DEFINE EVENT, DEFINE SEQUENCE, F03, the Salsa database shared with pinned lanes, and the Uno numerics relocation each state their conflict correctly.
- **P4.** The Uno hunk classification follows P4. R15 matches `uno.rs:117-120` and `ipopt.rs:59-61`.
- **R14** keeps every rung separately admitted and qualified, and rejects the library's private ladder.
- **No new third-party library.** typed-index-collections, rpds, quick_cache, enum-map, strum, nutype and petgraph are all pinned (`Cargo.toml:193-316`). pyo3's `experimental-inspect` is already enabled in pse-py (`crates/pse-py/Cargo.toml:72`). No derive heap-sizer is resolved, so DA-022's open item is correctly a lead.
- **Value rebind, guard survival, library-owned derivatives, typed status, original-space qualification and the cancellation drain** are preserved by R7, R8, R10 and R14 as written. The exception is the R7 demand-partition caveat in the table.
