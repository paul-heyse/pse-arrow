## Part A. Reuse and prediction inventory

### A1. Vocabulary and stamps (observed)
- Two distinct `StartPolicy` types. Native: `NoPriorStart | PreviousAccepted | Explicit`
  (`pse-backend-native/src/solve.rs`, consumed in `pse-runtime/src/math/solves.rs::admit_step` ~1959-2000).
  Study-occurrence: `Fresh | Continuation(SeedEdge) | Explicit{role,seed}` (`pse-model/src/study.rs:81`).
  Plus `StartSource` provenance enum (`pse-runtime/src/workflow/modeling/cases.rs:36`: ModelDefault, Case,
  Binding, Annotation, Predecessor, Continuation, Stored, Fixed) and staged `Start::{Specification, Accepted(k)}`
  (`pse-runtime/src/workflow/staged.rs:43`; `Seed(k)` named in the doc §17.6 is not in this enum in the tree: only two variants exist).
- `ReusePolicy {Fresh, AllowRebuild, RequireReuse}` (`pse-model/src/generated/enums.rs:17855`): allocation reuse
  only; `Fresh` calls `retained.clear()` (solves.rs admit_step ~1971).
- `Compatibility {layout, profile, data, backend}` (`solve.rs:643`). `same_session` compares layout+profile+backend; `data`
  is never compared (numeric data may differ). A seed needs only layout+backend (`WarmStart::validate`; memory_seed in
  `pse-runtime/src/workflow/study_execution.rs:506-526` compares layout and backend only).
- `WarmStart {origin, compatibility, payload}` (`solve.rs:846`), payload per backend (`WarmPayload`): `Root(Vec<f64>)` (KINSOL),
  `Nlp{primal, bounds(z_L,z_U), rows(lambda), barrier(mu), working(WorkingSet)}` (Ipopt, POUNCE), `Highs{primal, dual, basis}`.
  Clarabel/cone: none ("No external iterate warm start is claimed", `conic.rs:634`). SCIP: reoptimization session only.
- `StartReceipt {previous_attempt, seed, sparse_seed, transformations, submitted}` (`solve.rs:1253`), `SeedTransformation`
  = Normalization | Presolve{transformation, passes} | WorkingSet | InteriorRestart (`solve.rs:1209`). Provenance only; recorded from what ran.
- `execution::Retained` (`pse-backend-native/src/execution.rs:613`): ONE slot `session: Option<(Backend, Box<dyn Any>)>`
  plus ONE slot `advance: Option<(kkt::Advance, charge)>`. `session()` reuses when same backend and the adapter's `reuse` closure
  accepts; else rebuild (AllowRebuild) or `ReuseRefusal` (RequireReuse). Worker-owned, never crosses threads.

## Part B. Structure and decomposition

### B1. `pse-structural` and its consumers (observed)
- `incidence.rs`: `CaseIncidence::analyze` -> `StructuralAnalysis {scope, matching witness, over/under/square Part, blocks (BTF, prerequisite-first, BlockId, coupling class), contributions}` using pounce-presolve Hopcroft-Karp, DM, BTF (`PROVENANCE` string line 21). No numerical rank claim.
- `initialization.rs`: `Plan::from_analysis` (conditional blocks + explicit predecessor inputs).
- `flowsheet.rs`: `FlowGraph::admit`, components, witness, `unweighted_heuristic` tear; `pse-backend-native/src/tears.rs`: HiGHS MILP tear selection (`compile`, `solve`) and heuristic; `recycle.rs`: `CausalMap` fixed-point oracle.
- Main-solve-time consumption of `StructuralAnalysis`: as an admission GATE only. `structural::check(contract, pattern, bounds, mode, Some(witness))` is called in the NLP runner and `roots()` (`execution/runner.rs` ~98-107 and ~263-272), and in `square_response::SquareScope::admit`; the witness rides on the oracle (`structural::retain_nlp/retain_roots`) and in `routing::Structure`. It rejects structurally deficient problems; it does not reorder, partition or reduce the solve.
- `initialization::Block`s are consumed by `pse-runtime/src/math/initialization.rs` (`PreparedInitialization`, one KINSOL/NLP step per block with outside variables as fixed boundary values; `CasePlan::conditional` assembly.rs:620) and by `pse-compiler/src/workspace.rs:784` (`Plan::from_analysis(&case.structure)`). That runs inside `initialize_model` stages (authored) or conformance flows, producing `committed` values used as a seed (`conformance.rs:1340`). Neither the block permutation nor tear sets are stored as a solve-time structure: blocks are recompiled into separate executable cases; no `Retained`/`WarmStart` carries them.
- Tears: `TearMethod {UnweightedHeuristic, Highs}` -> `TearResult.selected` (`pse-runtime/src/math/flows.rs:33,120-160`); the result is fed back by the caller into an explicit `RecycleRequest.tears` (`strategies/requests.rs:60-70`). Selection is a user/tool step, not automatic.
- Block-sequential main solve exists only as the explicit sequential-modular recycle route (`CausalMap`, KINSOL `FixedPoint` + Anderson), which requires authored unit realizations and `NoPriorStart`.

### B2. Nested implicit functions (observed)
- Authored: `implicit <name> select <meaning> {...}` blocks in models (`packages/reference/thermodynamics/models/{peng-robinson,nested-equilibrium}.pse`; parser `pse-authoring/src/language/parser.rs:1744`). Compiler builds `ImplicitSelection` {Relation | Unique | Branch | Operational | MinimumScore}, anchors, sign restriction, `SelectionEquivalence` (`pse-compiler/.../executable/implicit.rs`; math owner `pse-math/src/implicit_selection.rs`). §7.2 of mathematics-and-compilation: expression use requires a justified selection; ordinary starts are numerical aids.
- Evaluation (`pse-math/src/implicit.rs` `Nested::evaluate` ~753-820): resolve anchor and `Configuration` (Fixed or Hints) -> `InnerSolver::solve` (injected `backend-native::implicit::Kinsol`, serial KINSOL on the already admitted worker, KLU, tolerances from policy, `verify` against original residual budgets) -> `Problem::derivatives`: one faer sparse LU of F_y (`SymbolicLu` prepared at construction), solves `F_y dy/dp = -F_p`, and second order via two-sided tangent contraction of residual Hessians; backward-error check; refuses singular F_y or unknown bound meeting the neighbourhood. Output: value, Jacobian, Hessian to the outer evaluator, so the outer NLP/root sees y(p) as a reduced-space function with exact IFT derivatives up to the compiled order.
- Variants: `RegimeFactory` minimum-score selection among alternatives (`implicit_regimes.rs`), `Affine` exact elimination (`implicit_affine.rs`, residual affine in unknowns; sparse LU), `CubicRoots` accelerator (`implicit_accelerators.rs`, `Accelerators::standard()` = {"cubic_roots"}).
- Interpretation: this is effectively reduced-space solving with inner solve + implicit derivatives, but (a) only for blocks the AUTHOR declared `implicit`, (b) each outer trial re-solves from the configured start (no previous root reuse), (c) KLU numeric factorization per inner solve and again for IFT (two factorizations per evaluation order>=1), (d) no code derives an `Implicit` from BTF blocks. Turning a BTF block into one would need: a block-to-`implicit` projection (rows/unknowns/inputs map directly from `initialization::Block` {members, inputs}), selection/uniqueness evidence (the compiler needs `ImplicitMeaning` other than Relation; BTF/matching proves neither a unique root nor nonsingular F_y), derivative-order compilation, an anchor/start (the structural block has none), and qualification of bound neighbourhood. `SelectionEquivalence` establishes uniqueness only for scalar affine/square-root forms.

### B3. Presolve (observed)
- Entry: `execution/runner.rs::nlp` constructs `presolve::Pipeline` (line ~163) for Ipopt and POUNCE NLP steps, including Root/Initialize/FeasiblePoint intents via `FeasibilityOracle`. `roots()` (KINSOL) has NO presolve. Cone/coefficient/factorable routes: not through this pipeline (not read in detail).
- Passes (`presolve.rs` `Pass` enum; `Policy::qualify` ~192-300): LinearBounds, RedundantRows, AffineElimination (library `linear_eq_reduction` + recovery), Fbbt (expression tape interval propagation), RankDiagnostics (LICQ diagnostics only), Auxiliary (safe auxiliary nonlinear reduction). `Policy::Auto` turns on only `linear_eq_reduction` and `fbbt` (and `enabled`); others need `Explicit`. Eligibility needs affine-row proofs from `pse-math/src/presolve.rs` `Facts` (AffineRow, tapes), exact equalities, coefficients above 1e-12 relative, tolerances >= 1e-12; `relaxes_rows()` (l1 exact penalty) forces Off.
- Postsolve: `Report` keeps `columns: PresolvedCol->OriginalCol`, `rows`, `transformation` hash, `Dimensions`, `proof` (certified infeasibility, re-verified in original coordinates); `Pipeline::finish` (pipeline.rs:725) recovers the original candidate/duals; `SeedTransformation::Presolve` records it; working set transfers only under an identical transformation hash. Original-space qualification re-evaluates the original model (`quality::qualify`).
- "Exact affine elimination": two places. Presolve `AffineElimination` (above, NLP routes, automatic under Auto when eligible) and nested `implicit::Affine` (authored implicit blocks).

### B4. Derivative availability (observed)
- `DerivativeOrder {Value, First, Second}` per compiled body; `CasePlan::available_order()/order()` (`pse-math/src/assembly.rs:771-775`), patterns `jacobian_pattern()/hessian_pattern()`; `CaseWorker::{objective, constraints, gradient, jacobian, hessian, jacobian_product}` (1214-1340). Second-order is the exact Lagrangian Hessian assembled over a sparse pattern.
- `HessianMode {Exact, LimitedMemory, GaussNewton}` (`pse-model/src/generated/enums.rs:4482`); Ipopt/POUNCE use exact if supplied else limited-memory; GaussNewton for admitted least-squares fits.
- Parametric programs: `CasePlan::parametric(parameters, order, ...)` (assembly.rs:585) appends declared parameters as extra (pinned) columns at the requested order; `PreparedSolve::with_sensitivity(ParametricPreparation::{Available, Unavailable(cause)})` (solves.rs ~375); used by KKT sensitivity (needs Hessian entries of pinned columns: Second), by square_response (First), and by the retained `Advance` (parametric Jacobian triplets).
- No Hessian-vector or directional-derivative evaluator: JVP is assemble-then-multiply; dynamics provide forward/adjoint over functionals only.
