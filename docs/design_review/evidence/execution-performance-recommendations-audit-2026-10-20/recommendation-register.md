# Recommendation-by-recommendation assessment

Supporting register for the [principal audit](../../reviews/design_review_execution-performance-recommendations-audit_2026-10-20.md).
The principal document owns the integrated judgment, findings, priorities and rule impacts.
These are review recommendations, not implemented changes or operator-approved dispositions.

**R1** is `docs/design_review_execution-performance-target_2026-10-20.md`.
**R2** is the principal review linked by
`docs/end-to-end-execution-performance-review-2026-10-20.md`:
`docs/design_review/reviews/design_review_end-to-end-execution-performance-target_2026-10-20.md`.
Its measurement report is supporting evidence, not a third independent review.

**Adopt** means action the stated direction within its supplied preservation constraints.
**Revise** means action only the corrected contract below. **Investigate** means action
the bounded investigation, without selecting its unknown winner/default. **Omit** means
do not action that proposal or diagnosis as written. Compound entries are deliberately
split: accepting one part does not accept all parts of its source finding.
