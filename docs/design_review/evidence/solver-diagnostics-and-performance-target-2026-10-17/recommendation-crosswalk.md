# Complete external recommendation crosswalk

This supporting assessment belongs to the [principal review](../../reviews/design_review_solver-diagnostics-and-performance-target_2026-10-17.md). Baseline: `9cf67375cdf6d0b06c2dc51bc10e1ff72b9a439c`, matching both external reports. Read-only source/library assessment; no new numerical execution or measurement. This file owns recommendation coverage, not adoption status or a competing overall verdict.

**R1** is [external_review_numerical_diagnostics_1.md](../../../external_review_numerical_diagnostics_1.md). **R2** is [pse-arrow-solver-performance-review.md](../../../pse-arrow-solver-performance-review.md). R1 references identify its original sections/subheadings; R2 preserves every numbered ID. R1- and U-identifiers below are local navigation identifiers, not identifiers attributed to the external authors.

Positions mean **retain** the direction, **revise** its diagnosis/contract, **conditional** on the stated decision, **omit** the specified proposal, or **existing** for an adequate implemented part. A retained direction remains Proposed, not authorized implementation. Findings F, opportunities O, inquiries Q and target contracts T refer to the principal review. Mixed positions within a row explicitly distinguish its subrecommendations. All numerical impact/effort estimates in the inputs remain hypotheses.
