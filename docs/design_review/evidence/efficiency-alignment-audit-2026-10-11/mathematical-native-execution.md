### F16 — callback representation and assurance boundaries

Sources: `pse-backend-native/src/assembled.rs::AlgebraicOracle::trial`; `pse-math/src/binding.rs::InstanceBinding::values_selected`; `assembly.rs::CaseWorker::{evaluate,constraints,jacobian,hessian}`; `execution.rs::{CompiledBody::worker_scoped,Worker::evaluate_seeded,evaluate_stages}`; callback/applicability entrypoints cited by original B/C.

The repeated semantic-ID insertion, per-instance duplicate-formal BTreeSet creation, identity-map lookup/conversion and bit-vector allocation, assembly lookups, and max-frame NaN fill are real. The immutable assembly knows enough to prepare coordinate/gather/contribution offsets once, with fixed/rebound values separated. Domain/Applicability stage arms copy the whole scalar frame; Block stages already have narrower gathers, so “every guarded stage” should not imply all stage kinds use the same wide route.

Correction: dense Hessian storage is allocated when `order >= Second`, not on every Value/First callback. Filling the maximum allocated frame on a smaller-order call is a distinct confirmed cost. The finding should describe widened Second-order outputs and max-frame filling separately. Packed Hessians and reusable buffers must honor the provider/backend's actual triangle/order contract; they are not a license to silently change declared derivative coordinates.

Replace “one finiteness check per publication boundary” with: establish finite coordinates before any provider/native consumer relies on them; carry a validated same-trial guarantee across immutable, premise-preserving operations; re-establish finiteness after new arithmetic, conversion, scaling, provider results or independently produced numerical state. A final persisted result is not the first consumer. Affine conversion or scaling can overflow finite input. Callback atomic output publication remains necessary, and the PS-10 original-case independent evaluation remains fresh. Merely checking a producer twice is not independence.

Applicability may use compact internal outcomes and hydrate rich observations for consumed diagnostics, but refusals must retain the offending trial, clause/instance, relevant permissions and causal attribution. A retained “last observation” cannot let an accepted candidate publish stale evidence after a different failed trial. Replacing accounting strings with stable keys is separable from the mathematical layout.

Preserve aliases, admitted conversions, missing-scalar refusal, bit-exact cache semantics (including signed zero), demand/order boundaries, guard-before-algebra, complete cancellation/deadline checks, memory admission and independent verification. This finding is an AP-07/DP-10/H5/H6/H12/H15/H18–H20 concern; it does not establish a current numerical defect. Preparation layout is a useful prerequisite to parts of F19, but unrelated accounting/evidence improvements need not wait for one monolithic rewrite.

### F17 — scalar preparation and evaluator ownership

Sources: `execution.rs::{compile_scope,compile_stages,CompiledBody::worker_scoped}`; `library/admission.rs::bounded_evaluator`; `library.rs::exact_evaluator`; pinned Symbolica evaluator source.

The per-order loop repeats scalar Horner/CPE optimization before order-specific dualization/vectorization. Up to three optimization invocations is established. “Cold preparation triples” is too strong: derivative construction, admission and vectorization do different work, and their relative costs are unmeasured. Likewise, cloning all orders into every worker is confirmed; a fixed linear gain across arbitrary bodies/cases is not.

Prepare the common exact scalar program once, then form legitimate per-order products. Demand groups should carry the highest order actually consumed, while full demand retains the required Second-order capability. Lazy instantiation must remain within the pre-admitted allowance or perform explicit growth admission; it cannot hide delayed allocations outside accounting. Public JIT/SIMD/export capability must not be inferred from an internal optimization.

An upstream immutable program plus attempt-local scratch API is the clean library-owned outcome. The inspected 3.0.1 public ownership shape still clones its instruction-bearing evaluator; a private ProgramView is not an adoption path. Scratch reuse between sequential attempts is optional and has the reset/ownership conditions in RC13. Do not couple basic order-demand improvements to a rule change or a dependency fork.

Preserve complete artifact/admission/provider identities, derivative-axis order, cancellation, per-order allowance and the independent original worker. Priority depends on body size and attempt count, but source supports unnecessary preparation/copying without a benchmark.

### F18 — cubic isolation, shared scratch and repeated implicit validity

Sources: `implicit_cubic.rs::CubicRoots::{admit,solve}`; `implicit_accelerators.rs::Cubic::admit`; `implicit.rs::{Factory::create_scoped,Nested::evaluate,Problem::derivatives}`; pinned Symbolica polynomial implementation.

Exact rational isolation and refinement are performed on each new cubic solve. However, the coefficient evaluator's temporary MutexGuard is dropped at the end of `.evaluate(parameters, &mut coefficients)`. Conversion to Rational, polynomial construction, `isolate_real_roots`, root refinement and original residual verification follow outside that lock. Correct the title and “lanes serialize” consequence: concurrent lanes serialize coefficient evaluation through this particular lock, not the entire certified root solve. This source does not establish absence of all upstream internal synchronization.

There are three independently assessable matters:

1. **Ownership and synchronization.** One factory Arc shares mutable coefficient scratch across multiple attempts. This conflicts with the literal attempt-owned evaluator sentence in §14.3 and the DP-19 ownership shape. The mutex provides explicit exclusive access and prevents a data race; no cross-attempt result corruption was demonstrated. A strict ownership-conformance concern is not evidence of unsafe concurrent mutation. Give each attempt a coefficient worker, or expose an explicit independently owned pure service and assess that owner against DP-19 rather than declaring a blanket exception under DP-20. Attempt-local scratch is the straightforward remedy; do not infer a need to rewrite exact isolation from this mutex alone.
2. **Isolation algorithm fit.** Exact bignum root isolation in a repeatedly called cubic property is a credible inner-loop fit issue. A numerical fast path is still only a proposal. A local hand-written safeguarded Newton/bisection solver should be challenged under DP-13/PS-09, even if short. Prefer library-owned candidate solving plus the application's remaining branch/completeness verification. Symbolica `roots_hot_start` is a candidate source, not a certificate. Near repeated/critical roots, degree reduction and rounding-sensitive sign tests require rigorous treatment. An original residual pass establishes candidate acceptability, not that another branch root was missed. Exact fallback must cover every unproved case. Current exact isolation is of the polynomial made from evaluated f64 coefficients; retain the separate original residual check rather than calling the entire original expression exact.
3. **Memo-hit validity.** Repeating selection/problem verification after a bit-exact identical trial is a real opportunity, but only unchanged premises can be carried. Value validity does not establish First/Second implicit derivative support, nonsingularity or neighborhood validity. Cache the complete input/anchor/options/provider/solver/branch/selection/order premises actually used, and upgrade validity on higher-order demand. Cancellation/deadlines remain live observers, never cached facts. Group provider calls by spec and inputs with the maximum required order only when error ordering, guard evaluation and attribution remain equivalent. Deriving checks from an already computed jet is sound only where that jet discharges the same check, not merely because both involve residuals.

Preserve exactly one original-residual-verified root in the declared interval, refusal on degeneracy/ambiguity, derivative meaning, deterministic branch selection, complete identity and fresh verification for new states. Split these closure obligations in the principal review; their remedies and prerequisites differ. Exact-algorithm priority needs call counts and scale evidence. The current mutex alone does not justify a high contention priority.

### F19 — conditional and block lifecycles

Sources: `pse-runtime/src/workflow/strategies/conditional.rs::UnitWorker::evaluate`; `math/initialization.rs::{MathService::evaluate_conditional_unit,stages}`; native retained-session/KINSOL seams.

Each unit evaluation clones a whole CaseValues, constructs new workers/oracle/value identity, uses `Retained::default()` and `warm: None`, then qualifies and commits. Each initialization block similarly passes through a full semantic attempt lifecycle. These physical boundaries repeat preparation and native allocation work for small subproblems. The categorical statement that initialization is dominated by orchestration is unmeasured; small arithmetic makes it plausible, not established for all initialization.

Retain one compatible unit-local worker/binding/oracle/session allocation under the enclosing CausalMap/attempt owner. Define its identity/reset and provider/scope lifetime explicitly. Existing session-retention patterns show this is not a need to weaken scientific qualification. Binding/value transport can improve independently of solver state reuse.

Correct “session and factor reuse is unconditional.” Symbolic sparsity/layout and allocated session capacity can remain valid while numeric Jacobian entries change with tears. Reusing old numeric factors unconditionally is not mathematically exact reuse. KINSOL must own its refresh/stale-setup policy, with accepted derivative compatibility and original-point qualification. Previous roots as starts can change the selected root or success/failure trajectory; they require an explicit uniqueness/branch policy and recorded start. Even retaining numerical state with a cold start must preserve the declared deterministic map/outcome policy rather than assuming a nonlinear solver's path is irrelevant.

A coarser admitted operation can execute declared BTF stages, retaining stage distinctions without rebuilding all outer machinery. Do not erase per-block qualification, commit/rollback, fallback, cancellation or PS-08 restoration. A 1×1 implicit equation is not an explicit assignment merely because it is scalar; direct evaluation needs an actual explicit evaluator/formulation proof. Preserve KINSOL ownership of nonlinear iteration (PS-09), resources until foreign drain, original-case checks and source attribution.

### F20 — square response, numerical rank and factor scope

Sources: `pse-runtime/src/math/solves.rs::{root_response,fresh_root_predictor}`; `pse-runtime/src/workflow/fitting/oracle.rs::response_rank`; `pse-backend-native/src/square_response.rs::{response,spectrum,solve_regular}`; `square_response/actions.rs::{SparseFactor,SparsePredictor}`.

The requested regular-square response route forms dense Fx/Fp, computes singular values using dense SVD, and solves using dense LU. The SVD asks for no U/V vectors; call it dense spectrum computation rather than implying full singular-vector storage. Dense n×n allocation and cubic factor work are confirmed. Honest memory withholding protects truthfulness but does not settle AP-07 fitness at claimed sparse scale.

Duplication is conditional: `fresh_root_predictor` is requested for an available retained/root-source path, and the dense response is also optional. When both requests exist for the same qualified original point, factor work overlaps. This is not every Root solve. Fitting's per-experiment responses and its observation/sensitivity rank matrix have different statistical consumers; do not replace all fitting rank work with a root-pattern certificate.

One factor can serve response RHS and prediction only when it is over the identical normalized original matrix, coordinate/row/support order, provider derivatives, original point and validity scope. A solver's last presolved/shifted/regularized factor is not automatically that factor. Sharing symbolic analysis is less restrictive than sharing numeric factors. Full requested response output remains n×np; sparse input does not make the answer sparse. Action-only consumers can avoid that output, while consumers requesting complete physical responses still need it or an explicit product-scope change.

**Reject the proposed alternative “structural matching plus a sparse backward error” as sufficient rank evidence.** A = [[1,1],[1,1]] has complete structural matching, and x=[1,1], b=[2,2] has zero backward residual, while A is singular and the response is nonunique. A = diag(1, 1e-12) likewise has complete matching and an exact solve, yet is below a declared 1e-10 singular-value cutoff. Neither structural regularity nor one good RHS establishes numerical full rank/conditioning. This is a PS-07/PS-12 assurance obligation, not a policy that can be waived for speed.

Sparse rank-revealing QR is a plausible alternative, but a pivot/diagonal tolerance on R is not the same certificate as a singular-value cutoff. The pinned suitesparse_sys feature list does not enable SPQR. Its complete factor/rank/error/memory/threading contract and linked availability must be researched before selection. No assurance or speed claim for SPQR is established here. Retain bounded dense certification where useful; change evidence semantics explicitly if a different mathematically sound method is adopted. See RC14.

### C-A01 — IBEX exclusion waiters retain active CPU admission

**Cause and workload.** Proof-heavy concurrent roots/implicit providers or independent original checks enter `root_isolation.rs`'s process-global IBEX mutex while already inside runtime `execution::compute_scoped`. The point arithmetic and selection-proof entrypoints loop on `IBEX.try_lock`, sleeping and observing cancellation/time, but never relinquish their active CPU permit. The same exclusion pattern appears in `point_arithmetic_core`, `SelectionVerifier::{promote,connect,refine_point,enclose_action_bounded,enclose_neighborhood,connect_chain_bounded}` and the later interval operation. Source inspection found no pause/resume bridge in this module; the actual wait bodies were read, not inferred from a zero-hit search.

**Consequence.** Several admitted CPU owners can be sleeping behind one synchronous IBEX call while unrelated eligible work cannot acquire compute admission. Increasing study concurrency can increase reserved idle CPU allowances rather than useful native concurrency. Resident proof/attempt memory legitimately stays charged. This is a capacity-coordination mismatch, not evidence that IBEX serialization can safely be removed, a deadlock, or a measured throughput loss.

**Evidence.** `pse-runtime/src/math/staged.rs::{ComputeOwner,step}` (native work closure installed with compute_scoped) and `math/jobs.rs` (same installation for job thread); `pse-backend-native/src/execution.rs::{ComputeAdmission,compute_scoped,pause_compute,resume_compute}`; `root_isolation.rs` wait bodies. Contrary examples: `petsc.rs::Admission::enter` and HiGHS/Uno lifecycle exclusion explicitly pause before waiting and reacquire against the original Execution. The pause bridge is currently feature-gated to HiGHS/Uno/PETSc.

**Standard.** AP-07, DP-20, H21–H23. Proposed medium priority for concurrent proof workloads; scope/frequency unmeasured.

**Correction.** Give IBEX wait admission the existing coordinated-compute behavior, or route proof exclusion through another owner that returns active compute capacity while queued. Reacquire the same admitted allowance under the original clock/stop before native work; memory/native-lifetime charges remain until drain. All request variants need an adequate original execution/scope bridge, not a fabricated fresh deadline.

**Preserve and prerequisite.** Preserve process-global native safety, deterministic proof behavior, cancellation during wait and native call, poisoned-lock refusal, failure/resource evidence, foreign thread ownership and cleanup. Only release the CPU allowance when the owning operation is quiescent: if a caller's native team is still doing work while one thread enters an IBEX callback, pausing that whole team allowance would be incorrect. Establish this caller condition or introduce request-local proof scheduling; a blanket `pause_compute` insertion is not the complete remedy.

**Falsifier/verification.** A competing compute job should be admissible while an isolated/quiescent proof job queues behind IBEX, while the queued proof must reacquire before entering the library and retain its original cancellation/deadline. A trace showing every such path already releases its enclosing CPU allowance, or that the proposed workload is explicitly outside supported concurrency, would narrow/reject the candidate. No existing test observed this interaction. A coordinator-arranged focused admission test is useful for remedy design, but source suffices for the current behavior; no benchmark is necessary to prove the mismatch.

### C-A02 — surrogate advancement copies the full growing library-state snapshot

**Cause and workload.** Every `RetainedSurrogate` Advance command runs the retained egobox worker, then sends `(worker.state.clone(), proposal)`. The public wrapper retains a second EgorState solely to support the automatic state snapshot/termination reads. Pinned EgorState/SurrogateState Clone includes owned historical sample arrays. Over a long sequential surrogate campaign with growing data, this recopies the growing history at each iteration; in the common constant-batch growth case total sample-copy traffic is quadratic in iteration count. During reply handling the worker state, previous snapshot and new snapshot can overlap.

**Evidence.** `pse-math/src/surrogate.rs::{Worker::advance,RetainedSurrogate::new,RetainedSurrogate::advance,state,is_finished}` and Command::Advance; `pse-runtime/src/math/surrogate.rs::{actor,Actor::phase,Actor::proposal,SurrogateHandle::proposal,PreparedSurrogate::advance}`; `/home/paul/.cargo/registry/src/index.crates.io-1949cf8c6b5b557f/egobox-ego-0.38.1/src/solver/egor_state.rs::{EgorState,SurrogateState}`. In the runtime actor, Command::Phase calls task.advance_within, reads before/after work counters and is_finished, then sends a PhaseReply with proposal/work/callback_terminal; Command::Advance returns the proposal, Command::Proposal reads it, and Command::Work reads counters. The phase consumer screens the statistical proposal against the original problem. None of these commands reads task.state(). The runtime consumes advance/work/proposal/termination information, not the complete historic snapshot. Searching RetainedSurrogate/state consumers across crates and reading these entrypoints found full snapshot reads in math tests, not the inspected production route. This does not prove there are no downstream users of the exported public method. No claim is made that cloning includes a GP n×n factor.

**Consequence/standard.** Avoidable sample/history allocation and copying beside each expensive acquisition/update, magnified by long studies and simultaneous campaigns. AP-07, DP-10/DP-20, H6/H16/H17/H26. Proposed medium priority where histories are large; no measured share.

**Correction.** Keep full actual library state with the retained worker. Automatically return a compact immutable progress/termination summary and proposal/work counters. Offer full state snapshots explicitly when a consumer requests them, with their ownership/retention charge visible. Do not reconstruct a reduced solver state or implement a second acquisition algorithm. A public snapshot API migration needs explicit consumer/API handling; snapshot demand must not simply disappear.

**Preserve.** Same egobox init/next_iter history, RNG/reproducibility, hyperparameter/clustering state, callback work accounting, source/fidelity identity, proposal/correction behavior, caps/deadlines/cancellation and native drain. The established retained library worker is a strength; fix its outgoing representation rather than replace it.

**Falsifier/verification.** If the state becomes cheap shared immutable data in the pinned library, or an ordinary product consumer requires the full snapshot every step, the diagnosis/remedy changes. Here Clone's owned ndarray fields and the consumer are decisive. After correction compare identical seeded proposal/termination sequences and ensure explicit snapshots preserve existing semantics; measure allocated/copied history bytes only if priority is contested. No workload executed.

### C-A03 — shooting value trials eagerly integrate forward sensitivities

**Cause and workload.** `pse-runtime/src/workflow/shooting.rs::ShootingOracle::evaluate` has a bit-exact point cache, but no derivative-order demand. On a new trial it sets Forward sensitivity whenever n>0 and rows or terminal outputs exist, integrates every window with that mode, constructs the full Jacobian and terminal gradient, and only then caches the point. `NlpOracle::{objective,constraints,gradient,jacobian}` all call it. Thus an objective/constraint-only rejected line-search trial still pays forward variational integration and derivative assembly. This is particularly material with many controls/anchors and state equations. It is a distinct nested-integration issue beyond F16's algebraic layout.

**Evidence.** `shooting.rs::{ShootingOracle::evaluate,Point}` and four NlpOracle consumers; `ShootingProblem::integration` and the dynamic integrate-window route. The cache efficiently shares all results at a repeated point; the missed distinction is a point that never receives a derivative request. Integral-objective adjoint evaluation is separately demand-driven later; its separate pass is not automatically redundant because this design does not retain compatible native adjoint checkpoints from the first pass.

**Consequence/standard.** Derivative work can be performed for rejected value trials with no consumer. AP-07, DP-10, H6/H12/H16, with PS-07/PS-11 preservation constraints. Proposed medium priority for dynamics optimization; actual native callback sequences and frequency are unmeasured.

**Correction.** Pass value/first-order demand into point evaluation and cache achieved order. Integrate primal-only for value requests; upgrade to first order only when requested, preserving bit-exact input and source/scope compatibility. Compare this with eager amortization when a backend routinely asks for derivatives at every point: the option can involve rerunning an accepted point, so it is not unconditionally faster. Reuse retained primal/adjoint state only through actual supported library lifetimes; do not pretend reports are interchangeable checkpoint sessions.

**Preserve.** Window anchors/continuity constraints, terminal/path outputs, scheduled changes, physical normalization, all requested derivative axes and declared exact derivative provider, finite/native-consumer checks, original scope/deadlines, fresh original-case qualification and truthful failure evidence. No silent finite-difference fallback or weaker original check.

**Falsifier/verification.** Trace value and derivative request sequences for an existing shooting NLP and count sensitivity-enabled integrations for rejected trials. If every evaluated point needs those derivatives and eager integration is cheaper than upgrades, narrow the finding to unsupported demand rather than force a change. After correction compare objective/constraints and gradients/Jacobians at the same points and exercise rejected trials, cancellation and discontinuities. Source proves current eagerness; no runtime speed claim is needed.

## Strengths, contrary evidence and opportunities not promoted to findings

- **Native library ownership is real.** KINSOL owns nonlinear iteration; native LP/MIP/conic adapters own solver execution; exact math, sparse factors and continuation primitives are library-owned. This supports DP-13/PS-09 in the inspected scope. Replacing wrappers with bespoke iteration/root selection would reverse that strength.
- **HiGHS is not simply a single global solve mutex.** `highs_lifecycle.rs` uses concurrent direct read admission, exclusive Uno/global scheduler-reset admission, retained-owner tracking and coordinated compute wait. Direct independent HiGHS solves can coexist. Its library/global-scheduler constraint and idle-state drain are concrete, rather than a blanket reason to accept every wait in every adapter.
- **Clarabel uses its retained update seam.** `execution/clarabel.rs` checks update eligibility and retains/rebuilds the actual library solver with compatible preprocessing settings. QDLDL's serial profile and scoped alternative linear backend settings are explicit. This contradicts an indiscriminate “all coefficient sessions are rebuilt” conclusion.
- **SCIP reoptimization and PETSc lifetimes are deliberate.** `scip.rs::reoptimize` frees the prior reoptimization solve, updates admitted objective and refreshes event/deadline ownership; it is not arbitrary nonlinear formulation reuse. PETSc's process lifecycle is guarded, it pauses compute while excluded, and its serial/MPIUNI capabilities are explicit. Detailed SCIP translation/status and full PETSc C++ behavior were not requalified here.
- **Continuation already separates factor scopes.** `kkt/path/qp.rs::predict` checks unchanged H/A and bounds/topology for the admitted parametric active-set solver. `kkt/path/arclength.rs::TangentWorkspace` retains pattern/symbolic/workspace state and refactors changed numeric matrices; orientation/backward checks remain. Dense spectrum work is used for explicit event probes rather than every tangent. A new generic “reuse factors everywhere” finding is not supported. Original KKT inertia/conditioning checks are not redundant merely because the native solver also factored another matrix.
- **IDAS scheduled replay is justified under this session design.** `dynamics/idas.rs::Session::{backward,replay}` replay earlier segments because IDAAdjReInit deletes stored checkpoints. Removing replay without retaining distinct native checkpoint/session ownership would lose the backward interpolation basis. The Diffsol checkpoint wrapper also handles a concrete library visibility/reset gap; it delegates actual integration rather than implementing its own BDF solver.
- **Smaller IDAS opportunity, not a material finding yet.** Each reverse segment recreates `(0..samples.len()).rev().filter(...)`, scanning later segments' samples before reaching its own. With K changes and S samples this can approach K×S comparisons, apart from necessary integrator replay. One reverse cursor or prepared segment ranges can preserve the “change-time sample belongs to later segment” rule and jump ordering. Integration likely dominates many cases; without a many-change workload this is a lower-priority local opportunity, not grounds for subsystem Revise. Do not use it to delete forward state sensitivities needed by second-order adjoint jumps or shooting endpoints.
- **Proof transport preparation may be repeated, but lifetime is unresolved.** `root_isolation.rs::encode` reconstructs transport nodes/edges/guards and C++ `root_isolation.cpp::Graph` reconstructs an IBEX graph/system per request. Frozen transport could be retained for a truly stable source/order; request-specific programs, bounds and provider premises must be distinguished first. IBEX Function/System mutable state and global safety preclude casually sharing a prepared C++ object across attempts. Source coverage was insufficient to recommend a complete cache or claim the graph preparation dominates.
- **Derived mathematics carries original meaning.** `pse-math/src/derived.rs::OriginalContract::{new,from_case}` validates normalization, coordinate/constraint identity, conservative incidence/support and obligations; factorable construction distinguishes exact from relaxed fidelity and retains correction obligations. This directly supports PS-10/PS-11, although the entire rewriting surface was not audited. Original correction is not waste simply because a relaxed/factorable solver returned success.
- **Collocation exact algebra has a different lifetime.** `collocation.rs::{element,jacobi_roots}` produces bounded-order rational stencils during preparation. Exact polynomial root work there is not analogous to the cubic per-point hot loop. No finding is supported merely because both use exact roots. Compiler wiring into every continuous/collocation path was not exhaustively traced.
