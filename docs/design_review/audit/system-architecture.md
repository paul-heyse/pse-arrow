# Integrated system account

**Interface-checked, 2026-11-01, source `19e7b49a67df24c1bf73005a6186e145e930d53a`.**
Read the [baseline, method and coverage](README.md) before using this account for a review.

The implemented system has coherent scientific and operational foundations. Authored relations
carry model meaning; quantity and resolved-syntax admission constrain interpretation; native
routing checks class and artifact eligibility; original quality is distinct from solver
termination; durable mutation is fenced; retained data and scientific permission are separate.
The native host now gives engineering investigations structured requests, provenance and unknown
states. These are source observations, not assembled-system qualification.

The remaining diagnoses concern how those owners compose, where meanings are repeated, and how
execution boundaries affect unrelated work. The preceding account's general native/Python
dual-authority story is obsolete. The current account does not inherit it merely because the
replacement still has other problems.

[DA-003](semantic-model-and-authority.md#da-003) and
[DA-006](semantic-model-and-authority.md#da-006) concern lookup and formulation interpretation

assembled by consumers. These are change-locality problems even where current behavior agrees.
They must not be conflated with a lack of scientific validation.

without establishing the promised producer permission.

quality permits deviations within the frozen variable tolerance. In the latter case, source
reasoning supplies a counterexample for an otherwise valid free continuous variable; no solver
failure was executed.

[DA-063](execution-fit.md#da-063) is a new liveness-isolation diagnosis: pending global study
capture also gates unrelated renewal and cancellation. Complete selector capture and
authenticated recovery must be preserved; they do not establish that liveness operations need
the same exclusion interval.

[DA-055](execution-fit.md#da-055) retains the narrower observation that durable points repeat
the generic lifecycle against shared per-problem protection.
[DA-043](development-tooling.md#da-043) identifies whole-generation/provider authentication on
small commands. Both have genuine safety obligations and unmeasured execution consequences.
The audit does not infer a bottleneck merely from their structure.

Uniform relation generation remains an investigation under
[DA-059](boundaries-and-composition.md#da-059), bounded by actual consumer uncertainty.
The broader retention-family, file-boundary, paging and invariant-enforcement diagnoses
(DA-056, DA-034, DA-035, DA-060) are downgraded because the inspected facts do not establish
duplicated obligations or an unfit workload.

## Implications for a subsequent review

these diagnoses and compare corrections. It should preserve scientific admissibility,
original-result truth, authenticated ownership and exact settlement rather than treating fewer
mechanisms as an end in itself. Diagnostic labels and current source arguments are not approval
to change contracts, reopen decision-closed reuse work or implement remedies.

package. Its independent challenge and publication checks are recorded in the collection README.
