## Existing capabilities that constrain remedies

`ModelingPackage::with_declarations` computes parented edits by declaration identity and
explicitly stops treating the old document text as authority for the edited IR.
`prepare_flow` selects semantic topology independently of a numerical case's incidence.
`DeclaredExecution::inspection` provides a specialized semantic projection;
`prepare_declared` admits the Solve procedure, while `initialize_declared` retains the full
initialization report. `LocalStudy` and explicit recording have distinct effect contracts.
Scientific research publication already previews admitted deltas and rechecks protected
evidence before publication. These are implemented source paths, not executed journey evidence.

The recycle and exchanger campaigns contain real authored witnesses and independent checks.
Their fixture/oracle eligibility does not establish ordinary scientific-input eligibility.
The exchanger's unknown-area and pressure-extrapolation permissions remain material to its
meaning. A first-delivery interface must consume the actual initialization/report owner and
original physical checks rather than reconstructing a second equipment graph or success rule.

## Expanded target versus existing defects

Missing P2–P7 scientific breadth, the selected ordinary source-to-case journey and the proposed
process-facing authoring surface are target-design work. Their non-delivery is not itself a
violation of the currently supported scope. Empirical materials, population representations,
multiphase storage and physical cycles challenge target adequacy without establishing that an
existing narrowly admitted formulation is incorrect.

The solution review must distinguish implemented capability, documented intended contract,
unsettled design and required future qualification. In particular, structural checks do not
prove physical validity, and source inspection does not establish conservation, derivative
accuracy, reference equivalence, performance or completed PS-G1–PS-G3 qualification.
