# Preparation, assurance and reuse

**Architectural fitness: Revise. Behavioral and scientific adequacy: unresolved for the assembled preparation boundary. Overall decision: Revise.**

The preparation subsystem has the right principal scientific separations: authored process meaning, checked source selection, admitted immutable mathematics, demanded evaluator products, case values, attempt-owned native state and truthful outcomes. These distinctions govern the inspected operations. The problem is their composed physical realization. A warm study point still enters a fresh compiler generation; individual admitted bodies repeatedly trigger observation of the entire executable-backed runtime; a memory hit still encodes and offers its full portable description for durable publication. Some of this work runs under a process-wide loader lock and cannot observe cancellation while it runs or waits. Solver-view reuse therefore does not establish that the complete preparation operation is reused.

The live incident is consequential evidence of the assurance path, not the sole basis of this judgment. During a bounded 15-second sample of an existing Python flash sweep, SHA-256 compression accounted for 97.39% of weighted sampled user-space cycles. A separate process sample recorded repeated hot-cache file reads and native math threads waiting/working. The observed executable was frozen while source continued changing. Current source establishes the repeated-work mechanisms independently; its exact pass counts cannot be assigned to that older binary. Neither observation demonstrates a remedy's speedup or establishes every preparation cost.

The preferred direction is to make validity reusable at the lifetime of its actual premises, keep pure prepared products independent of protection/publication effects, and bind new cases through those products. Full receiving-context and persisted-payload checks remain at genuine trust transitions. Every new numerical state retains its own domains, residuals, bounds, closure and outcome assessment. An operation lifetime is useful only if an owner actually protects the required runtime and artifact premises; a remembered boolean, timestamp or loader counter is insufficient.

## Responsibilities and semantic model

The composition root is runtime workflow preparation. Dependencies should flow from authored meaning and case intent into selected immutable products, then attempt execution; canonical persistence and receiving-context assurance supply explicit boundary facts/effects. They must not redefine the mathematical product or make every expression an independent operation lifetime.

**Model adequacy:** source, semantic body, instance attribution, selected dependencies, physical context, derivative product, case values, candidate permission, scientific outcome and stored result are adequate consequential distinctions in the inspected scientific preparation model. Complete body keys and opaque admitted witnesses govern behavior rather than merely naming output records. The validity-lifetime model is weaker: an observed runtime namespace is repeatedly re-established at a body boundary, and a compiler retention callback also carries durable publication. The operation does not expose a reusable protected validity owner or a clean separation between a pure prepared value and its new selection's effects. Those missing operation/lifetime distinctions obstruct supported composition (PA01–PA03); adding more domain-named result rows would not fix them.

The current cold route protects a selected revision, rechecks or hydrates its dependency closure, obtains a checked immutable revision, creates a fresh `CompilerWorkspace`, publishes that revision into Salsa, specializes/projects/groups its outputs, and requests each semantic body. A retained body can save arithmetic admission, but the canonical attachment validates replay compatibility and publishes durable reachability before returning it. A persistent candidate adds protected discovery, descriptor/payload qualification, scratch reservation and strict reconstruction. Then `bound_case` forms a complete view key; an existing solver view rebinds values, otherwise the view/evaluator artifacts are prepared. Attempt-owned numerical execution and original assessment follow.

This separation matters. A cold miss avoids local runtime observation on the missing-candidate probe. A service view hit shares plans/artifacts and can rebuild value-dependent coefficient/presolve facts without changing immutable topology. Initialization deliberately prepares a base and retains a staged native session through value overlays. Those strengths defeat the broad claim that every numerical iteration recompiles everything. They do not remove work before the view cache.

Preparation, mathematical reuse, native execution and persistence are separate costs and validity scopes. Required full-input scientific analysis is legitimate; multiplying host-wide observation or portable encoding by body and case boundaries is not required by the mathematical meaning.

## Findings and their relationships

PA01's runtime assurance granularity, PA02's repeated preparation composition, PA03's effect/serialization granularity and PA04's interruption gap are independent closure obligations. PA02 multiplies exposure to PA01/PA03. Fixing hashing alone leaves repeated semantic projection and publication work; retaining a compiler alone can suppress effects tied to query execution. A safe correction must reconcile them. None requires replacing the solver, relaxing physical checks or adopting a different database.

### <a id="pa01"></a>PA01 — Complete runtime observation is repeated at individual body-use boundaries

**Principles/gates/scenarios:** AP-04, AP-07, DP-09/10/18/23, G9; S01/S02/S04/S07. **High consequence** for ordinary studies and interactive preparation.

**Interface-checked evidence.** `FileObservation::capture` reads the complete regular file in 65,536-byte chunks and hashes it; `verify` captures it again. `LocalRuntimeObservation::capture` observes the artifact, executable and executable-backed ELF mapping set, with exact-path deduplication and end-of-capture file verification. `LocalReplay::with_current` runs full `observation.verify` before and after the operation under `with_local_runtime_scope`. `CanonicalBodyRetention::{get,retain}` obtains a `validated_namespace`; persistent reuse also rechecks a namespace and reconstructs under `with_current`. The cohort shares an overlapping full observation among at most 32 members and removes its slot at seal; sequential uses establish a new cohort. It is not a study/operation validity lifetime.

For the inspected source, a loaded path normally receives a capture plus end verification within each full observation, and the guarded use observes twice. This is a structural pass description, not a measured count in the frozen installed binary. Exact-path deduplication is useful but cannot remove the repeated lifetime. SHA itself is not the architectural defect; substituting a faster digest retains host-byte work at each body boundary.

**Consequence.** An unchanged small mathematical result can trigger full scans of large unrelated executable closure, repeat across bodies/points, and serialize loader publication/removal while doing it. Loaded PyArrow/NumPy files participate even though they do not express that body's mathematical dependency. Their inclusion is intentional under exact deployed compatibility, but the target needs a better owned lifetime for that guarantee. The [bounded measurements](../evidence/preparation-assurance-and-reuse-2026-10-08.md) confirm repeated hot-cache reading and dominant sampled SHA CPU in one real preparation phase; they do not establish all runtime costs or a future improvement.

**Correction, Proposed.** The receiving/runtime integration owner should establish one full observation for an actually protected operation or immutable runtime generation, pass an opaque validity owner into its preparation consumers, and reuse it while its premises hold. Persisted inputs still require exact bytes/dependencies/interpretation at receiving qualification. A source-qualified producer may retain its separate stronger guarantee. Unknown contexts use fresh preparation. Mutable solver/provider state and new numerical checks remain outside this reuse authority.

**Remedy maturity and counterexample.** Diagnosis is settled; the protected lifetime is a design decision. Manager-owned native installation generations alone do not protect Python's whole loaded closure or concurrent configuration changes. A late plugin load, symbol-scope mutation, unowned file replacement or configuration update must invalidate/refuse the authority or be genuinely excluded for its lifetime. A process-wide cached success or loader counters alone would mishandle this legitimate case. No recommendation removes checks solely because the local hash sample is large.

**Verification.** Distinguish cold receiving qualification, unchanged sequential body/value uses, concurrent waiters and changed relevant context. Show that stable validity is reused, changed premises force admission, and corruption/role mismatch still refuse replay. Exercise an unowned/dynamic context's fresh route. Use existing observation controls where possible; later paired complete-operation measurement can establish benefit without an invented SLA.

### <a id="pa02"></a>PA02 — Warm workflow composition rebuilds a compiler generation before reaching prepared-view reuse

**Principles/gates/scenarios:** AP-01/03/04/07, DP-08/09/10, PS-11, G9; S01/S02/S03/S06. **High consequence** for case/experiment growth.

**Interface-checked evidence.** `ModelingPackage::prepare` obtains `checked_selection`, calls its `canonical_workspace`, then `MathService::prepare_modeling_revision`. `MathService::canonical_workspace` constructs a new `CompilerWorkspace` and frozen canonical retention attachment. `CompilerWorkspace::new` creates new Salsa storage and inventory. `publish_modeling_revision` shares the checked revision without rechecking all declarations, but specializes/projects/groups in that fresh generation. Only downstream `bound_case` probes the solver-view key and rebinds cached values. `prepare_bound_operation` calls `declared_execution`, which itself calls `prepare`, then `prepare_declared`/analysis preparation. Both the ephemeral study frontier and durable `prepare_study_candidate` consume that path.

Dynamics similarly prepares to inspect authored modes, then mode-specific preparation calls `prepare` again. Fitting resolves each experiment and calls `prepare`; transient fitting additionally uses mode preparation. Those distinct scopes can need new products; the repeated shared basis is the concern. The stored-study creation basis cache in `DeclaredStudyAdmission` is an improvement, but ready execution still has this composition. Preparation counters for views/rebinds do not count every pre-view source, specialization, body-assurance or publication operation.

**Consequence.** Correct body and solver caches coexist with repeated selected-request, fresh-database, projection/normalization and effect work. Salsa's maintained local revision/query reuse is discarded at each operation boundary. Attempt ownership is valid for mutable evaluators and native work, but does not require a new pure compiler lifetime for every value point. Source-count growth and case-count growth compound before the cached solver view can help.

**Correction, Proposed.** Reuse a complete immutable selected preparation/basis at its complete source, binding/demand, physical/provider, formulation/profile and admission dependencies; bind current occurrence attribution and numerical values separately. Alternatively preserve a bounded single-writer local compiler accelerator across compatible selections, with explicit revision updates and eviction. Move protection/root effects out of skipped pure queries before adopting the latter. Do not introduce another generic cache, retain whole ancestor source graphs, or share mutable attempt state.

**Remedy maturity and counterexample.** Existing selected/body/view owners make immutable prepared-product reuse a mature direction; exact complete-product identity and lifetime integration need design. A value used as a structural literal, fixed/free change, absent-to-present policy, mode branch or stronger output/derivative demand cannot be treated as a value-only hit. Different experiment instance attribution must remain current even when mathematics is shared. Retaining one globally mutable workspace would serialize independent consumers and recreate an ownership problem.

**Verification.** Compare a warm composition with a clean fresh preparation on unchanged, ordinary value, consumed structural value, demand/order, provider/context and absence changes. Preserve A/B/A coexistence, current spans/IDs and complete original checks. The existing thousand-point test's `views <= 1` is useful view evidence but insufficient alone; a bounded composed control must reveal work before that view cache. No mandatory instrumentation framework follows.

### <a id="pa03"></a>PA03 — Immutable retention also performs full durable encoding/publication on a hit

**Principles/gates/scenarios:** AP-01/02/05/06/07, DP-08/10/18/19, G4/G9; S01/S02/S04/S05. **Material consequence** for warm preparation, local reasoning and future incrementality.

**Interface-checked evidence.** The compiler's `ModelingBodyRetention` contract describes immutable lookup and ownership retention. A grouped tracked `semantic_body` calls it. Its canonical implementation performs synchronous `handle.block_on` store work under a selection mutex. A memory hit invokes `publish_with_namespace` before returning. `publish_with_producer_key` reserves scratch, encodes the whole recipe, packs an envelope and hashes it. `publish_product_inner` serializes all selected dependencies, hashes the payload/dependencies and constructs a blob before checking acknowledged admission. The canonical store can reuse an already-rooted exact description atomically; it does not eliminate these preceding encodes/hashes/crossings.

Blueprint §14.3 describes mathematically pure tracked preparation that writes no data and lists retention lookup/accounting effects. The current canonical attachment adds durable publication. This specification/implementation difference is an effect-contract defect, not evidence that specifications and source are duplicate authorities.

**Consequence.** A warm immutable body lookup acquires publication/recovery obligations and can fail on storage availability, even when its mathematics is already usable. Each body can repeat portable byte/dependency processing during an unchanged study. More importantly, the needed effect is coupled to whether a tracked query executes: reusing a memo under another selection must not silently omit that selection's required reachability. Merely retaining the current compiler to remedy PA02 would hide this obligation. Current fresh workspaces avoid that particular skipped-effect case at the price of repeated work; no present missing-root result was demonstrated.

**Correction, Proposed.** Expose pure immutable retrieval/construction separately from protected dependency/root admission. Reuse an owned encoded description or store-issued descriptor and admit exact required reachability at the selection/operation boundary; group independent effects where the canonical contract allows. Preserve a short coherent publication before protection ends, full dependency qualification and exact uncertainty recovery. Local preparation should name the effects it needs rather than discover a store, executor and loader protocol behind `get`/`retain`.

**Remedy maturity and counterexample.** The separation is mature; descriptor reuse and grouped root admission remain proposed. An unchanged body under a new revision can have new selected absence/membership/source premises, and an old product root can have been released. Reusing the old publication acknowledgment without current dependency/root admission would be wrong. Store reachability must remain a real operation; caching encoding must not cache permission or ignore retention expiry.

**Verification.** Show pure body reuse can be exercised without publication infrastructure; show every newly protected selection establishes its exact required roots even when no mathematical query reruns. Changed dependencies, released roots, expired protection, concurrent identical publication and uncertain acknowledgment must retain current behavior. Verify that warm unchanged publication does not regenerate/transfer unchanged full recipe bytes solely to establish the same effect. Existing store controls remain the oracle for visibility, not a production flag.

### <a id="pa04"></a>PA04 — Runtime observation and cohort waits do not participate in interruption control

**Principles/gates/scenarios:** AP-05/07, DP-19/20, PS-11, G9; S02/S05. **Material consequence** for interactive cancellation and shared resource contention.

**Interface-checked evidence.** `FileObservation::capture`, `LocalRuntimeObservation::{capture,verify}`, `LocalReplay::{with_current,validated_namespace}` and `NamespaceCohort::wait` have no cancellation/deadline input. The file-read loop and plain condition-variable wait cannot notice a caller's stop. `prepare_modeling_revision` does cancel its job and await completion; the math job supervisor retains population/CPU/accounting through native join and TLS destruction. That drain is correct, but interruption cannot take effect inside these assurance operations. A follower can occupy an admitted math job while waiting for the leader's whole observation; “32 members” is not a bound on wait duration or byte work. Full-cohort arrivals wait and then retry observation.

**Consequence.** Cancelling preparation may continue all remaining host scans or wait for another observer before reaching a checkpoint and releasing its owned resources. Multiple jobs can spend CPU admissions waiting on one observer or loader serialization. This is disproportionate for a value edit/stop operation and compounds PA01. The review does not assert a demonstrated deadlock, fixed maximum stop time, lost charge or current scientific-outcome misclassification.

**Correction, Proposed.** Reduce assurance to its genuine lifetime first, then make remaining full capture and follower waits interruptible under the actual operation's cancellation/original clock. Admit shared observation as its own bounded work before followers acquire compute resources where appropriate. A stopped follower must leave without cancelling a leader still needed by others; the leader's work remains charged until it drains. Do not release resources when the caller merely stops waiting.

**Remedy maturity and counterexample.** Stop propagation and explicit wait ownership are mature mechanisms already present elsewhere; coordination placement needs design. If a shared observer still has live consumers, one cancelling waiter must not destroy it. If observation crosses a non-interruptible foreign operation, report that boundary honestly and join it. A new timeout must not be relabeled mathematical incompatibility or silently select another solver.

**Verification.** Stop during a large capture, a follower wait, full-cohort retry and reconstruction; verify typed stop, correct charges through drain and surviving consumers. Panic/poisoned leader settlement and cache clear/retiring behavior remain. No solver-budget tuning or weakened workload substitutes for the interruption contract.

## Runtime trust boundary and unresolved premises

**U01 — The supported receiving-runtime protection needs a consumer-level argument before broadening its lifetime.** The inspected glibc 2.39 callback holds a recursive loaded-object write lock. It protects link-map publication/removal; it is not a freeze of all loader/provider/symbol state. Reopening an existing object can change global lookup scope without another publication; unload destructors precede the removal lock. `gnu_get_libc_version() == "2.39"` does not identify distribution patches or interposition. [Iteration source](https://github.com/bminor/glibc/blob/glibc-2.39/elf/dl-iteratephdr.c), [open source](https://github.com/bminor/glibc/blob/glibc-2.39/elf/dl-open.c), and the [supporting platform evidence](../evidence/preparation-assurance-and-reuse-2026-10-08.md) bound this statement.

The current unsafe root contract excludes JIT/direct executable mapping, concurrent runtime configuration mutation and provider/plugin callbacks during immutable reconstruction; later provider handles/native use have separate admission. Therefore the primitive's limitations alone do not demonstrate a wrong scientific result. Whether exact reconstruction consumes uncovered mutable state, and which owner can protect/exclude it through a larger preparation lifetime, remains unresolved. The proposal must inventory only consequential consumed premises and establish their actual owner, not claim that ELF hashes or unchanged counters attest every runtime fact. An opaque/dynamic embedding may legitimately take fresh preparation. Do not turn a narrow upstream loader premise into arbitrary-host safety.

**U02 — Assembled scientific equivalence of the corrective composition is not established.** Complete identities, strict payload receipts, original physical checks and typed stops are source-backed constraints. No new end-to-end dynamic/fitting/recycle/reference qualification was run. The historical reference comparison's separately stated approximately ±10% assessment is not a solver stop criterion or authorization to relax original numerical tolerances.
