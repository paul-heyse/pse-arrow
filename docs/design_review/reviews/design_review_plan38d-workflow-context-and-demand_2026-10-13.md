# Plan 38d workflow context and demanded products — target design review

**Decision: Accept at Proposed / Interface-checked evidence level.** The bounded target is behaviorally adequate and architecturally fit. It gives scientific context, occurrence identity and requested products explicit owners while preserving original-model acceptance, native ownership and recording uncertainty. No blocking finding or SHOULD deviation was identified in the inspected target. This decision accepts a design; it does not qualify its implementation or the assembled simulator.

The functional target is the process-simulator profile's physically meaningful, diagnosable edit/re-solve, study, recycle, dynamics and estimation workflows, including extension without copied scientific policy. The scoped design must handle finite but potentially large variant spaces, selected occurrence sets, uneven block/experiment costs, long sampled trajectories and concurrent admitted work. Sparse/coupled structure, stiff integration and branch-sensitive inner solves remain scientifically consequential; adding lanes cannot erase those dependencies. Resource premises are the existing shared admission and finite work/deadline envelope, not an invented throughput or capacity promise.

The review examined the complete proposed ADR and Plan38d, the changed architecture sections and adjacent contracts, plus selected source seams listed in slot 10. It assessed architectural fitness and behavioral adequacy independently. Current ADRs, chosen mechanisms, working rules and dependency pins supplied context rather than acceptance criteria. No build, test, probe, benchmark, reference campaign or storage mutation was run. No numerical superiority, universal event qualification or durable acceptance is inferred from source inspection.

Excluded are whole-product qualification, new physical models/providers, general event-model expansion, new solver families, portable native-history serialization, stateful inner-map continuation, arbitrary study generator callbacks, and the broader Plan38e storage topology/backup/GC work. The minimum occurrence and prefix acknowledgement interfaces necessary for the selected workflows remain in scope.

## 2. Decomposition, ownership and dependencies

The composition root remains the workflow executor. It connects source construction, admitted scientific operations, native execution and result recording through their existing owners. The proposal does not create another planner, scheduler or persistence authority.

|---|---|---|---|
| Authored source and compiler | Construct selected revisions; define physical/provider identity, structure and stable block IDs | Immutable source/prepared artifacts; selected construction uses admitted revision operations | A new finite structural alternative changes its constructor/definition and admission, without copying frontier or solver policy. |
| Study occurrence policy | Explicit ordering, usable-result dependencies, seed roles, exact start permission and refusal | Stable occurrences and effect/claim facts; frontier readiness is derived | Policy can be exercised from occurrence and scientific facts without native or store startup. |
| Scientific workflow and shared policy | Original class, consumed derivative/accuracy, branch/start permission and actual effective profile | Per-operation assessments and scoped overlays | Block overrides vary native realization/control within the original scientific contract. Scheduling does not interpret permission. |
| Recycle composition | Actual causal map, frozen inner starts/branch/accuracy, raw-map observations and original assessment | Neutral unit allocations; bounded map evidence | Native iteration can change while map meaning remains with the scientific owner. |
| Native integrator / experiment worker | Backend-specific live history, reset/rebuild, derivative availability and owner affinity | Native mutable state on its owning thread; destruction/drain within admission | Replacement exposes capability differences rather than pretending every backend has the same reinitialization API. |
| Demand and result producer | Requested scientific products, mandatory completion/coverage and original locators | Owned bounded sample batches and explicitly demanded terminal retention | Scientific completion remains independent of subscriber ownership and persistence ACK. |
| Recording owner | Append/close, occurrence incarnation, exact acknowledgement and uncertainty | Existing cursor/storage lifecycle; no new topology | Representation changes remain local to recording/projection and their consumed contracts. |

The source supports these extension seams: `pse-model::study` already separates occurrence identity, ordering, usable-result permission and seed availability; `PreparedBlocks` retains original and conditional products; `PreparedRecycle` constructs a causal map and independent original observer; fitting owns experiments and point-cache upgrades; result projection and canonical execution own cursor and acknowledgement semantics. Those are Interface-checked footholds, not evidence that the new mechanisms are implemented.

## 3. Contracts, authority and constraints

### Selected operation contracts

**Lazy variants.** A descriptor is an immutable reconstructible input over admitted source and finite structural alternatives. Selected ordinal identity, selection extent and predecessor/start policy exist independently of preparation or lane order. Construction of a selected revision is an explicit source-owner operation; it is not an effect hidden in descriptor inspection. Each selected variant receives complete physical/provider/source admission before dispatch. Invalid or unsupported science becomes an attributable occurrence refusal; a failed variant cannot become a usable predecessor merely because it reached an operational terminal state.

The target deliberately bounds descriptors and selected occurrences without compiling the Cartesian space. This supports large potential spaces with a bounded selected question. It does not claim exhaustive optimization or permission to enumerate an unlimited space. Explicit-point inputs remain a meaningful supported form through the same executor. Stable occurrences are separate from content equality: equal bindings do not silently merge distinct occurrences or their effects. The coordinator clarified that lazy seed capability remains Pending until selected-science and overlay admission, before claim; it is not guessed from the descriptor. Existing E2 compare-and-swap and owner-incarnation authority remain in force. This makes the preparation/claim boundary explicit without adding a second occurrence authority.

**Block profiles and ready execution.** Compiler-issued block identity selects an override in the applicable prepared structural scope; schedule position is not identity. Unknown or incompatible overrides refuse. The effective-profile owner resolves native selection/settings and finite controls against each actual block. Original physical acceptance, mathematical intent, branch/start permission and aggregate clocks/work remain invariant. Ready work requires numerical, execution, validity and provider independence, not merely different incidence blocks. Canonical overlay commit and freshly bound predecessors preserve observable causality; failed blocks cannot mutate the authored specification. Stable IDs do not promise that a changed structure retains the same blocks or their compatibility.

**Frozen recycle map.** The scientific owner freezes actual inner authored starts, branch context and accuracy; retained neutral allocations carry no undeclared continuation. Raw `G(x)-x`, physical Err/Tol, sample role, latest failure and last successful state remain separate. Available context is reported as actual context; unavailable and inapplicable facts are not conflated. Repeatability/noise observations apply only to the named envelope. Frozen context does not prove global single-valuedness, uniqueness or contractivity. An unsupported or history-dependent map must refuse or use an explicitly appropriate selected method. Native libraries own damping, delayed Anderson and numerical history. Finite opt-in restarts reset that actual history and use an authorized screened start within the original aggregate caps and terminal vetoes.

**Dynamics and live samples.** Ordinary output samples and safe output waits retain live owner-affine history. New-IVP, parameter, mode, event or start changes invoke the backend-qualified reset/rebuild route. A saved state is a new input, not a serialized native-history checkpoint. The live producer is opt-in and bound before launch to the existing run lifecycle; it produces owned bounded batches only at completed sample boundaries. Original locators, requested/achieved coverage and terminal interpretation remain available without retaining a second full trajectory merely for streaming.

Computed/provisional samples, actual recording ACK and terminal complete/partial/unknown recording are different facts. A prefix is not a complete horizon, and append ACK is not scientific acceptance. Output waits obey the original cancellation/deadline and native drain; escaped buffers retain their own ownership. Dropping one subscriber releases that subscription's ownership rather than reclassifying scientific completion. Remaining producers, subscribers or recording obligations still follow the run's explicit lifecycle. The proposal's compact/full retention distinction avoids silently requiring both full in-memory output and streamed copies.

The coordinator clarified two necessary realizations of these contracts. Live batches carry the actual attempt locator: an accuracy retry can change the primal trajectory, so first-attempt observations cannot be relabeled as final science or joined into another attempt's prefix. Compact execution retains produced ordinals/coverage and incremental original-check summaries independently of retained sample arrays. Otherwise avoiding full retention would erase the premises of completion. These distinctions are adequate and remain required even if a particular backend or request does not retry.

**Fitting and effort.** Demand includes selected native-solve internals as well as requested objective, derivative and statistical products and mandatory original assessment. Consequently Value demand may still require derivatives used by the selected native method; it omits derivatives that neither method nor consumer needs. Same-point upgrades preserve parameter/event/start/provider meaning and compare physical predictions rather than assuming that a changed integration method yields identical primal steps. Owner-affine contexts reset for new parameter trials; retained preparation is distinct from numerical history. Independent experiments reduce in declared order; profile chains preserve their causal seeds. Covariance withholding, exact/Gauss–Newton distinctions and surrogate RNG/snapshot ownership survive narrowing output.

Independent fitting experiment concurrency has its own finite `experiment_workers` setting, default one, as clarified by the coordinator. It is distinct from profile-chain worker width; neither setting independently multiplies shared capacity. Sequential retained contexts therefore remain the default realization while admitted independent experiments can opt into parallel work.

Effort adaptation belongs to the existing bounded StepControl, is off by default and changes only the next displacement after a qualified endpoint. Comparable profile-scoped counters are required; unknown, zero or foreign counters use the declared baseline. Effort evidence grants no new branch permission and resets no rejection/work/deadline allowance.

No new unit/property model is introduced, so this review does not reconstruct model-specific constants or certify their fidelity. It accepts the contracts that preserve and re-establish the applicable physical checks when selected science or numerical state changes.

**Well-posedness.** Variable roles and structural analysis remain under authored-model/compiler admission. Selected structural variants require actual mode-qualified admission before native dispatch. Conditional initialization uses complete square structural products, explicit predecessor inputs and model identities; structural nonsingularity does not establish numerical rank or domain validity at newly bound values. Topology, numerical incidence and execution/provider/validity dependencies remain distinct. Coupled tear decisions retain globally shared costs/groups and an independently checked whole-graph DAG witness. Dynamics retain their supported initialization/index and derivative contracts. No proposed scheduling or lazy-construction step authorizes ill-posed science to reach a solver.

## 5. Numerical mechanisms and execution

The credible physical route is selected preparation plus bounded in-flight native work, rather than eager preparation followed by a larger pool. Value changes reuse immutable preparation and compatible worker allocations; structural alternatives incur their necessary selected compilation/admission. Ready blocks and independent experiments may run together under one existing admission envelope, while coupled SCCs and causal chains remain coupled. Canonical commits and declared reductions are intentional synchronization needed for meaning. Long output uses bounded owned batches and demanded terminal retention; it does not accumulate every emitted batch in a second cache.

| Stage / owner | Formulation, derivative and scaling contract | Class / capability and reuse | Termination / outcome / physical checks |
|---|---|---|---|
| Selected revision preparation | Original guards, physical typing, roles, provider derivatives and resolved scaling; no speculative Cartesian compilation | Actual mode-qualified structural admission; immutable preparation reused only under complete dependencies | Invalid/unsupported occurrence refuses before dispatch with source attribution; no invented ready predecessor. |
| Conditional block solve | Original conditional equations and fixed predecessor bindings; derivative order and scaling from each effective profile | Admitted class-specific native operation per block; neutral compatible reuse; complete ready-dependency witness | Finite original scope; qualified coordinates only commit; failure/cancel preserves specification; full original unscaled/physical assessment remains. |
| Recycle map / native iteration | Frozen authored inner starts, branch/accuracy and provider meaning; original domain guards and normalized/raw differences remain distinct | Admitted causal map with native fixed-point/acceleration history; no own generic acceleration controller | Original work/deadline/restart caps and terminal vetoes; typed limit/evaluation failure; independent raw-map and full original checks grant acceptance. |
| Live integration / sample production | Original DAE/ODE, event/jump, consistent-start and requested/achieved derivative semantics; original coordinate scales | Owner-affine backend history across ordinary samples; qualified new-IVP/event reset or fresh fallback; bounded output | Original time/cancel/drain; achieved horizon and missingness explicit; derivative/closure/domain checks survive compact demand; sample/prefix cannot certify terminal completeness. |
| Fitting experiment / native fit | Same physical experiment definitions; exact versus approximate derivative order and statistical conditions explicit | Selected native method's required products plus requested consumers; retained per-experiment workers, qualified reset; ordered independent reduction | Original candidate assessment, rank/bound withholding and physical upgrade comparison; no zero/guessed derivative or covariance. |
| Continuation StepControl | Existing authored path and branch meaning; comparable work observations affect only displacement | Existing bounded scientific composition around native operations | Qualified endpoints alone adapt; baseline fallback; no renewed cap or bypass of connected-path/original endpoint checks. |

These are proposed obligations, not executed numerical claims. Neither a small returned result nor a bounded queue alone proves workload fit. Here the design also removes specific incidental work: unselected structural compilation, unused response/sensitivity products and duplicate full trajectory retention. Actual factors, events, sparse workspace sizes and backend behavior still require their scoped implementation evidence.

## 7. Findings and preservation constraints

**No material finding was identified in the bounded proposed target.** There is no F01 and no manufactured SHOULD deviation. Current-source differences such as shared `PreparedBlocks.backend`, eager explicit study points and fitting's unconditional steady Jacobian work are stated pending replacements in the subject, not defects in the proposed architecture.

Preserve the following strengths during implementation: original-specification rollback and final assessment; explicit operational versus scientific dependency semantics; one owned admitted seed carried through its authorized use; native-owned iteration/history; backend-specific reset/fallback; ordered floating reduction; original cancellation/deadline/resource retention until drain; and provisional/ACK/terminal recording distinctions. Renaming these concepts while permitting consumers to infer their meaning independently would invalidate this acceptance.

## 9. Alternatives and tradeoffs

| Alternative | Assessment / selection |
|---|---|
| Existing explicit lists, shared block profile and post-run output | Remains useful for small explicit studies, uniform solves and ordinary retained results. Alone it does not serve the selected lazy variant, mixed-block and preterminal-output capabilities without eager work or delayed visibility. |
| Proposed bounded descriptors, contextual profiles and demand | Selected: it adds only the required context/identity/ownership contracts at existing seams and removes specific work. Costs include deterministic construction/recovery, exact profile applicability, buffer ownership and demand-upgrade checks. |
| Library-owned numerical work with application composition | Selected within the proposal: retain complete native solving/history capabilities; keep only scientific branch/accuracy/acceptance, finite composition and output policy with their owners. Native mechanisms cannot supply application occurrence identity or persistence meaning. |
| Simplest viable implementation | Sequential retained contexts, explicit selected ordinals and one bounded run-owned producer can establish the full semantic contracts before independent parallel work. This is compatible with the target; it needs no second scheduler or generic workflow language. |
| Eager derivative/trajectory products or a separate live-output framework | Not selected: material extra computation/retention and lifecycle machinery need a demonstrated consumer/reuse benefit. Neither is necessary to preserve mandatory original checks. |
| Stateful inner continuation or portable native-history checkpoints | Different consumed contracts, not harmless optimizations. Reopen design when a supported workload needs them; declare branch/history/replay semantics and backend capability then. |

Frozen inner starts can be less opportunistic than warm inner continuation, and ordered commit/reduction can limit throughput. Their concrete benefit is stable scientific causality and reproducible interpretation. The choice remains sound without claiming fastest convergence. Representative evidence of infeasible ordinary workloads, an unmet history-dependent target or a backend unable to honor the consumed contract would reopen it.

## 12. Decision and follow-up

**Behavioral/semantic adequacy:** adequate for the declared target contracts. **Architectural fitness:** satisfied for all seven applicable foundations. **Overall: Accept**, at Proposed / Interface-checked evidence level, for the identified G0 target and its explicitly bounded capabilities.

No correction blocks dependent production work. Implementation must realize the accepted boundaries and controls, including actual defaults: opt-in live samples and effort adaptation, experiment worker width one, no new universal acceleration/restart default, frozen inner recycle context, qualified backend reset/fresh fallback, exact seed/occurrence identity and original scientific assessment. Preserve slot 3's coordinator clarifications in the authoritative implementation contracts. A production implementation that only adds reports while retaining hidden warm history, eager undemanded work, duplicate full trajectories, mixed-attempt samples or inferred ACK/branch authority would require reassessment.

The next consequential decisions are the Plan38d investigations' actual supported envelopes and optional-policy choices. Their evidence can retain existing defaults or narrow unsupported cases explicitly; it cannot promote target acceptance into convergence, event derivative, durable recovery or assembled qualification. This review establishes no such promotion.
