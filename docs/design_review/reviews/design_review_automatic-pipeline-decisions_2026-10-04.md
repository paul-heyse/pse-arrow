# Automatic pipeline decisions: bounded N0 review

**Decision: Accept at Proposed target-design evidence.** The automatic amendments to
ADR-0154/0155, ADR-0158 and Plan 25n's selected contracts form a coherent design for the
changed request, assessment, evidence, native-binding and recorded-interpretation boundaries.
No material blocking correction remains in the inspected proposals. This decision does not
accept the existing implementation, resolve AF-01–AF-08, qualify a solver or accept an ADR.

The useful organizing distinction is permission versus execution. The request owns the original
target, guarantees and allowed alternatives. A pure resolver selects one next operation using
actual facts and products. The driver executes that operation under the enclosing scope, while
the scientific owner independently decides what the original candidate establishes. Native
layout, arithmetic and work remain with their library and adapter owners. The durable boundary
records the requested interpretation rather than silently applying a later Auto default.

## Responsibilities, contracts and representative changes

| Owner | Meaning and responsibility | Consumer boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Request / strategy declarations | Original target and guarantees, explicit backend/profile/mechanism constraints, start/branch permission and finite controls | Resolution may narrow permission; observations, available products and adapter defaults cannot create it |
| Compiler/math products | Distinct numerical incidence, execution/validity and coupling projections; complete reconstruction and actual accuracy products | BTF is structural evidence; reconstruction/sheet/action contracts determine admissible reduction and reuse |
| Pure resolver | Deterministic one-next-decision policy using cheap facts, conditional capabilities, actual products, observations and remaining allowance | Returns Prepare, Execute, Assess, Finish or Stop; expensive production is an explicit effect |
| Driver / execution scope | Dispatch, product-state updates, termination, inclusive work and retained allocation lifetimes | One state feeds both the next decision and trace; no scientific report interpretation or independent workflow retry policy |
| Scientific assessor | Original checks, conclusion/cause, composed candidate permission and independently admitted retained artifacts | Native termination remains visible; original rejection, assessment inability and terminal operation failure remain distinguishable |
| Native adapter and source library | Effective typed profile, actual classification/layout, separator binding, primitive operation admission/observation and teardown | Library arithmetic, inertia, quality escalation and globalization remain library-owned; incidental offsets do not escape into workflows |
| Public / durable boundary | Versioned request/document interpretation, generated vocabulary and layered identities | Header admission precedes current nested decoding; history retains its bytes and interpretation |

These boundaries are justified by concrete variation axes. A new recovery profile changes the
adapter and admitted policy, rather than statistical stopping or publication. A new scientific
target supplies its callable contract and assessor, rather than duplicating the numerical driver.
A native source revision changes products consuming that build, rather than immutable evaluator
mathematics. The request meaning itself is a legitimate shared contract migration across callers,
generation and durable readmission; its coordinated breadth is necessary, not evidence of
avoidable coupling.

N0 preserves declared variable roles and the compiler's degree-of-freedom/class/structural
admission. Numerical incidence, control/validity dependencies and objective/inequality coupling
are deliberately different projections: a control cycle does not invent rank, and a topology
edge does not prove solve order. Structural BTF alone does not prove a regular selected sheet.
Complete original assembly includes non-port unknowns; partial local values stay transactional.
An objective-only reduced optimization or objective-free feasibility problem can legitimately
have no retained equality rows. A positive-dimensional underdetermined qualified-root request
is refused instead. These are coherent target requirements; N1/N4 must realize them and retain
diagnostics mapped to original model elements.

## Numerical effects, accuracy and lifetime

| Stage | Formulation / derivative / scaling contract | Class/capability and outcomes | Admission, tolerance and independent check |
|---|---|---|---|
| Preparation / producer | Preserve authored guards and complete correspondence; demand actual Value/First/Second/actions rather than whole-case blanket First; normalization belongs to evidence | Conditional producer support is distinct from ready evidence | Visible bounded production under the same scope; support and exact provenance do not imply zero numerical error |
| Native execution | Effective typed profile; Partitioned/FD consume analytic First and conservative support, including stationary objective support; approximation/degradation stays observable | Actual target class and admitted adapter capability; native status remains separate from original conclusion | Poll/admit primitive work and full storage before operation/allocation; source-owned Schur and library-owned iteration |
| Original correction | Frozen original target, original bounds/guards/objective and requested guarantees; excludes recursive selection of the corrected auxiliary family | Only an admitted correction capable of supplying the missing guarantee | Same enclosing scope; no automatic copying of auxiliary duals, global or infeasibility certificates |
| Original assessment | Scientific owner evaluates the complete original candidate with original physical/normalized criteria | Original acceptance, rejection or inability, with typed cause and permission | Native final validation and independent assessment are charged once, including failure; neither may cross a strict cap before its next unit is admitted |

The complete-reservation alternative is sound only as an enforcement bound, never a fabricated
measurement. It must cover all work admitted under it, including validation, assessment, quality
escalation and fallback. Retaining unknown actual work after a fully bounded operation therefore
does not weaken a hard cap. A partial native subtotal cannot replace an unknown inclusive count.
The proposal also honestly limits deadlines: checks around indivisible work can observe an
overrun, but late evidence cannot become successful completion.

The native addition is appropriately located at the operations that know the real layout and
allocation extents. Existing `TNLPAdapter::classification` and builder Schur configuration establish
a fitting source boundary. Existing Schur code can fall back after unsuccessful factor work;
this makes a notification after construction or an invocation counter insufficient. The proposed
fallible synchronous seam supplies binding before consumption, actual primitive observations and
terminal abort propagation while leaving matrix construction, Schur arithmetic, inertia and
numerical fallback within POUNCE/FERAL. Panic containment and worker-local attempt reset prevent
the observer from becoming ambient mutable policy.

Both Schur factors, coupling/separator/refinement storage and simultaneously live fallback objects
retain their allowances through destruction. Separator fraction alone does not admit memory.
The fork's complete source family and immutable revision must be coherent before native use;
the source/build identity invalidates only products consuming it. These are implementation
obligations for N5/N6, not assertions that the new seam already exists or has a proven bound.

## Findings, alternatives and verification obligations

No material open finding was identified in the revised N0 proposals. ADR-0155 now explicitly
distinguishes the historical 25m document versions from its next-current amendment; ADR-0154/0155
also distinguish 25m's original evidence owner from 25n's automatic-amendment disposition owner.
ADR-0154's cap wording now expressly rejects work without a pre-operation hook or complete bound,
preserving unknown measured counts under a fully retained conservative reservation.
The review does not introduce another status ledger or supersede the diagnostic findings.

The simplest viable alternative to a new engine is the selected pure next-decision operation
over existing products, adapters and effect driver. Retaining caller-authored retry ladders would
leave automatic users assembling mechanisms and repeat policy across scientific consumers.
An always-correct-again rule would waste an admitted original candidate and still could not
manufacture global/branch/certificate guarantees. Direct original assessment where correspondence
suffices, admitted correction where it can help and typed refusal otherwise is the smaller
coherent contract. Conversely, permitting implicit replacement starts would weaken legitimate
Explicit/NoPriorStart requests; the empty default origins preserve their meaning.

For native observation, a wrapper around opaque calls cannot know failed Schur work, actual
classification or simultaneously live storage. Reimplementing Schur would create a competing
numerical owner. The source-owned seam is justified by those concrete missing capabilities and
has an acknowledged maintenance cost until upstream supplies equivalent contracts. Upgrading
or replacing the library reopens this choice when those consumed contracts are available; no
speedup or universal backend preference is asserted.

## Authority route and final decision

ADR-0154/0155 remain proposed amendments; ADR-0158 remains a proposed native binding decision.
The architecture amendment route covers blueprint §14, §17.4, §§18.6–18.8, §19.3 and §20.5 with
its revision row. Existing accepted records are preserved or superseded through the prescribed
route when a conflicting clause requires it. Decision-PR acceptance and publication remain
separate from implementation authorization and this review.

The selected current cutover is SolveSettings v2, NumericalStrategyDocument v2,
StudyOperation/StudyRequest v3, StudyDefinition v5 and JobPayload v7, with changed identity frames
and header-first readmission. Internal binding can migrate before activation, but stored v1
settings must not gain the new Auto meaning in that intermediate state. The immutable native
source artifact and complete family pin are N6 prerequisites, not an upstream release promise.

**Behavioral/semantic adequacy:** Accept for the proposed changed contracts, with runtime
realization unqualified. **Architectural fitness:** Accept for these boundaries and scenarios.
**Overall:** Accept at Proposed target-design evidence. Changed-contract implementation may
proceed against this design; no production scope is closed by the decision. Plan 25n owns
implementation/disposition and Plan 25k owns assembled qualification and measurement.
