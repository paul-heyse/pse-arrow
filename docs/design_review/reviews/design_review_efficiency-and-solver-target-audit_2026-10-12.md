# Independent audit of the efficiency and solver target review

## Assessment and decision

**Decision: Revise the consolidated target before adopting it.** The [source review](design_review_efficiency-and-solver-target_2026-10-12.md) identifies material architectural problems: local model work depends on remote canonical-store activity, solver preference and transition policy have several owners, and useful native state and observations do not consistently survive at their consumed lifetimes. A local admitted model, one governing solver-policy seam, operation-shaped reuse and shared resource placement are strong directions. They can improve the product without waiting for a wholesale persistence replacement.

The audit does **not** endorse the source review's proposed topology as the uniquely justified remedy. A store-free computation path, durable edit acknowledgement, automatic cross-session visibility, and independent writers are separate decisions. Immutable records still need publication, recovery, readers and collection. Several claims about missing capabilities are also incorrect: session-free compiled validation, structural block execution, per-block initialization routing, native HiGHS updates, actual retained POUNCE/FERAL factors and admitted profile-likelihood parallelism already exist. Preserve and extend these paths.

The solver target needs substantive corrections. A universal allocation-free second solve is unsupported; retained native state can include value-sensitive policy history; IDAS continuation is different from restart; KINSOL normally returns the last evaluated fixed-point iterate; restoration-aware Ipopt callback snapshots are available; sparse QR does not preserve SVD diagnostic meaning. Hard cancellation must remain terminal even when richer diagnosis would be useful. Advisory history needs explicit branch, budget and statistical interpretation rather than a promise that snapshotting makes execution deterministic.

**Architectural fitness: G9 fails for the inspected current scope and the replacement target requires revision.** The source-supported execution and policy-locality defects remain after correcting overstatements. **Behavioral adequacy: Revise narrowly; broader guarantees remain unresolved by this audit.** Missing recycle history is an unmet PS-08 requirement, and observation/capability fidelity needs correction. No new path to a falsely accepted solution was established. Existing independent original-space assessment is a material strength, not whole-product qualification.

This is a DESIGN / TARGET audit against Core 3.4, Efficient Architecture 1.0, Process Simulator 1.5 and the pse-arrow binding. The principal coordinator owns this integrated judgment; bounded source and library inquiries plus a fresh independent target assessment support it. Findings and rule positions here are recommendations, not live dispositions. Future plan creation must reconcile this audit and the source review and present rule changes for confirmation. No plan, ADR, architecture section or product implementation was changed.

## Scope, evidence and distinguishing scenarios

The functional target is the profile's best-in-class simulator and blueprint §0/§0.2: interactive edit → re-solve without structural rebuilding for value changes; sweeps, sensitivities, optimization and fitting; recycles and tears; dynamics from the same model definitions; extension with units, properties or solvers; and compiling/testing a responsibility with its actual dependencies. Product-shaped build, test and Python-boundary consequences are included. General host and agent policy are outside this audit.

The inspected baseline is `6c368c88acda548d421b44e8fc4f457c95bcec7e`, with the source review, its evidence and its README edit already present as another author's uncommitted work. Those documents were preserved. The audit reads all fourteen findings, eleven principal rule impacts, the thirteen solver impacts nested under RC10, the relevant supporting inquiries and all thirty-nine Plan 36 packet scopes. Prior reviews and dispositions are leads; current rules and pins describe the subject rather than deciding merit.

| Scenario | Required distinction and observed boundary |
|---|---|
| S01 — Edit a value, bound or fixed role and re-solve | Reuse immutable structure only while its complete premises hold. Refresh numerical state and original assessment; classification-changing bounds may invalidate a native layout. Current local preparation is partly reusable, but runtime opening/selection still crosses the canonical store. |
| S02 — Sweep, fit or analyze many related cases | Freeze consumed prepared products, distinguish independent points from declared continuation edges, bound live native sessions and share the aggregate budget. Existing fitting profile chains already compose admitted parallel work. |
| S03 — Fail a recycle or stop a native method | Preserve authored specification and last successfully evaluated state; record raw map residuals, stop cause and unavailable evidence. Cancellation does not authorize another method or extra diagnosis. |
| S04 — Continue dynamics, cross an event or restart a process | Live integrator continuation, discontinuity reinitialization and state-only restart have different history and derivative guarantees. Partial trajectories must retain their actual committed prefix. |
| S05 — Publish, read, retire or restore history | Distinguish computation authority, durability acknowledgement, publication visibility, occurrence identity and payload ownership. Concurrent readers and crash uncertainty survive a single-writer design. |
| S06 — Add a unit/property or replace a solver | Preserve one authored model and physical/provider contracts. | S07 — Compile/test policy or validation locally | Pure policy and typed local validation need no running database or unrelated native solver. Actual Cargo/dev/build closure must still be checked before claiming complete compile isolation. |
| S08 — Grow one sparse solve or several concurrent workloads | Match dependency-ready blocks, independent experiments and native inner teams to one deployment envelope. Resident sessions, queued work, analytics, process IPC and active CPU have different obligations. |

Current source/interface claims are **Interface-checked**. Remedies, comparisons and expected benefits are **Proposed**. This audit ran **no probes, product builds/tests, services or measurements**. It does not establish latency, allocation counts, convergence superiority, native ABI correctness or assembled scientific qualification. ## Working authority, immutable products and durable effects

The cleanest first boundary is a **locally owned admitted model product**. Model preparation consumes authored declarations, resolved providers/physical context, selected structural roles and demanded programs. A solve consumes that immutable product plus current numerical bindings and requested solve policy. The composition root chooses whether edits, attempts and outputs are recorded. Neither semantic preparation nor a pure method decision should acquire a store protection simply to prove a local product still means what its owner already established.

`pse-runtime/src/workflow/modeling/canonical.rs`, `math/modeling.rs` and `math/preparation.rs` show the relevant current boundaries. `BasisKey` already includes dependencies, bindings, demand and limits. Improve reuse by separating stable structure from current attribution and demand only where those dimensions are genuinely independent. A derivative-demand change can require a new program; it must not be ignored to improve cache hits. Native compilation remains an effect outside tracked compiler queries. Lazy admission of distinct structures during discrete studies can avoid preparing an enormous possibility space up front, while each actually consumed bundle remains frozen and owned.

Salsa 0.28.4 can govern pure dependency recomputation. Its clones are shared storage; mutation cancels and waits for outstanding handles. Freeze owned products for studies, release query handles before mutation and bound separately owned live lineages. Database-local/interned identities are unsuitable as durable keys across database generations, processes or restarts. LRU does not by itself bound input/dependency metadata or foreign heaps. Keep the allocation owner and generation-rebuild mechanism until an alternative covers those obligations. An optional content-addressed disk memo can coexist with one semantic dependency authority; its load validation, serialization/version and native-artifact trust costs need justification.

Durability belongs at the acknowledgement boundary. If an API edit promises survival of an immediate native crash, successful acknowledgement must follow the required durable append or an independently surviving writer's committed acknowledgement. A background append in the same process is insufficient. This need not put canonical rehydration on the next solve's critical path. Automatic durable recording and automatic publication can compose with local computation; explicit publication is an intentional API choice with affected history, artifact and analysis consumers.

The proposed immutable record still has transitions: publish a complete manifest, retry an uncertain acknowledgement, select a latest outcome, retire an occurrence, retain shared payloads, protect a reader and restore a consistent closure. Minted occurrence ids make idempotent intent easier; they do not tell a caller whether a particular effect committed. Store-native uniqueness, references and locked transitions can own appropriate parts. SurrealDB references cannot protect arbitrary external files, and a sequence token does not prove its claimant remains current.

The [state/boundary inquiry](../evidence/efficiency-and-solver-target-audit-2026-10-12/state-reuse-and-boundaries.md) compares complete alternatives:

| Alternative | Benefit retained | Decision-changing costs and conditions |
|---|---|---|
| Local snapshots + current remote publication tier | Removes canonical computation coupling while retaining independent writers and existing durable APIs | Keeps transport/server machinery for consumers that use it; simplify transitions separately. Viable without deciding topology first. |
| Local snapshots + runner-owned journal/catalog and child workers | Local edit/solve and crash-isolated native work without peer database clients | Needs durable acknowledgement, exclusive writer succession, child attribution/late-message rejection, catalog arbitration, readers/GC and backup. Fits only if runner failure/lifetime semantics satisfy the product. |
| Local snapshots + SQLite WAL catalog + immutable files | Independent same-host processes, library-serialized writes, no application server | Busy/retry/checkpoint and file/catalog coordination remain; WAL is unsuitable for network filesystems. Exact Rust binding/version, schema and operational fit remain unselected and unmeasured. |
| Per-writer journals/manifests + shared arbitration | Local appends and immutable analytics snapshots | Discovery, heads, ordering, retirement, referenced closure and recovery can recreate a bespoke database. Compare the whole lifecycle. |
| SurrealDB-native coordination + immutable files | Shared mutation and richer catalog behavior remain library-owned | Remote uncertainty, leases/readers and external-file atomicity remain. Appropriate where independent remote/surviving consumers need them. |

Independent same-host writers and remote or independently surviving workers should be decided separately. This audit compares both writer models because no new product premise selecting one was supplied. The storage choice is **unresolved**; the local computation recommendation is not.

For every file-backed candidate, expose only an exact committed immutable manifest, with objects durable before catalog visibility and commit acknowledgement. Define journal discovery and terminal ordering for `latest_results`, `results(run…)`, promotion, analysis and reclaim. Seal complete Parquet chunks or explicitly recoverable IPC segments; an incomplete Parquet row group is not a promised crash-safe prefix. Preserve gaps, partiality and terminal outcomes. Hold GC before taking a backup snapshot and retain the referenced file/journal/start closure until copying ends. A database backup does not automatically include external files or unpublished work.

A fixed grace period cannot preserve an arbitrarily paused reader. The source target's mid-read retirement proposal is a capability weakening requiring an explicit rule decision. Compare snapshot pins, owned open-file handles on supported Linux paths and read/GC exclusion before selecting it. Child-process pipes likewise need bounded backpressure, authenticated attempt/owner identity, cancellation/drain and late-completion rejection; they remove WebSocket machinery, not these obligations.

## One solver-policy seam with class-specific scientific execution

The target should have **one authoritative place for preference and transition policy**, composed with the existing typed eligibility and original-assessment seams. A request supplies problem class, physical/provider context, representation/derivative capabilities, bounds/domain/branch conditions, explicit backend choice, requested assurance, allowed reformulation/global search and start/warm policy. Class-specific builders propose candidates. Contextual adapter assessment determines whether each candidate is actually usable with its required artifacts/settings. Placement supplies an executable resource allocation. Neither static `admit` nor a history recommendation substitutes for these checks.

This is ordinary scientific composition. It need not be one giant function, a universal serialized plan or a new controller beside existing Auto logic. A new backend should add its capability/profile binding and consumed method implementation, with policy changes only for genuinely new scientific preference. A new workflow should consume the same request/decision/assessment primitives rather than reproduce the router. Explicit selection remains explicit; unavailable evidence is distinct from mathematical refusal; root, VI, least-squares stationary, feasible, stationary NLP and globally certified results retain different permission.

Resource-aware selection is legitimate when explicitly requested among equivalent permitted profiles. Available capacity can ordinarily control placement; where it changes profile selection or effective width, record that choice and preserve accuracy/branch permission. Do not prohibit all resource-aware policy or let transient pressure silently redefine the answer. Fitting should jointly choose algorithm, derivative mode and required experiment products. Its existing point-cache/adjoint-upgrade behavior is a strength; retaining compatible per-experiment contexts and avoiding unconsumed derivative/trajectory work are further Proposed opportunities, not established performance gains. Exact Hessian, Gauss–Newton, covariance, profile and event-sensitive derivatives retain different contracts.

Three layers remain useful. Mathematical rules choose eligible representations and candidates. Synchronous typed observations can trigger bounded safeguarded transitions. Advisory history may rank or tune permitted candidates. The observation path used for correctness/policy must not depend on lossy telemetry delivery. One compact in-memory attempt summary can serve decisions, user explanation and optional persistence without requiring a per-event journal or cost-accounting framework.

Transitions consume typed causes and screened proposals. Cancellation, deadline, contract invalidity, panic and infrastructure/resource failure are terminal vetoes. Existing aggregate work/deadline ledgers must survive every successor; a transition cannot reset them. “Best merit” needs original-space normalized physical residual/feasibility, domain and branch provenance and deterministic tie handling, not objective alone. Projection is an explicit transformation with rescreening. Near-singular factor evidence may appear during execution, not at initial binding. A policy stop must distinguish available diagnostic/start evidence from a successful final solution.

Preserve `quality.rs`, `strategy.rs`, `blocks_step` and immutable initialization overlays: each candidate is evaluated against the original problem, and only that assessment grants use. Per-block policy must consume complete numerical plus execution/validity dependencies and retain a fresh assembled original check. Existing `PreparedInitialization::strategies` already makes contextual block decisions; the gap is coherent suitability, independently configurable profiles and appropriate parallel readiness, not inventing the first structural route.

### Recycles, diagnosis, advice and reproducibility

Recycle history is the immediate behavioral priority. Record bounded raw map residuals and the last successful map evaluation, plus acceleration/restart, domain failures, inner solve provenance and truncation/drop information. KINSOL's accelerated step norm is not `G(x)-x`; its default last-evaluated return is useful. Choose `KINSetReturnNewest` explicitly if exposing that option. A causal map with branch-dependent warm-started inner solves may not be a repeatable function of tear values. Define a frozen branch/start context or treat changed context as a new map before enabling Anderson. Noise floors and bounded restart rules matter; acceleration is not universally monotone or safe.

Automatic boundary diagnostics already name model elements. Rich numerical diagnosis can add factor observations, infeasibility explanations, derivative/domain evidence and replayable start state where consumed. At a hard stop, use retained evidence or a reserved/separately requested diagnostic allocation. Do not defer cancellation until Ipopt leaves restoration. Its restoration-aware `get_curr_iterate` can capture original-space primal/dual information during callbacks; it does not expose every restoration slack, and the ordinary final returned point can be older. Report unavailable evidence explicitly.

Replay has several levels: reconstructing original inputs, replaying effective decisions, rerunning numerics and resuming native state. Record the level promised. A replayable bundle needs immutable model/case/provider/physical inputs, effective settings, start and transform/branch dependencies, executed decisions/observations and build/runtime premises. Pin the actual effective permutation/tuning/limits, not only a mutable history reference. Existing export/record paths can carry this before any journal redesign. A frozen plan does not make wall-clock stopping, adaptive observations or concurrent native execution bitwise deterministic.

History is advisory and must leave a complete baseline policy when unavailable. Reordering methods can change a nonconvex branch or which candidates receive a finite budget; eligibility alone does not authorize either change. Statistical records must define their estimand, cohort, phase/queue/execution intervals and competing outcomes. External interruptions and policy stops can be informative censoring; conditioning on plan position/start does not automatically remove adaptive exposure bias. Conservative within-cohort summaries and tie-breaking can be useful without unsupported causal claims about solver superiority. These qualifications belong to the history contract, not a new mandatory analytics research program.

The [pipeline inquiry](../evidence/efficiency-and-solver-target-audit-2026-10-12/pipeline-diagnostics-and-advice.md) supplies detailed counterexamples, per-impact positions and verification conditions.

## Native reuse, mathematical methods and operation-shaped parallelism

Reuse has four distinct scopes: immutable structural analysis, retained allocation, numerical state and value-sensitive policy history. A compatible layout does not imply a reusable factor; a fresh factor does not imply a fresh scaling/rescue policy; a retained allocation does not grant warm-start permission. Complete profile/start/reuse-after-terminal contracts must name these distinctions.

The pinned-library audit establishes the following corrections; the [native inquiry](../evidence/efficiency-and-solver-target-audit-2026-10-12/native-contracts-and-methods.md) gives exact files/versions and limitations:

- Ipopt 3.14.20 repeats native symbolic initialization when it reaches ordinary factorization, even with `warm_start_same_structure`; early exits are exceptions. That option retains matrix conversion structure as well as TNLP maps. Its bounds path still allocates arrays, so zero allocation is not a valid universal acceptance criterion.
- Bound-value changes can be compatible when fixed/free, finite/infinite, equality and treatment classifications remain unchanged. Refresh compatible numeric bounds; rebuild when those map premises change. Freezing all bound values is stronger than necessary for the target.
- SUNDIALS KLU reanalysis occurs at actual setup after initialization; NoInitSetup can initially use the retained numeric factor and an already-satisfied start may exit first. Existing KINSOL cache reuse is restricted by compatible settings, non-Fresh policy and independently feasible prior quality; it is not reuse after every failure.
- Live IDAS continuation preserves multistep history without `IDAReInit`. Reinitialization serves a new IVP/discontinuity or a state-only restart and loses that history. `IDA_NORMAL` output can be interpolated. Diffsol has its own retained solver/state contract; do not apply an IDAS API to it.
- The project POUNCE custom factory genuinely retains FERAL symbolic backend state by role/ordinal, forces new-application numeric refactor and discards bad-quality/fatal leases. Upstream convenience batches instead construct fresh applications; batching and retention are different axes and may serve different workloads.
- FERAL `n_tiny=0` is a useful inertia-policy screen, not an exact-inertia theorem. Perturbations can change the represented matrix, while some preserve inertia under separation. State the actual perturbation/mode and confidence. Preserve the concrete Pardiso caution: the pinned Ipopt interface can mask a negative-eigenvalue deficit. A conservative profile restriction is different from claiming Pardiso's mathematical domain is convex-only.
- No-HSL does not leave user scaling as the only Ipopt option: gradient-based scaling is available and default at this pin. Scientific nominal scaling remains a defensible choice, with start-dependence and derivative-scale tradeoffs. Honest solution-level parity is appropriate regardless of which linear solver is used.
- Affine blocks need one factorization plus bounded solve/refinement and original acceptance, not literally one solve while also promising refinement. Exact polynomial isolation certifies an exact defining polynomial; map physical floating parameters and branch/uncertainty meaning explicitly. Near-multiple sensitivity does not invalidate the exact certificate.
- Prefer concrete library owners: PETSc SNESNEWTONTR for suitable root trust-region needs; native KINSOL difference-quotient Jv for suitable repeatable opaque residuals with scaling/preconditioning; NLEQ-ERR for its actual Newton/VI composition scope. Do not replace root acceptance with bounded least-squares stationarity or label a damping method a singular-Jacobian cure.
- Sparse QR may supply a new rank/diagnostic tier but cannot silently replace current singular-value threshold and near-null singular-mode semantics. Retain bounded SVD where that interpretation is required. SPQR build/API fit was not qualified here.

Parallelism should exploit actual independent work: ready blocks, independent profile/fit experiments, shooting windows where their semantics permit, and native inner teams for coupled problems. Existing profile-likelihood chains already account worker/core/stack demand and use quiescent dispatch. Existing CPU, population and active-job counters represent different obligations; reducing their count is not a demonstrated simplification. Preserve charged parked sessions, tentative-acquisition release, no hold-and-wait deadlock, parent-resident memory and actual native join before release.

Declare resource use for the **selected complete profile**, including nested libraries. Ipopt's MUMPS mutex is conditional and local to the selected interface/library, not a universal direct-MUMPS lock. SCIP can invoke nested Ipopt/BLAS resources; Clarabel's selected backend matters. PETSc MPIUNI process lanes isolate independent solves and do not parallelize one solve with MPI. A SUNDIALS context per thread is supported; sequential migration remains unsettled. FERAL's persistent pool needs an actual admitted width seam, but its source-backed bit-neutral driver design does not justify universal width sensitivity. Faer uses ambient Rayon; explicit scoped installation can retain library-owned parallelism.

Capacity and scientific eligibility are distinct, but not independent of the requested profile: a request for an unsupported inner width can be a profile incompatibility, while unavailable capacity is placement. The target cannot both preserve the present `threads != 1` refusal unchanged and move all width handling into placement without specifying that boundary. Process lanes and a separate analytics executor remain children of one deployment CPU/memory/spill/cache envelope. Separate pools do not create separate machines. The [parallelism/coverage inquiry](../evidence/efficiency-and-solver-target-audit-2026-10-12/parallelism-and-coverage.md) records the current shared owner and admitted compositions.

## Additional findings

### <a id="au01"></a>AU01 — Compatible HiGHS updates resend the complete numerical model through scalar setters

**Cause and evidence — Interface-checked.** On the retained staged coefficient path, `execution/highs.rs` reaches `pse-backend-native/src/highs.rs::Session::update`. It updates every column cost/bound, matrix nonzero, row bound, objective setting and Hessian for every compatible update. The session retains layout but no complete consumed value delta. A one-bound edit therefore resends unchanged sparse coefficients and vectors through scalar C calls. The production consumer path and exact HiGHS 1.15.0 bulk-vector API were independently checked by two inquiries; this is separate from the incorrectly disputed native-update capability.

**Principles/scenario and consequence.** AP-07/G9, DP-10; S01/S02; H8/H12/H13. Repeated compatible sparse LP/QP steps pay avoidable native crossings and update work even after session retention. No complete-workflow speed or asymptotic improvement was measured. Transport, upload verification and independent original assessment still consume broad input.

**Proposed correction and owner.** At the native coefficient adapter, use existing cost/column-bound/row-bound Range/Set/Mask APIs for whole-vector or selected updates. Bulk vectors are the simpler first remedy. If a trusted normalized predecessor delta or retained uploaded snapshot earns its detection/memory cost, skip unchanged matrix/Hessian content and update actual changed entries. No general bulk matrix-value API was established. Keep a checked full-update/rebuild path and one coefficient authority; do not add a per-entry hash ledger solely for this optimization.

Preserve effective binary clipping, coordinates, coefficient-to-zero changes, objective sense/offset, Hessian semantics, native errors and explicit basis/start policy. Commit a new content stamp only after all mutations succeed. A partial native mutation must invalidate or fully restore the session even under RequireReuse; the current update-error-to-reuse-refusal path is not a sufficient contract for a new delta implementation. Diagnostic/lexicographic mutations must also restore or invalidate the uploaded-content premise.

**Verification — Proposed, not run.** Compare full rebuild/full update/bulk/delta for one-cost, one-bound, one-coefficient, coefficient-to-zero, broad, Hessian and incompatible-layout changes. Inject failure after a successful mutation, including forbidden rebuild. Observe submitted operations, effective native values and fresh original acceptance; distinguish explicit basis use from implicit retained state. Count removed calls before claiming removed work and measure representative full routes before claiming speed. This finding can be corrected independently of journals, topology and common routing.

### <a id="au02"></a>AU02 — Retained native solver policy can remain value-history-dependent after a fresh numeric factorization

**Cause and evidence — Interface-checked, outcome impact conditional.** The project's POUNCE `retained::Lease` keeps an actual FERAL backend when the pattern is unchanged and forces a numeric refactor for a new application. The pinned FERAL fork's `numeric/solver.rs` retains `mc64_retry_not_adopted` by pattern. Its source explicitly documents that a non-adopted retry for one numerical matrix suppresses a potentially useful rescue for later values on the same pattern. Auto scaling/ordering choices also have retained history. Thus allocation/symbolic retention plus “fresh numeric factor” does not by itself establish fresh numerical policy. Applicability depends on the selected Auto/rescue profile and reached native path; no falsely accepted result or material regression was reproduced.

**Principles/scenario and consequence.** AP-04/AP-05, G4/G6, PS-07/PS-11; S01/S02/S08. A later independent attempt can inherit value-sensitive rescue decisions from earlier work assigned to the same retained backend, changing permitted retries, convergence/work or numerical confidence without an explicit start/policy dependency. This extends F07 beyond previous factors and L-BFGS/basis vectors.

**Proposed correction and owner.** The native profile/reuse contract must classify and reset value-sensitive heuristic state at independent-attempt boundaries, or explicitly declare/pin its reuse as numerical policy history. Prefer a library reset/configuration seam that retains pure symbolic analysis and allocation; compare fixed profiles, fresh backend fallback or a value-aware library correction where no seam exists. Do not reimplement MC64 or numerical factorization. Preserve useful existing factor retention and per-attempt counter truth. Within one declared continuation chain, retained heuristic policy may be an intentional choice rather than forbidden state.

**Verification — Proposed, not run.** Identify reachable profiles and state-reset hooks in the pinned fork; compare fresh versus reused backends on the same target matrix after different preceding values, including singular then numerically rescuable same-pattern cases. ## Priorities, prerequisites and Plan 36 coverage

**Priority follows consequence.** First preserve truthful outcomes, terminal cancellation and original-space acceptance while fixing recycle history and numerical capability/observation errors. The largest architectural priorities are store-independent local preparation, one governing solver-policy seam and explicit native reuse/resource contracts. Result batching, native bulk updates and bounded diagnostics can improve independently. **Prerequisites are local to the consumer.** Complete dependency/context ownership enables local preparation; acknowledgement and reader/API contracts enable persistence replacement. Exact compatibility/reset contracts enable safe session reuse; complete execution dependencies and resource declarations enable parallel blocks. Trustworthy records and branch/budget policy enable advisory history. None requires a journal as a universal first step. Measurements can distinguish complete alternatives when useful, but do not gate a source-established defect or method admission.
