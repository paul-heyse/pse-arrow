## Coherent domain model and ownership

The proposals model consequential operations, rather than merely their outputs. A task carries the immutable original problem and binding, frozen final numerical policy, strategy, start permission, scope and retained-product inventory. Pure resolution consumes explicit facts and produces finite admitted work. The driver owns effects, observation and charging; transition consumes typed observations. Existing original completion determines scientific result permission.

| Distinction | Owner and consequence |
|---|---|
| Capability incompatibility versus failed trajectory | Routing owns capability facts; strategy history owns numerical observations. Failure does not falsely disqualify a backend |

These distinctions are adequate for the changed scope and govern proposed resolution, execution, assessment and retention. Their authoritative realization remains an implementation obligation. Shared strategy belongs in existing model/compiler/math/native/runtime owners. Generated traces use the existing declaration route. Foreign sys crates isolate ABI ownership without creating a second workflow engine or mathematical authority.

## Declared recovery preserves selection and permission

ADR-0154 corrects overbroad no-fallback interpretation without arbitrary substitution. Explicit selection remains binding; changing backend requires named alternatives. Same-backend profile recovery can be lawful after numerical failure because capability incompatibility and trajectory history differ.

Required invalid contracts, task exhaustion, cancellation, infrastructure failure and panic terminate the affected task. Lawful numerical failures may take declared, applicable and affordable transitions. Optional refusal/local exhaustion can preserve the base route. Iteration caps alone do not establish stagnation. Independently validated original infeasibility can terminate execution. Derived success receives auxiliary assessment, never automatic original completion.

One ExecutionScope spans preparation, rungs, nested work and assessment. Attempt abandonment stops a trajectory without poisoning cancellation for successors. Late indivisible calls cannot contribute retained/accepted evidence after exhaustion. The direct one-rung strategy remains a complete minimal case.

## Derived families, accuracy and selection

ADR-0155 preserves one authored model. Families declare reconstruction, bounds/guards/inequalities/objective/selection, actual incidence and derivative/action support. Terminal identity, conditional equivalence, block coverage and approximate correspondence differ. Block completion does not prove simultaneous satisfaction. Auxiliary duals/stationarity/certificates do not transfer. Reconstructed work needs original assessment at frozen final policy; an extra original solve can be omitted only when all required observations and permission already exist.

Opaque oracles keep admitted partial/JVP/HVP contracts. Wrappers cannot manufacture exact derivatives or infer empty incidence from Value support. Accuracy does not silently promote physical/backward-error acceptance to certified forward accuracy, nor let estimates satisfy certificates. Demand names action/output, order, source, normalization, allowance and evidence class. Inner tolerances follow admitted amplification; a fixed tighter factor is insufficient for ill-conditioned roots/nested jets.

New identity frames preserve historical bytes. Proof/root/jet/setup/transport products have distinct validity; stronger numerical accuracy may require recomputation without invalidating structural proof. Same-point promotion reuses unchanged exclusion. Nearby reuse needs a valid uniform chart or certified chart chain covering the segment with common-root connections. Endpoint certificates, overlapping boxes or same regime do not establish connectedness. Rejected transport leaves prior lineage intact.

Canonical operational Value semantics survive when start-independent selected meaning is unestablished. Warm roots cannot change selection. Selected endpoint minima may change sheets, but do not qualify connected tracking.

## Scoped foreign ownership

ADR-0157 defines narrow integrations. Uno’s profiles, curvature limits and matching HiGHS linkage are explicit. Shield all entrypoints/getters. Rust panics and terminal causes cannot cross ABI or enter recoverable retries. Sparse order, signs, width and lifetime remain integration obligations.

TSPSEUDO declares an inner profile rather than relying on a different default. Controls act or refuse; effective settings/stops are recorded. Binding availability cannot alter explicit selection. Actual acquisition/ABI/callback/lifetime controls remain required; proposal acceptance does not qualify linked libraries.

## Findings, alternatives and verification

**No new material finding was established in the corrected proposals.** Earlier findings retain their evidence and 25m dispositions. Private retries duplicate interpretation; a universal engine absorbs scientific authority. Cold-only operation cannot supply continuity or required reuse. Shared strategy is the smallest viable composition preserving direct minimal execution.

Foreign integrations cost acquisition/ABI/lifecycle ownership; narrow profiles bound those costs. Licenses or hypothetical ranking do not reject libraries. Planned isolated transitions, reconstruction/accuracy, selected-sheet controls and actual native controls are proportionate. Scripted tests cannot qualify bindings; no exhaustive campaign is added. No unit/property model changes through proposal acceptance. Their conformance is not newly established. Plan25k must exercise assembled consumers; K5, unsuccessful original-case receipts and performance limits remain unchanged.
