# Independent audit of efficiency alignment

## Decision and comparative assessment

**Architectural fitness: Revise. Behavioral/semantic adequacy: Revise in the narrow seed-discovery contract; otherwise not accepted beyond the inspected evidence. Overall decision: Revise.** This judgment is independent of the [original review](design_review_efficiency-alignment-codebase_2026-10-11.md). Its strongest diagnoses survive: interactive numerical edits enter complete structural ingress; stable preparation and canonical settlement are repeatedly established; actual shared writes and local exclusion serialize independent durable work; scalar index metadata determines publication grain; numerical callbacks and conditional stages repeatedly reshape or reconstruct stable inputs; narrow tests and generators inherit broader dependencies than their responsibility requires.

The audit changes several consequential conclusions. A second cache is not automatically a second semantic authority. A mutex does not mean the entire cubic root solve is serialized: only coefficient evaluation is under that lock. SurrealDB's configured pinned RocksDB backend registers `FOR UPDATE` reads for commit conflict detection; unchanged readers do not require exclusive access to one another. Public Rust SQL and invariant inspection routes exist, so absence of a Python facade cannot establish absence of the claimed capability. The engine's ownership, budget and drain wrappers are not shown to duplicate capabilities that DataFusion already discharges. The original G8 failure on that basis is withdrawn. The test fixture constructs the store; `Operations::from_store` does no I/O, so lazifying that constructor does not remove the fixture's store dependency.

Several corrections are also necessary before adopting remedies. Changed expressions require reference extraction even when names, parents and imports are unchanged. A stat tuple does not prove content identity. Structural matching plus small backward error does not establish numerical rank. Unconditional numeric-factor reuse can change nonlinear behavior. Final publication is too late for checks needed before native/provider consumers use a value. Tombstones cannot safely be deleted merely because a deadline passed. A complete acknowledgment accumulator does not yet exist just because individual append acknowledgments were checked. A result snapshot and a live stored handle have different retirement semantics.

The most important additional functional defect is bounded seed discovery presenting an incomplete search as absence ([A01](#a01)). Other additions concern repeated seed admission/decoding, IBEX waiters retaining active CPU allowance, growing surrogate snapshots, and eager shooting sensitivities ([A02](#a02)–[A05](#a05)). These are independent causes with distinct corrections; they do not justify a universal cache, scheduler or numerical rewrite.

The design has substantive strengths to preserve: physically checked bindings and quantity conversions; exact selected presence, absence and membership premises; semantic mathematical identities separated from attribution; original-space assessment independent of the solver's success; library-owned native iteration and factors; explicit attempt, lease, generation, close and seal meanings; lost-acknowledgment settlement; cancellation/drain ownership; decoded-row verification for scalar selection; and exact seed compatibility/provenance. These support a credible correction within the existing decomposition. They do not establish unexamined scientific adequacy or erase the execution-fit MUST gaps.

### Preparation lifetime and current eligibility

The missing interactive value intent is the clearest target failure. `ModelingPackage::with_declarations` reloads complete old source/documents, clears source attribution and persists a replacement. `SourceIndex::references` parses expression occurrences and structural requirements. `MathService::workspace` creates fresh compiler storage, and body descriptions copy complete selected premises. Direct lower/upper/fixed-state edits are separate operations. A consumed authored-value edit therefore performs work the existing numerical binding route can avoid, although no-op and out-of-closure edits can preserve selection/basis reuse.

The correction is explicit edit intent and an owned preparation lifetime. A direct numerical binding can be exposed before implementing a granular incremental compiler. Delta ingress should reparse changed reference-bearing content, and invalidate unchanged rows where scope membership, absence, shadowing or imports change lookup. Editing `x==a` to `x==b` is the counterexample to F02's proposed name/parent/kind/import-only rule. Preserve exact parent/CAS, no-op return and existing data/fit ownership; omission from a modeling delta does not authorize deletion of separately owned documents.

`declared_execution` and `resolve_case` really call preparation twice. Demand differs only when fixture names extend it, so two calls need not mean two distinct basis compilations or receiving qualifications. Compute final resolved demand once, or share a semantic-only specialization with executable completion. A longer-lived engagement must own exact selection/physical/provider/registry/validation context, current protection and settlement, with bounded idle retention. Renewal cannot resurrect an expired/released pin. Issued or uncertain effects keep their original owner, clock and drain obligations after new admissions stop.

For product descriptions, separate immutable mathematical content from current selection eligibility and reachability. Share one exact premise encoding initially. Narrowing per-body premises is optional and requires a completeness argument including absent names; mathematical content identity does not prove current source admissibility. An engagement may reduce repeated checks but never turn an old acknowledgment into new publication permission. Off-path or skipped settlement changes completion/effect semantics and requires an explicit bounded owner; it is not a consequence of merely caching bytes.

Observation also has several lifetimes. Scoped receiving file capture checks cancellation before each 64-KiB read and sealing, and qualification uses completion-owned cancellation/join. Unscoped executable/outer paths have different limits. Python outer checkout observation executes attached, while local replay startup uses a detached path. Current checkout state is not timeless executable provenance. Reuse an admitted artifact/image observation only under a real unchanged-premise owner; a stat tuple alone cannot exclude same-inode modification, timestamp ambiguity or wrong mapped-path association. Loader counters/maps do not establish arbitrary interposition absence. Changed supported image context must rekey/re-admit or refuse. Native-cache verification already occurs once per real operation and settled records have a conservative retirement path; widening that lifetime is an opportunity requiring a failure model, not proof of a current redundant check.

### Relational boundaries and the development loop

Validation context lifetime is an actual reuse boundary, but not simply a weak-map absence search. `EngineSession` and prepared plans hold a strong `ModelAssembly`; validation-only calls may lose it. Physical inventory uses a derived factory with its own model map, so retaining its preparation does not automatically preserve the deployment factory assembly used by validation. Carry exact immutable registry/implementation owners, and retain a bounded assembly where it saves reconstruction. Replacing owner identity with a claimed semantic hash needs complete function/settings/generation equivalence; pointer identity is conservative, not unsound. `Collection::finish` reconstructs `FieldCheckedBatch::from_parts` after safe generated completion and loses admission; preserve the established contract across the ownership-only operation. Raw ingress, FFI and IPC structural safety remain separate duties.

The public `pse_rules::invariants::run_invariants` compiles and executes a report through `EngineSession`; public `EngineSession::prepare_sql` resolves and checks SQL through the native session. Physical inventory actually prepares/executes grouped relational selections. This is sufficient contrary evidence to reject F24's claimed absent implementation route and the proposed wholesale commit-or-retire choice. Default unbound file-cache envelopes and an unread hashing setting can still be disproportionate. With a sufficiently large pool (including 4 GiB), the unused metadata/statistics envelopes reserve 66 MiB of accounted capacity; this is not a 66-MiB RSS measurement and smaller pools reserve less. Narrow that correction to eager unused reservation/configuration; keep syntax, ownership/generation, accounting, model-result/physical-cache and drain semantics with actual consumers. ## Additional audit findings

New findings are qualified by this review. Their priorities follow consequences; they are not an implementation sequence. Each correction is Proposed, each diagnosis Interface-checked. Supporting local inquiry IDs are provenance, not disposition IDs.

### <a id="a01"></a>A01 — A bounded pre-eligibility seed search reports exhaustive absence

**Cause/scenario (S06).** Public `ModelingSolvePreparation::with_stored_start(..., StoredStart::Latest)` consumes `Operations::latest_seed`. `canonical_execution.rs::result_seed_candidates` returns only 64 compatible, nonretired headers, ordered newest first, before terminal eligibility is checked. `latest_seed` skips nonterminal attempts after that cutoff and returns `None` without continuation. The caller reports “no retained seed matches preparation.”

**Consequence/principles.** A compatible newer multi-step run with at least 64 unsealed headers can mask an older sealed scientifically usable seed. Explicit Latest spuriously refuses available work; a cold-fallback consumer would lose reuse instead. AP-02/AP-04/AP-05/AP-07, DP-02/DP-08/DP-19, G2/G5, PS-11: incomplete discovery is represented as absence. No false successful numerical result is alleged. Priority Medium functional correctness.

**Evidence/limits.** The principal read the query, selector and public start consumer; inquiry B02 supplies the complete counterexample. This global-search example does not transfer to exact-predecessor studies, which constrain attempt. No fixture was executed.

**Correction/preservation.** Page in deterministic order under the original clock/cancellation until an eligible seed or exhausted absence, or expose eligible headers only at terminal admission. Distinguish incomplete discovery from absence. Preserve exact compatibility, retirement, scientific seed permission and corruption/infrastructure errors; never admit unsealed prefixes.

**Verification.** Retain one older usable sealed seed; append 64 newer compatible unsealed headers. Latest must select the older seed or explicitly report incomplete discovery. After terminal admission it selects the newest eligible occurrence. This is a focused functional control, not a performance campaign. Scientific fixture helpers must establish actual permission.

### <a id="a02"></a>A02 — Seed selection discards its admitted object and forces repeated complete reopen/decode

**Cause/scenario (S06).** `durable.rs::latest_seed` calls `seed` but returns only `SolutionId`; `run.rs::with_stored_start` calls `seed` again. `seed_document` itself separately opens result protection and calls `record`, which opens completion and enumerates seed inventory. `prediction` repeats the full seed-document decoding even for a prediction-only consumer.

**Consequence/principles.** Durable continuation/Latest start repeatedly opens protection, reads inventory/payload and decodes substantial warm state on the critical path; temporaries can overlap. AP-07, DP-10, H12/H16/H19, PS-11. Priority Medium. This survives merely optimizing F12's post-seal finish tail.

**Evidence/limits.** Principal read `Operations::{seed_document,seed,prediction,latest_seed}` and start consumer; inquiry B01 traces candidate/prediction use. Same-process ephemeral handoff is contrary evidence that store reread is inevitable. Open/decode counts and byte magnitude were not measured.

**Correction/preservation.** Return/pass one owned admitted seed with header, exact warm payload, optional prediction, completion permission and explicit snapshot/live lifetime. A completion-only read avoids unrelated inventory. Bound reuse by actual attempt/seed/compatibility/ownership; immutable bytes do not confer current stored permission. Preserve exact bits and recorded start lineage.

**Verification.** Count one selected seed's opens/decodes through discovery/start/prediction and compare identical starts/provenance. Expiry/retirement before independent subsequent use must follow the declared live-pin or snapshot contract. No new general seed cache is required.

### <a id="a03"></a>A03 — IBEX exclusion waiters keep active compute capacity while sleeping

**Cause/scenario (S07).** Native runtime work installs `execution::compute_scoped` with `ComputeOwner`. Root-isolation point/proof entrypoints loop on the process-global `IBEX.try_lock`, sleeping and checking cancellation/deadline, without the pause/reacquire behavior used by HiGHS/Uno/PETSc exclusion paths. Concurrent proof-heavy solves can hold several CPU allowances behind one library entry.

**Consequence/principles.** Unrelated eligible compute waits while admitted owners are idle behind exclusion. AP-07, DP-20, H21–H23. Priority Medium for concurrent proof work. This is not a claim that IBEX exclusion is unnecessary, that a deadlock exists, or that throughput loss was measured.

**Evidence/limits.** Principal read wait bodies, `ComputeAdmission` and runtime owner; inquiry C-A01 covers all enumerated proof entrypoints and contrary adapter behavior. Caller-team quiescence across all nested proof uses was not established.

**Correction/preservation.** Return active CPU allowance only for a quiescent queued operation, then reacquire under the same original clock/stop before native entry. Retain memory/native safety/drain ownership. If a native team still works while one callback waits, pausing its whole allowance is incorrect; use request-local scheduling or a narrower owner. This caller condition precedes a blanket pause insertion.

**Verification.** Hold IBEX, queue an isolated quiescent proof owner, and admit unrelated compute; observe reacquisition before library entry and cancellation under the original deadline. Keep poison refusal, deterministic proof results and actual foreign drain. A source trace proving all relevant callers already release capacity would narrow the finding.

### <a id="a04"></a>A04 — Surrogate advancement automatically copies a growing full library snapshot

**Cause/scenario (S07).** `pse-math/src/surrogate.rs::RetainedSurrogate` Advance sends `(worker.state.clone(), proposal)` after each actual egobox advancement, while the library worker retains its original state. Pinned egobox-ego 0.38.1 state Clone includes owned historical samples. The runtime phase/proposal/work/termination consumer does not need that entire history every step.

**Consequence/principles.** Long campaigns recopy growing history each iteration; constant-batch growth yields quadratic cumulative sample-copy traffic, with worker/old/new snapshots temporarily live. AP-07, DP-10/DP-20, H6/H16/H17/H26. Priority Medium for large histories. No claim that full GP covariance/factors are cloned or that this dominates acquisition.

**Evidence/limits.** Principal read worker reply/state/termination and consumer shape; inquiry C-A02 traced runtime consumers and pinned owned fields. The public `state()` API and tests are real snapshot consumers; external Rust consumers were not enumerated.

**Correction/preservation.** Keep actual solver state in the library worker. Return compact progress/termination/proposal/work by default; full snapshots remain explicit demand with accounted ownership/API migration. Preserve egobox history, randomness, clustering/hyperparameters, proposal original screening, limits/cancel/drain. Do not implement a reduced second solver state.

**Verification.** Compare identical seeded proposal/termination/work sequences, preserve explicit snapshot behavior, and show no growing automatic history clone on the runtime path. Measure bytes only if priority needs settling; current copying is source-established.

### <a id="a05"></a>A05 — Shooting value-only trials cannot avoid forward variational integration

**Cause/scenario (S07).** `workflow/shooting.rs::ShootingOracle::evaluate` has a bit-exact point cache but no order demand. For a new nonzero-variable point with rows or terminal outputs it integrates windows with Forward sensitivity and builds Jacobian/gradient state. Objective/constraints and derivative callbacks all call this method, so a value-only rejected trial pays derivative work.

**Consequence/principles.** Dynamics optimization with many controls/anchors/state equations can perform unconsumed variational integration. AP-07, DP-10, H6/H12/H16; PS-07/PS-11 constrain correction. Priority Medium/conditional. This is a nested integration issue distinct from F16's algebraic layout.

**Evidence/limits.** Principal read evaluate and objective/constraints/gradient/Jacobian callbacks; inquiry C-A03 traced windows/integration. Actual solver request sequences and wasted-work share are unmeasured. Repeated-point eager reuse is an existing strength.

**Correction/preservation.** Express requested/achieved order and permit primal-only trials, upgrading when derivatives are actually requested under the same point/source/scope. Backend policy may deliberately choose eager amortization where every point needs derivatives; establish that tradeoff rather than force laziness. Reports are not retained native checkpoints. Preserve scheduled events, anchors/continuity, physical normalization, derivative axes/source, finite consumers, original checks and failure truthfulness.

**Verification.** Trace actual value/derivative callback sequences and sensitivity-enabled integrations for rejected trials. Compare values and derivatives at identical points, including discontinuities/cancellation. If all normal points need derivatives and upgrades cost more, narrow the requirement to explicit supported demand/policy rather than claim a speedup.
