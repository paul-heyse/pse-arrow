# Pipeline diagnostics and offline review artifacts

The existing diagnostic architecture is a useful foundation for deeper assessment after Plan46. It already separates scientific permission from observation; supplies operation, producer and native-attempt identities; retains coherent source events and explicit loss; offers selected detail, CPU/timeline capture, source maps and invocation comparisons; and projects retained evidence into Arrow. Its strongest property is that uncertainty remains visible rather than becoming fabricated work, convergence or causal attribution.

**Overall decision: Revise for the requested target.** These components do not yet form a self-contained offline review product or a complete targeted CLI workflow. The decisive gaps are a selected artifact closure with an independent sealing operation (F01), frozen interpretation that does not reinterpret recorded clocks and relationships through the reader's live environment (F02), and CLI composition of the existing selection, analysis and export capabilities (F03). These are related but different obligations. They warrant completing existing owners, not presuming that another observability platform, diagnostic database, generic analysis language or reproduction system is needed.

**Behavioral/semantic adequacy:** the examined live and selected-evidence contracts have substantial source-backed strengths; the full offline target is inadequate. **Architectural fitness:** violated for the combined requested capability because consumers must reconstruct the missing artifact and workflow meanings. Acceptance of those existing components at their limited scope would not accept the enclosing target. No numerical experiment or new performance measurement is claimed by this review.

## 1. Scope, target and evidence

This independent design-tier, target-purpose review applies Core 3.4, its efficiency heuristics 1.0, Process Simulator 1.5 and the pse-arrow binding. It examines HEAD `e9d24396cb8394b8a15c249abd52cd3390e65cd4` with the current dirty architecture, proposed ADR and Plan46 documents preserved as supplied design. Those documents describe proposed or recorded work; their presence and status are not acceptance criteria. The review judges the functional target and selected standard, then records rule impacts in §11.

The target has three coupled outcomes:

1. Useful deeper causal assessment of the end-to-end simulation/numerical pipeline, while running and after completion, including the behavior introduced or exposed by Plan46.
2. A targeted CLI workflow that discovers actual work, selects the relevant evidence, inspects it and develops source-linked explanations and next observations.
3. A self-contained, externally shareable **offline review pack** containing selected model/run inputs and settings, diagnostic raw data and profiles, analysis and selected results. This is review of recorded evidence. It does not promise runnable reproduction, native continuation, environment reconstruction or build/provenance attestation.

Ordinary physical engineering accuracy remains **0.001 where selected by the existing scientific policy**. Diagnostic thresholds, scaled/native norms, derivative/backward-error tolerances, rank thresholds and KKT criteria retain separate meanings. Environment/provenance proof is permanently eliminated. No full wheel campaign, compulsory profiler stack, new long benchmark campaign or blanket numerical-default change is proposed.

Modes considered are square/root simulation, NLP/coefficient/conic/discrete optimization, sensitivities and responses, fitting/shooting, dynamic integration/adjoints, studies, recycles and horizons, plus preparation and result access. The review includes ephemeral, durable, managed-worker and native-child relationships where their evidence crosses the boundary. It does not requalify every numerical method or unit/property model. Concurrent operations, incomplete captures, retries, skipped or unavailable requested products, growing studies/trajectories, and a process that has exited are material cases.

Decisive source inspection covered `pse-diagnostics::observation` and `inspection`, runtime observation/inspection/invocation/capture owners, workflow diagnostic operations/source maps/replay/result export/study selection, native observation and selected Uno callbacks, evaluator observation, Python diagnostic/run/result interfaces, and the actual diagnostic/trace-query CLI implementations. Blueprint §23.1, §15, §19.2 and §24.2 and relevant proposed ADRs/Plan46 packets supplied intended contracts and qualification boundaries. Source establishes an implemented route, not its assembled runtime qualification. Current library skills were used as routing context; stale capability maps and library-utilization catalogs were not used.

The coordinator reports no intervening diff in `crates`, `python`, `scripts`, `Cargo.toml` or `pyproject.toml`; the inspected production source basis remains `e9d24396c`. The desired direction is scientific/producer owners → copied evidence → pure interpretation and library queries → CLI/review-pack presentation. Pack composition references scientific results without granting scientific use. It may read a sealed result selection through its existing protected export, but its later external reader must not depend on the store, a native handle or a live checkpoint directory.

Local testability is already plausible: low-level producers and interpreters take copied typed inputs, selected-detail writers have explicit state, and scientific export has a separate owner. Preserve this separation. A pack test should not need a solver merely to establish path closure, temporal selection or partiality. A short actual run is appropriate only for the propagation boundary it exercises.

## 3. Meaning, contracts and preservation constraints

The model of observed execution is substantially adequate for its current purpose. `CoherentRecord` carries source producer/generation/sequence/family/time/phase/coordinates/norm and fields with units and availability. Current and recent evidence are independent; source sequence is not durable append order. `InspectionDocument` and `Statement` carry facts, hypotheses or limitations with evidence references and next observations. `InvocationEvidenceDocument` distinguishes original qualification, terminal/drain, response readiness and requested-product availability. A missing endpoint is not zero time or failed science by inference.

The missing domain distinction is **live observation versus frozen recorded review**. A copied checkpoint can be live input, a retained prefix, or a pack member. That distinction changes the valid clock, related-artifact resolution, completeness and retention interpretation; its absence cannot be repaired by merely copying files into a directory.

| Contract | Existing strength / required target boundary |
|---|---|
| Exact identity | Diagnostic operation/producer locators remain separate from semantic and canonical run/attempt identities. Cross-process links retain exact targets; absent parents never rebound to a convenient root. Pack members need explicit links to those original identities. |
| Temporal scope | Live reads legitimately use a recent lookback. Frozen review must use a recorded anchor or an explicitly selected recorded interval; it cannot use the external reader's current monotonic clock. Clock alignment can remain unknown. |
| Coverage | Source-issued, observed/offered, queued, acknowledged, sampled-out, dropped and unacknowledged populations are distinct. Pack sealing does not turn sampled or missing evidence into complete capture. |
| Availability | Disabled, not requested, not supplied, refused, nonfinite, overflow, failed and lost evidence retain their source meaning. An unavailable profile or physical pullback is not zero work. |
| Numerical progress | Native accepted/trial/checkpoint semantics and native/scaled norms are not original physical convergence. Static branch eligibility is not an executed branch. Original assessment remains independent. |
| Resource ownership | Readers and captures retain copied data, not solver handles or cancellation leases. A stopped exporter does not stop science; native charges end only at actual drain. |
| Completion | Diagnostic pack completion means its selected artifact operation ended with declared membership and coverage. It is separate from capture completeness, scientific completion and selected-result usability. A failed scientific run can have a complete review pack. |
| Interchange | Raw/versioned documents remain authority. Arrow/SQL/rendered reports are derived views with explicit loss. Unknown formats refuse or remain explicitly unavailable; they are not silently interpreted under new defaults. |
| Analysis | Findings cite actual events/populations and policy. Correlation, work differences and descriptive inclusive means do not establish a causal performance winner. Next observations discriminate hypotheses and do not automatically execute science. |

### Physical semantics and well-posedness

| Quantity/model element | Dimension/unit and basis | Convention/validity | Authority and diagnostic obligation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Original variables, residuals, bounds, balances and results | Original physical units, material basis and reference state | Original model/provider envelopes; selected engineering accuracy | Physical/model/numerical-policy owners. Pack must carry the selected interpretation and assessment, not just native scalars or solver indices. |
| Native residual, direction, objective, feasibility/gap | Native/scaled or original as actually supplied | Field-specific coordinate mapping and norm; some units explicitly unspecified | Performing adapter/oracle owner. Preserve unavailability; do not normalize unlike solvers into one convergence score. |
| Dynamic output/step/checkpoint | Physical model time versus native step, replay and checkpoint counts | Accepted output, native return, adjoint replay and incomplete trajectory differ | Dynamic/workflow owner. Requested output count is not executed vector work or total internal steps. |
| Timing and resource facts | Wall seconds/nanoseconds, process/thread CPU, RSS, reservations, factor/workspace extents | Inclusive/shared and live/lifetime scopes remain explicit | Performing owner/capture source. No subtraction or summation invents exclusive operation CPU, wait causes or private peak memory. |
| Diagnostic thresholds | Source norm/coordinates or explicitly dimensionless ratios | Caller-selected evidence sufficiency and classification policy | Diagnostic interpretation owner, never physical acceptance or solver stopping policy. |

This subsystem does not formulate a new physical problem or change variable roles, degree-of-freedom analysis, incidence decomposition or solver-class admission. Those remain model/compiler/analysis responsibilities. Review artifacts must preserve their model-named conclusions and original identities when selected; they must not equate structural refusal with numerical nonconvergence. Their current detailed all-model adequacy was not re-audited here.

### Observation overhead and execution fit

Ordinary collection already uses prepared producer facts, bounded copied state and separate selected detail. Full histories and thread clocks are explicit choices; collection-off is a genuine absence. Source detail uses a nonblocking finite writer and reports loss rather than blocking callbacks. These choices should be preserved. A universal per-row event, full source payload on every callback, synchronous query/export in a native hook, or unconditional additional numerical evaluation would be a regression.

The target justifies some auxiliary work because explanatory evidence and sharing are concrete functional requirements. Apply selection before copying/decoding; use compact identities and hydrate selected sources later; stream raw/IPC files at export instead of simultaneously holding all replay, detail, profiles and scientific payloads. Whole-pack processing may be necessary to close membership, but repeated scans for each consumer are not. Do not universally enlarge hub retention to implement offline review. Give explicit recording/export its own finite lifetime and independent partiality.

No observer overhead number or identical deadline-limited trajectory is established. Short off/on/selected-detail controls of actual work and scientific outputs are the appropriate follow-up where a new observer is proposed. Numeric overhead claims require measurements; this review does not introduce a benchmark SLA.

## 7. Findings and corrective contracts

### F01 — Individual evidence exports do not define a self-contained review closure

**Principles/gates:** AP-01–AP-05, AP-07; DP-08, DP-19, DP-21, DP-24; G2/G3/G5/G7/G9. **Scenarios:** S03–S05/S08. **Consequence priority:** high for the requested external sharing capability.

**Diagnosis — Implemented / source-inspected:** result IPC export (`workflow/connected_results.rs::ResultReader::export_ipc`) preserves registry schema and original run/attempt/manifest/terminal lineage with an incomplete-file boundary. It uses Arrow **stream-format** `StreamWriter`, then finish/flush/sync and a no-overwrite final hard link; it is not a FileWriter/footer-based export. ReplayDocument v2 exports substantial authored/physical/provider source context, constructors/settings/starts/native attempts and coverage, but expressly includes runtime/native/build reconstruction premises and can refuse portable case/analysis export for caller-supplied implicit capabilities. Diagnostic source JSONL, capture documents and Arrow views are additional valid pieces. Managed diagnostic readback is explicitly bounded current-attempt selection rather than all historical retries. The bounded search covered `workflow/replay.rs::{ReplayDocument,ScientificReplay::document_with_limit}`, `workflow/connected_results.rs::export_ipc`, `capture/{detail,evidence}.rs`, `workflow/study_diagnostics.rs`, `python/pse/{diagnostics,_runs,_workflow,_artifacts}.py` and the actual trace adapter. No examined API or CLI composes these into one declared externally readable selection with its membership, required interpretation dependencies, missing members and independent seal. This is a bounded negative claim over the listed owners and CLI, not a claim that no existing files can be shared or that existing exports lack useful context.

**Trigger and consequence:** an engineer exports result IPC and a capture directory, or serializes ReplayDocument and copies profile files. The external reviewer must discover which model/settings/starts/attempts, source maps, coverage documents, analysis policy and result subset belong together; resolve machine-local paths; and decide whether absent evidence is unrequested, lost or merely omitted. A valid review can also be refused because reconstructible science needs an unavailable factory or implicit capability even though recorded inputs and observations suffice to review the failure. Merely archiving the directory does not close these meanings.

**Correction — Proposed:** define one selected offline-review operation in the existing workflow/artifact/export composition boundary. It consumes exact retained run/attempt/invocation or diagnostic-operation selections, requested model/input/settings context, selected raw/profiles/analyses and selected result relations/ranges. It emits ordinary documented files plus a versioned index describing original identities, member roles, relative references, temporal scope, interpretation/schema versions, independent availability/coverage and selection completeness. Keep raw data intact; translated local paths belong in the derived index and never silently mutate source records. Results retain their original qualification and partiality. The pack can be complete for a failed run or an explicitly partial evidence selection; complete must describe the declared selection, not invent complete capture.

Required explanatory dependencies must be present or explicitly unavailable under the requested review scope. Optional member failure does not invalidate independent recorded science. A fully self-contained claim cannot be made while required explanatory context remains only a local-store path. Native executable factories and build witnesses are not prerequisites for reviewing recorded evidence. Reuse Replay/source exporters where their scope fits; preserve Replay's stronger reconstruction contract rather than weakening it to solve a different problem.

Seal the pack only after its owned exports finish/drain and the selected immutable membership is fixed; retain readable incomplete output on failure. This needs a simple manifest/private-file publication argument for a local writer, not a new distributed transaction service, signature/provenance framework or diagnostic database. Stream/select source and result material rather than eagerly cloning the entire runtime or every study member.

**Preserve/challenge:** a valid failed run with one missing optional CPU profile must remain shareable; a selected complete result with missing required unit/reference context must not be advertised self-contained. A study with historical retries must distinguish selected-current scope from selected-all-retained-attempt scope rather than using an older retry as the current attempt. External consumers must be able to review a pack without native libraries or source-store access.

**Verification — Proposed:** move the produced pack to a fresh path, make original source paths/store/checkpoint directories unavailable to the reader, and inspect membership, raw evidence, model/settings and selected results using only pack contents. Cover failed/partial runs, an unavailable executable capability, an intentionally unrequested profile, missing required context, changed original files after selection, duplicate/conflicting identities, output exhaustion and interruption before seal. Assert that failed export cannot produce a complete seal and that no scientific outcome changes. A short actual managed/native-child anchor can test propagation; path closure and sealing logic should also be testable with copied fixtures.

<a id="f02"></a>
### F02 — Live checkpoint interpretation is not frozen recorded-evidence interpretation

**Principles/gates:** AP-02/AP-04–AP-06; DP-02/DP-08/DP-18/DP-19/DP-21; G2/G4/G6/G9. **Scenarios:** S04/S07/S08. **Consequence priority:** high, because it changes what an external reviewer sees.

**Diagnosis — Implemented / source-inspected:** `crates/pse-runtime/src/inspection.rs::inspect_image` calls `ClockMapping::observation_now`, which projects the reader's current Linux CLOCK_MONOTONIC into the producer observation clock. `crates/pse-diagnostics/src/inspection.rs::select_window` then trims detail and producer recent records against that current lookback. Related cross-process observations are opened from neighbouring process-checkpoint filenames. `crates/pse-runtime/src/observation_target.rs::ClockMapping` is explicitly a same-host operational mapping; it is not a portable time origin. Summary expiry marks retained copied facts stale; it does not delete all current/cumulative facts. This is intentional live-inspection behavior and must not be misreported as universal data disappearance.

**Trigger and consequence:** retain a checkpoint containing recent coherent records, read it long after the completed operation, or move it to another host/boot. The same selected lookback can discard recorded recent evidence or reinterpret freshness using the new clock. Related lookup can observe a different local file realization, or simply fail despite the relevant member being included elsewhere in an archive. Thus copying the current checkpoint and rerunning live inspect cannot promise stable offline analysis. It can still expose useful current/cumulative facts; that narrower capability does not solve the temporal/relationship problem.

**Correction — Proposed:** add an explicit frozen-reader operation over recorded artifacts. Its input includes the recorded selection/reference endpoint or a caller-selected interval in the source clock and an immutable supplied member resolver. It never projects from the reader's current monotonic clock, discovers live neighbours, attaches processes or starts collection. It preserves the original recorded freshness/coverage and separately reports what the recorded image can establish. Cross-process alignment uses the mappings actually captured with their uncertainty; unknown alignment remains unknown. Absolute event order across unknown clocks is not invented.

Retain existing live inspection and expiry semantics for live use. A frozen exported record does not renew live summary eligibility and is not a recovered current process. One interpreter may share pure helpers with the live path, but the clock and resolution contract must be explicit at the boundary. This correction requires no host/build attestation: portability is obtained by using recorded times and supplied evidence, not proving the reader environment.

**Preserve/challenge:** partial/hard-death prefixes stay partial; freezing cannot create final drain or missing history. A deliberately selected recent interval on a recorded capture should remain selectable, while no interval may manufacture records preceding capture activation. Supplied related members may be missing; the resolver must report that loss without substituting another run or reading arbitrary live files.

**Verification — Proposed:** analyze the same immutable artifact at different reader times and after relocation and obtain the same recorded rows, coverage and evidence references for the same selected recorded interval. Exercise incompatible/missing cross-process clocks, absent members, known loss, stale-as-recorded images, an exited process and a malicious/erroneous local sibling file. Assert no store/native/service access. Live-mode tests retain their original current-time expiry/lookback behavior. The coordinator's bounded passive artifact probe in §10 confirms this distinction without a new simulation; it does not qualify the proposed frozen reader.

### F03 — The CLI exposes fragments rather than the targeted evidence-review operation

**Principles/gates:** AP-02/AP-03/AP-05/AP-06; DP-08/DP-16/DP-21/DP-24; G7/G9. **Scenarios:** S01/S02/S06/S09. **Consequence priority:** medium; it prevents the requested operator workflow and repeatedly transfers composition knowledge to callers.

**Diagnosis — Implemented / source-inspected:** `python/pse/diagnostics.py::main` actually defines `list`, `inspect`, `capture`, `capture-launch` and `read-detail`. Inspect accepts an explicit numerical-history policy; capture selects CPU or timeline; capture-launch gates an owned command behind profiler enablement; read-detail pages raw source records. `scripts/diagnostic_trace.py` actually selects finite `timeline`, `numerical` and `coverage` SQL. The numerical SQL is a useful narrow NLP-shaped projection, not a general analysis of every existing native family. The API additionally exposes preconstruction `Diagnostics.collect(DetailRequest)`, static source maps, joined invocation evidence/explanation/comparison and Arrow projections. Result/context export has separate owners. The CLI does not compose these APIs into selected source-detail recording, frozen invocation/physical-context analysis and offline-pack export/readback.

**Trigger and consequence:** an analyst starts with the CLI, needs complete selected evaluator/native events, compares two completed invocations, or follows a diagnostic source reference into model/run context and selected results. They must write Python/Rust orchestration, understand synchronous construction selection and managed budget propagation, serialize retained objects, and manually join policies/coverage/files. Different callers can make different interpretations of the same target operation. This is not evidence that the existing CLI is unusable; its current bounded passive/capture subset remains valuable.

**Correction — Proposed:** extend the current CLI as a thin composition of owned operations, with explicit requests and typed results. The functional route is discover → exact inspect → select needed future recording or explicit diagnostic work → read/compare retained evidence → export/frozen-review pack. Preserve current commands where their meaning fits; names/number of commands are implementation choices. Retained-input analysis must work from files/pack members without a RunResult or live Runtime. Selection should expose the actual families, interval, sampling/complete requirement, source/managed allowances and activation acknowledgement. Arbitrary launched commands can promise selected source detail only if they use a cooperating activation boundary; otherwise report that capability unavailable rather than inventing pre-start coverage.

Use the existing long-form Arrow field projection for generic selected record queries and existing interpretation/comparison functions for domain statements. Keep Trace Processor's three finite views as optional derived tools; do not grow a bespoke query engine or duplicate native-family semantics in Python/SQL. Explicit new scientific diagnostics require their own existing numerical operation/deadline and must be distinguishable from passive analysis. Render facts, hypotheses, limits, source references and optional next observations consistently.

**Preserve/challenge:** ordinary listing/inspection must remain available without scientific services; lack of perf/Trace Processor must not prevent raw/detail/input/result review. Recording must acknowledge before constructing the work it claims to cover, while attach-after-start remains a partial suffix. A command/capture error must preserve both independent outcomes and drain owned resources without changing unrelated science.

**Verification — Proposed:** a CLI-only short journey selects a concrete question, requests the relevant future detail under an acknowledged cooperating route, inspects exact records and coverage, compares retained invocations, exports selected context/results, then opens the relocated pack without original paths/services. Include disabled/refused/unsupported family, interrupted recording, no native handle, missing optional profiler, managed child loss and failed science. Tests should assert typed semantics and source linkage, not particular wording or an invented command count.

### Relationships and corrective order

F01 is artifact membership/publication; F02 is the meaning of reading frozen evidence; F03 is the operator composition surface. Fixing F02 alone does not produce a shareable closure. Copying files under F01 without F02 still reinterprets time and links. Adding CLI flags without either merely automates manual ambiguity.

Consequence priority puts external fidelity and self-containment first. Prerequisite order starts with the frozen-read and selection/completeness meanings, then composes and seals existing exports, then exposes those same operations through the CLI. This is a contract dependency, not a staffing schedule. Narrow native/analysis facts can proceed independently when they answer a concrete selected question; they should not wait for a universal metric inventory or new platform.
