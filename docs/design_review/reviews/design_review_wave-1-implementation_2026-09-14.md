## 5. Representative journeys

**Ordinary extension:** A new relation starts with one registry declaration and document mapping where needed. Builder checks and generators derive fields, rows, accessors, schemas and documentation. A new invariant also needs a real rule predicate plus valid/violating examples. Generated volume is not semantic duplication. A new math operator legitimately needs an operation contract and algorithm; a tag without lowering or auxiliary behavior must remain unsupported.

**Meaningful change:** Full rename compares original source bytes and actual base rows, resolves references, renders edits, reparses and preserves target/binding identities. `amend_rename_sources` permits an explicit new case model-revision binding only after renewing that proof. The current engine log records the full atomic rename test passing. The stronger changed-input Driver-versus-clean scenario remains an acceptance obligation; successful unchanged-input memo tests do not establish it.

**Alternate representation:** Publication admits rows, canonicalizes them, finishes IPC/Parquet, decodes them and compares full normalized preimages (`publish.rs:204–249`). Restore repeats membership, actual content and stage admission before final identity checks (`open.rs:317–354`). Direct Model restore still only verifies source artifact integrity/span bounds, not equality to its row projection (F1).

**Interruption:** Immutable orphan objects may remain after a failure; they do not move the visible ref. Local locking/conditional replacement prevents stale writers from silently overwriting a newer target. Cancellation before rename preserves the old ref; the local protocol explicitly distinguishes a post-rename durability failure. Separately, `Driver::run` can lose terminal attempt attribution on `?` exits (F3). Atomic visibility and recorded failure are different obligations.

Owner: compiler/catalog maintainers; revisit before terminal attempt implementation and when diagnostic volume exceeds the admitted envelope.
