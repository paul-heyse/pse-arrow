## Material net changes

**F04 — Correct or adjust.** The original recommendation risked promoting runtime composition into proof of duplicated formulation authority. `modeling/cases.rs::lower_closure_requirements` already consumes specialized closure requirements, compiler annotations, endpoint/context and original lineage. Contextual scales and effective policy legitimately arrive late. Retain the requirement that process consumers use prepared meaning, but leave the inherited duplication diagnosis unresolved unless a concrete repeated semantic decision or divergent consumer can be demonstrated. Omit a presumed compiler migration as a prerequisite. The DA-006 primary account is neither closed nor rewritten by this editorial correction.

**F03 — Improve, together with F06.** `completion.rs::derive_permission` already combines aggregate scientific permission with local validity, while `product_permission` selects by quantity and `step_permission` supplies a separate scope. Broader product families need exact step/member/reference-point identity and analysis assumptions, not just another quantity in a global list. Preserve independent successful-step evidence without upgrading a failed sibling or whole-request coverage. Structural analysis has no physical candidate to qualify: applying a universal successful-state predicate would recreate F06 through the permission mechanism. Point-derived products still require their actual point's scientific permission. These are extension-contract risks; no current multi-member corruption was reproduced.

**Packet A — Improve.** The sourcing chapter's smallest-delivery table and first complete example explicitly require one selected source → Rust extraction/reconciliation → typed admission → guarded SurrealDB publication → ordinary case → reopened readback demonstration. Reuse existing adapters; implement new machinery only where missing. The original conditional-new-import wording must not make that requested demonstration optional. No additional scientific family or universal catalog is introduced.

**F01, F05 and gates — Improve.** Preserve initialization reports on success as well as failure; the successful wrapper also selects only the last result. Apply complete dependencies to each consumed prepared artifact without hashing every late submission value into symbolic structure. Keep F06's outcome-ownership contradiction under G2/AP-04; it does not establish a PS-G2 structural-admission defect. Route-specific well-posedness qualification remains unestablished here.

## Combined target coverage and remaining decisions

The package's Model/Specification/Query/MethodRequest/Study/Solution separation is retained through §2, §3 and F05/F06. Material/state/basis/datum, physical storage, transfer and operating-cycle meaning remains in §3, S01/S07/S09 and P2–P5; physical time and shared-parameter meaning remains in S08 and P6. Native/external substitution is assessed by complete demanded behavior, derivatives and effects in S10/§8. Scientific sourcing remains a real first-delivery path rather than an asset-count exercise. Full released/configuration coverage remains P0/P7 work rather than a claim from the twelve acceptance witnesses or 55 notebook families.

The audit found no basis for an additional universal scientific framework or a replacement roadmap. Its additional recommendations refine the existing operations: explicit task-to-route admission, task-appropriate product prerequisites, scoped product identity and artifact-granular preparation dependencies. They do not manufacture new diagnosis IDs for already documented target obligations.

The combined correction prevents two interactions that the original wording left exposed: F03 must not undo F06 by requiring every product to have a successful physical state; F04 must not become a compiler-refactor barrier to C while A–D can consume existing prepared contracts. F01's full report and F02's producer authority remain higher-consequence implementation corrections than catalog wording, although catalog fixes precede dependent public-contract freezing.

Consequential choices remain visible: exact ordinary records and sources, text/declaration editing policy, finite matched reference configurations, the actually exposed product scopes, and supported physical/numerical contracts of later dynamics and substitutions. Selected-case evidence settles those choices. They are not silently decided by library availability or this review's retained architecture.
