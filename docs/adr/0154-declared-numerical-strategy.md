# ADR-0154: Compose declared numerical strategies under original permission

## Context

The integrated solve reviews identify duplicated numerical policy, erased failures and missing composed retention. The existing no-fallback clauses prohibit lawful declared recovery along with unsafe backend substitution.

## Scope

Amend the numerical recovery clauses of ADR-0083/0152 through blueprint §§18.6–18.8; retain their other mathematics and contextual-routing decisions. Establish generated strategy and recorded interpretation boundaries in §19.3/§20.5. ADR acceptance remains a separate decision-PR operation.

## Drivers

Preserve original scientific permission, explicit applicability, bounded lifetime and change locality while integrating the complete Plan 25m target. Mechanism substitution and pure policy testing must stay within their responsible owners.

## Options

Keeping private workflow retry loops duplicates meanings and requires expensive testing. A monolithic solver or new workflow engine would absorb scientific policy. Select shared domain strategy in the existing crates, with class-specific library operations and injected test seams.

## Outcome

Use a declared strategy resolved purely from original/derived problem facts, capabilities, starts, available products and finite allowances. Inject execution and original assessment into the effectful driver. Capability incompatibility and numerical trajectory history remain different inputs. Explicit selection never changes backend unless named alternatives permit it. Numerical failures can take an admitted transition; invalid required contracts, task exhaustion, cancellation, infrastructure and panic end the task. Optional mechanism refusal may preserve the base route. One enclosing ExecutionScope spans preparation, all rungs, nested work and final assessment; each work unit is charged once. Attempt abandonment is distinct from cancellation. Original completion, auxiliary use and artifact retention are separate permissions. NoPriorStart excludes inherited proposals; Explicit never silently substitutes another entry seed; PreviousAccepted requires result permission. Scientific statistical/control/time-step/durable policies retain their owners. Generated traces record preparation, constant/rejected/native outcomes, actual profile, cause, scope, start, accuracy, work and permission. Preserving migrations retain historical bytes.

### Automatic composition and assessment amendment

Plan 25n makes automatic composition the current-version Auto default. One request owns the
original target/guarantees, explicit backend/profile/mechanism constraints, branch/start rules and
finite allowances. The pure resolver chooses one Prepare, Execute, Assess, Finish or Stop decision
from producer-issued Ready/Conditional/Unavailable/Incompatible capabilities, actual products and
observations. The existing driver binds effects; no new workflow engine or numerical iteration is
introduced. Explicit declarations use the same binder. Scientific workflows retain their own loops.

Operation prerequisites, produced evidence and before-use demands replace strategy-global accuracy
admission. Original assessment returns its scientific product, conclusion, typed cause, candidate
permission, independent artifact retention and disjoint work; native termination is separate.
Original correction binds the frozen target and excludes recursive selection of the auxiliary
family being corrected. Approximate curvature on that original target requires no redundant solve
when original assessment already establishes its requested guarantees. Unestablished global,
infeasibility, dual or branch guarantees receive refusal, not invented correspondence.

Hard counter limits require pre-operation hooks or complete conservative reservations covering
native final validation and original assessment. Actual unsuccessful work is charged exactly once.
Unknown measured work remains unknown; a complete reservation retains its full allowance when it
cannot be reconciled. Work without a pre-operation admission hook or complete conservative bound cannot satisfy a hard cap. Cancellation, required
contract/resource/infrastructure failure and panic remain terminal. Default replacement-start
origins are empty; only the admitted request grants them. Same-entry profile recovery is distinct
from substitution of its candidate. NoPriorStart forbids inherited starts, not immutable programs.

### Consequences

Migrate producers and consumers together and remove replaced paths after targeted acceptance. Plan 25m retains its original packet/finding evidence; Plan 25n owns the automatic amendment and AF-01–AF-08. No parallel ledger is added.

### Compensating controls

Typed admission/refusal, finite work, actual native contract tests and independent original assessment constrain integration. No license restriction or hypothetical benchmark ranking excludes a selected library.

### Confirmation

**Proposed:** scoped decision review precedes changed-contract implementation. Prior source reviews establish the library/design basis, not acceptance or product qualification. Named checks are planned, not executed claims.

## Pros and cons

The target makes consequential distinctions explicit and permits isolated policy testing, at the cost of coordinated producer/consumer migration and foreign lifetime contracts.
