# ADR-0155: Prepare derived families with original reconstruction and consumed accuracy

## Context

Derived systems and selected-root reuse need explicit correspondence to authored science. Numerical-source exactness and residual convergence do not establish forward or nested derivative accuracy.

## Scope

Amend prepared-family and current identity contracts, preserving one immutable authored model and all historical hash bytes. No generic second mathematical IR or project differentiation engine is introduced.

## Drivers

Preserve original scientific permission, explicit applicability, bounded lifetime and change locality while integrating the complete Plan 25m target. Mechanism substitution and pure policy testing must stay within their responsible owners.

## Options

Cold rebuilding avoids retention decisions but repeats expensive exclusion and does not solve continuity. A generic derived program wrapper overclaims derivatives. Select existing mathematical owners with explicit family and accuracy operations, library differentiation/factors and verifier-owned branch evidence.

## Outcome

Compiler/math preparation owns immutable enumerated families and separately bound anchors/parameters. Each declares reconstruction, original bounds/guards/inequalities/objective and selection obligations, complete actual incidence, actual derivative/action support, and terminal identity, conditional equivalence, block coverage or approximate correspondence. Auxiliary status, duals and certificates cannot transfer to the original. Reconstruct, correct where necessary, and assess with frozen original policy. Symbolica composes representable math; opaque oracles retain only their admitted partial/JVP/HVP support. Consumers demand certified bounds, permitted numerical estimates or unresolved handling explicitly; original physical/backward-error contracts are not silently converted to certified forward-error requirements. Key products by their consumed structure/value/source/profile/order/accuracy/branch dependencies with new versioned frames. Retain exclusion, uniqueness, numerical roots/jets and sheet transport independently. Same-point promotion reuses unchanged proof; nearby transport requires a uniform verified chart or certified common-root connections covering the whole segment. Endpoint box overlap or same regime cannot establish connectedness. Failed trials do not commit lineage.

### Composition, evidence and recorded-interpretation amendment

Plan 25n derives numerical incidence, execution/validity dependencies and objective/inequality
coupling as different projections of one authored model. Control-sensitive scheduling carries
validity without fictitious Jacobian entries. Complete original assessment follows block/tear
assembly, including all non-port unknowns. Empty retained rows are legitimate for objective-only
optimization or objective-free feasibility; complete reconstruction with no retained coordinates
is assessed directly, while underdetermined qualified-root requests refuse.

Existing ReconstructionOracle suppliers compose admitted local factories/projections, complete
maps, RHS realizations, sheet/action contracts and propagated accuracy. Structural BTF alone does
not establish a regular selected sheet. Preserve simultaneous execution or an initialization-only
proposal when that correspondence is absent. No synthetic regime ranking or second mathematical
IR is introduced. Actual evidence binds source, original coordinates/point or covered region,
derivative/action order, normalization, branch, allowance and evidence class; preparation support
cannot certify its future output. Product state and trace consume the same owned observations.

The coordinated current cutover uses SolveSettings v3, NumericalStrategyDocument v3 (version and
body), StudyOperation/StudyRequest v4, StudyDefinition v6 and JobPayload v8; the corresponding
strategy/request/preparation and durable request identities receive new frames. Header checks
precede current nested decoding. Historical bytes/readability stay intact; execution requires
explicit current readmission or typed refusal through the same executor, never implicit new Auto
interpretation. Internal binding migrates before public default activation. Native source revisions
invalidate only products consuming that build, not unrelated immutable evaluator mathematics.

### Consequences

Migrate producers and consumers together and remove replaced paths after targeted acceptance. Plan 25m retains its original packet/finding evidence; Plan 25n owns the automatic amendment and AF-01–AF-08. No parallel ledger is added.

Compiler view identity uses `pse.compiler.modeling-view.v4` for the complete consumed
profile, including class-proof work and assembly contribution, index and worker limits.
Historical v2/v3 frames retain their original bytes. Evaluator artifact identity remains
keyed by optimization and evaluation policy; assembly limits do not change an artifact
that does not consume them. Complete public preparation documents carry these typed
controls instead of relying on an unconfigurable compiler ceiling.

The historical Plan 25m preparation cutover changed serialized JSON contracts: operation
descriptors and study requests used version 2, study definitions version 4 and job
payloads version 6. The automatic amendment above defines the next coordinated current boundary. Their version boundaries are checked before decoding nested
current profiles. Unsupported historical readmission is an explicit typed refusal;
retained records and payload bytes are not rewritten or supplied with invented defaults.
SQL readiness fingerprints cover SQL declarations, not the schema of opaque JSON values.

### Compensating controls

Typed admission/refusal, finite work, actual native contract tests and independent original assessment constrain integration. No license restriction or hypothetical benchmark ranking excludes a selected library.

### Confirmation

**Proposed:** scoped decision review precedes changed-contract implementation. Prior source reviews establish the library/design basis, not acceptance or product qualification. Named checks are planned, not executed claims.

## Pros and cons

The target makes consequential distinctions explicit and permits isolated policy testing, at the cost of coordinated producer/consumer migration and foreign lifetime contracts.
