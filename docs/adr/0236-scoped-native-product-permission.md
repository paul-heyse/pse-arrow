# ADR-0236: Preserve original scope in native scientific product permissions

## Context

Completion identifies derived products by quantity alone and composes validity with aggregate permission. Protected exports accept caller-provided usability. Plan64c C04/C05 implements audited F02/F03 corrections under operator-authorized native delivery.

## Scope

Amend blueprint §21.1 and §21.5 for scoped facts and versioned durable interpretation. No new result store, permission service or scientific analysis family is selected.

## Drivers

An independently qualified step must survive a failed sibling without upgrading it. Structural diagnosis has task applicability without a physical candidate. Point products require original point, coverage and mathematical validity.

## Options

Quantity-only lookup aliases repeated products. Exporter-local reconstruction duplicates policy. Select completion-owned scope and reader-owned projection over original protected completion, retaining registry and transport owners.

## Outcome

Use explicit task or step/member/reference-point scope where required. Compose permission with that exact original scope. Bind exported usability to protected producer facts and actual coverage; caller metadata cannot strengthen it.

### Consequences

Migrate producers and local/durable/export consumers together. Increment changed completion and pack interpretation versions; unsupported historical unscoped facts cannot default to usable. No automatic stored-data rewrite is authorized.

Completion uses interpretation 3, stored completion 4, and review pack selection/request/index 3. Pack interpretation 2 could contain caller-asserted usability, so a current reader refuses it rather than promoting it merely because its wire fields look familiar.

Lazy covariance propagation attaches its original point, scientific-use decision and mathematical validity to the produced covariance row (relation interpretation 3), while the earlier immutable completion remains unchanged. Protected readback checks this producer fact against the original step/point. This uses the existing covariance result owner rather than a second permission ledger.

### Compensating controls

Retain unknown/withheld products and diagnostic access. Whole-request decisions remain distinct from independently scoped products. Generated projections follow the generator route.

### Confirmation

Targeted scope, failed-sibling, forged-label, protected-coordinate and historical-version controls precede assembled acceptance. No runtime qualification is claimed here.

## Pros and cons

One scientific decision owner prevents divergent publication interpretations; coordinated API and document migrations are required.

## More information

Plan64a A02/A03 and Plan64c C04/C05 own execution and evidence. RC04 is accepted direction; formal ADR status remains proposed.
