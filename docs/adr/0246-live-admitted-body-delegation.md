# ADR-0246: Delegate one admitted native root to its original private child

## Context

The private root child reconstructs source mathematics and consumes physical/proof replay
streams under the parent-bound request. This repeats potentially expanding normalization.
The live parent already holds the admitted body, owns scientific assessment and independently
verifies the child's proposal. Plan65e F03 separates transfer of that result from source replay.

## Scope

Amend blueprint §14.3/§18.8 for provider-free scalar/square implicit roots delegated to the
parent's original authenticated private child. This proposed target adds no general body
deserializer or durable artifact admission. ADR-0245's canonical membership/replay contract
remains unchanged; JSON v3 stays active pending complete replacement acceptance.

## Drivers

Retain authored/native scientific meaning and original ownership while removing repeated
source admission from the child. Transfer its execution/diagnostic closure; retain complete
original admission evidence with the parent through reap and verification. Use existing native
prepared-body stages and supplier mathematics, without a second mathematical IR.

## Options

Source replay preserves today's assurance but repeats normalization and proof-request
construction. A general allocation hook would need Numerica/GMP/MPFR, TLS workspaces, derivatives
and global symbols; a workspace hook alone is insufficient. Stock `Atom::read` lacks complete
packing checks, while ordinary import/rename normalizes again. Select checked supplier-native
transfer plus private complete-body restoration. This confines new work to validated native
representation and supported remapping, but changes the child's assurance to live-parent
delegation and leaves subsequent native preparation bounds separate.

## Outcome

A compiler-owned issuer accepts only its actual live admitted body. Seal its association with
the successful evidence while `Request::admit` has both. Bind complete native contents and
specification, physical interpretation, source/occurrence/token inventories and original evidence
commitments. Physical operations, physical boundaries and mathematical proofs have three
separately ordered sequences; preserve their versions, order and multiplicity without inventing
an interleaved chronology.

The parent retains its immutable receipt-bearing portable bytes and body through reap and
original-coordinate verification. No second decoded receipt tree is needed solely for
delegation. The child receives the complete native execution/diagnostic closure, original
bindings, intervals, residual identities, parameter bits, solve/evaluation limits and deadline,
plus attempt-bound evidence commitments. Registry/prerequisite reconstruction inputs remain
parent-side when no transferred stage needs an external resolver; otherwise transfer refuses.

Only the authenticated private handler issues a process-local, nonserializable one-attempt
execution capability, bound to channel, executable/build, request, native profile, deadline
and exact mathematical environment. Decoding is passive. The live issuer/endpoint supplies
authority; commitments detect substitution. No nested record receives replay authority, and
no descriptor grants readiness, scientific permission or publication. This route performs
no source or receipt replay.

### Consequences

Restore every native stage, domain guard, applicability plan, branch-continuity result,
quantity, effect and attribution inventory. `PreparedBody::compact` drops optional views/tokens
and is not a full restore API. Restoration must not call source admission, analysis,
differentiation or replacement. Later demanded support/derivative/evaluator preparation remains
separate native work, with its independent resource obligations and strict-memory refusal.

Add supplier-owned checked canonical transfer. Before unchecked packed views, validate tags/
flags, integer/length encodings, exact child/coefficient extents, parent exhaustion, denominator
validity, depth and arithmetic. Preserve actual exact-rational and MPFR Float realization,
including precision and both serialized components. Validate Float text/precision before later
unchecked Numerica conversion; rational-only support does not cover ordinary existing literals.

Map supported plain formals/fixed builtins to already registered roles with exact attributes.
Missing capacity, unsupported state/attributes/callbacks or mapping refuses. Numeric symbol
order alone is insufficient: canonical sums also compare little-endian packed bytes. Establish
canonical-order preservation or separately admitted bounded repair, with no ordinary normalize
or assumed allocation-free comparator fallback. Pre-admit input/output overlap, rewritten
headers/keys, traversal and scratch before growth under the original owner and cancellation.

### Compensating controls

Challenge malformed packing, missing/changed stages, substituted evidence/context, unsupported
state, packed-width/order boundaries, denied growth, cancellation and one-shot reuse. Show
restoration enters neither normalization nor source analysis. Preserve original proposal
screening and independent stop/reap/unknown-drain custody. At activation migrate the selected
private producer/receiver/callers and delete their replaced source-reconstruction mechanism
and tests together. Canonical replay retains its separate owner.

### Confirmation

Independent source assessment established that `compile_problem` needs the prepared body and
root controls; full receipt/registry consumption belongs to source reconstruction. This is
Interface-checked support for the decomposition. Supplier transfer, complete restore, child
activation, complete memory bounds, scientific qualification and measured benefit are unproven.
Plan65e owns implementation; formal decision acceptance remains separate.

## Pros and cons

The child can reuse completed parent admission without rebuilding source machinery. The price
is a complete native transfer profile, explicit compatibility and a tightly scoped capability.
Unsupported state and later derivative preparation cannot be concealed by successful decoding.
