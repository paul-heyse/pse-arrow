# ADR-0246: Delegate one admitted native root to its original private child

## Context

The private root child reconstructs source mathematics and consumes physical/proof replay
streams under the parent-bound request. This repeats potentially expanding normalization.
The live parent already holds the admitted body, owns scientific assessment and independently
verifies the child's proposal. Plan65k E03 separates execution delegation from source replay.
Further inspection establishes that the parent prepares the numerical product before encoding;
its unknown-coordinate First product already contains both Value and First programs.

## Scope

Amend blueprint §14.3/§18.8 for provider-free scalar/square implicit roots delegated to the
parent's original authenticated private child. This proposed target adds no general body
deserializer or durable artifact admission. ADR-0245's canonical membership/replay contract
remains unchanged; JSON v3 stays active pending complete replacement acceptance.

## Drivers

Retain authored/native scientific meaning and original ownership while removing repeated
source admission from the child. Transfer its execution/diagnostic closure; retain complete
original admission evidence with the parent through reap and verification. Use existing native
compiled stages and supplier mathematics, without a second mathematical interpreter or IR.

## Options

Source replay preserves today's assurance but repeats normalization and proof-request
construction. A general allocation hook would need Numerica/GMP/MPFR, TLS workspaces, derivatives
and global symbols; a workspace hook alone is insufficient. Checked prepared-atom transfer
would also require coefficient/state restoration, canonical remapping and later preparation.
Symbolica 3.0.1 serializes `ExpressionEvaluator<T>`, but stock deserializers do not validate
indices before unchecked execution and can restore symbolic settings/external-function state.
Select supplier-owned checked transfer of the existing f64 instruction program and complete
native compiled-stage restoration. Preserve the parent's numerical realization and remove
child admission, analysis, differentiation and coefficient reconstruction for this fixed
profile. Stock decoding, machine-code transfer and a project-owned arithmetic interpreter
are not selected.

## Outcome

A compiler-owned issuer accepts only its actual live admitted body. Seal its association with
the successful evidence while `Request::admit` has both. Bind complete native contents and
specification, physical interpretation, source/occurrence/token inventories and original evidence
commitments. Physical operations, physical boundaries and mathematical proofs have three
separately ordered sequences; preserve their versions, order and multiplicity without inventing
an interleaved chronology.

The parent retains its immutable receipt-bearing portable bytes and body through reap and
original-coordinate verification. No second decoded receipt tree is needed solely for
delegation. The child receives the complete unknown-coordinate Value/First execution/diagnostic
closure, original bindings, intervals, residual identities, parameter bits, structural Jacobian
support, solve/evaluation limits and deadline, plus attempt-bound evidence commitments. The
single selected-partials First template contains the required Value programs. Registry,
source-value preparation, factorization/backsolve machinery and later product preparation
stay parent-side. Any recursively encountered provider or external-function requirement refuses.

Only the authenticated private handler issues a process-local, nonserializable one-attempt
execution capability, bound to channel, executable/build, request, native profile, deadline
and exact mathematical environment. Decoding is passive. The live issuer/endpoint supplies
authority; commitments detect substitution. No nested record receives replay authority, and
no descriptor grants readiness, scientific permission or publication. This route performs
no source or receipt replay.

### Consequences

Separate the existing immutable execution template from its preparation owner and mutable
worker scratch. Source-bearing callers keep their original binding checks; they and the child
use the same stage engine. Extract the fixed root execution core used by KINSOL and verification
from general `Problem` preparation/factor machinery. Do not make preparation optional throughout
the generic body or manufacture a dummy `PreparedBody`.

Restore every compiled Block/Require/Domain/Applicability/Branch field, including gather/scatter,
finite-check ranges, captures, layouts, lineage, predicates, plans and branch separation. Preserve
ordered residuals, coordinates, CSC support and complete instance/member/source/occurrence
attribution. Issue the parent's execution-attribution projection only after the existing
`SourceBindings::check_compiled` succeeds. The child binds that projection to this attempt;
it gains no general source-binding witness. Reconstruct and poison fresh mutable frames/buffers
under existing reset rules; do not transmit warmed working state.

The supplier profile preserves exact f64 constant bits, instruction/operand order, stack extent,
parameter/constant boundary and result aliases. Validate every index, nonempty Add/Mul,
destination protection, definition-before-read, fixed builtin discriminant/arity, extent and
encoding exhaustion before constructing the existing evaluator. Refuse external functions,
callbacks and unsupported control flow; omit preparation-only settings/hot-start expression
trees. Verify actual emitted instruction coverage before selecting that first profile.

Pre-admit actual input/output overlap, programs, layouts, nested stages, attribution and fresh
worker/scratch allocations under the original owner; serialized counts cannot grant allowances.
Preserve original admission/cancellation errors. Requested backing admission is not an RSS or
whole-solve resource bound. Packed-atom inspection is independent passive functionality;
canonical atom remapping is not needed for this child route.

### Compensating controls

Challenge malformed program indices/control flow, missing/changed stages/layouts/attribution,
asymmetric sparse Jacobians, substituted evidence/context, denied growth, cancellation and
one-shot reuse. Show restoration enters neither normalization, source analysis, differentiation
nor MPFR restoration. Preserve original proposal
screening and independent stop/reap/unknown-drain custody. At activation migrate the selected
private producer/receiver/callers and delete their replaced source-reconstruction mechanism
and tests together. Canonical replay retains its separate owner.

### Confirmation

Independent assessment follows actual KINSOL residual/Jacobian/JVP callbacks and `Problem::verify`
to the unknown worker; selected-partials compilation includes Value through First. The fixed
numeric stage engine does not need the original symbolic body. This is Interface-checked
support, not implementation. Checked supplier loading, native execution/preparation separation,
complete restore, child activation, complete memory bounds, scientific qualification and measured
benefit remain unproven. Plan65k owns implementation; formal acceptance remains separate.

## Pros and cons

The child reuses completed parent preparation, numerical conversion and operation order. The
price is complete compiled-stage/layout validation, execution-attribution issuance and a scoped
capability. Further derivative demands are explicitly unsupported in this profile; decoding
cannot conceal that limit. Native ownership separation remains substantial work even though
supplier transfer is narrower than prepared-atom restoration.
