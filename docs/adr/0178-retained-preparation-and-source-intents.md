# ADR-0178: Retained preparation and explicit source intents

## Context

Plan 36 RC01–RC05/RC20 select typed numerical intent, complete incremental source admission and retained preparation under explicit ownership. The selected target preserves ADR-0164's scientific authority, independent receiving qualification, exact canonical settlement, unsafe compiler/store trust assertions and controlled Linux loader profile. Repeating those obligations at unchanged internal crossings adds work without adding a new trust boundary.

## Scope

Refine the affected preparation/receiving clauses of ADR-0164 and add the selected Rust/Python source intents and compiler ownership. Its unrelated canonical, lifecycle, historical receipt, native ABI and scientific meanings remain. ADR status and any supersession bookkeeping follow the decision-PR route. P-A1–A7 and adjacent P-D2 own implementation. Optional publication R36-03 is unselected: preparation still synchronously settles its promised canonical effects before success.

## Drivers

A value change should reuse structure while retaining exact current lineage. Complete premises are shared immutable content, not permission. Compilation should invalidate its consumed dependencies and publish one coherent admitted result. Context observations describe their actual time and role.

## Options

Whole-package JSON edits, repeated preparation and per-body complete receipt encoding retain redundant work. Caching permission flags, stat-only receiving identity or optional publication would weaken or change the selected contracts. Select explicit intent and bounded held engagement with exact renewal and settlement.

### Consequences

Migrate actual Rust/Python/study consumers and generated DTOs together. Payload/receipt schema activation is an explicit controlled operation: fence writers, settle uncertain old effects, snapshot reachable history/borrowers, materialize derived records preserving bytes/predicates/provenance, verify against the old oracle, then activate all consumers together. Interrupted backfill retains old authority. Undecodable history refuses activation. No production activation/deletion is authorized by writing or implementing this target; fixture controls precede a separately reviewable production operation. After activation retain one production route, not dual writes or a compatibility backend.

### Compensating controls

Charge retained input/products and scratch before allocation. Every new value and trust transition retains full relevant admission. Unknown dependencies invalidate conservatively. Physical specialization and independent original assessment cannot be replaced by raw fixture extraction or cached validity. Explicit full export/replacement remains available to its real consumers.

### Confirmation

Proposed target reconciled from the approved plan and completed investigations, with independent bounded target review accepted. Real migrated-source controls and P-G1 qualification remain open; source inspection or prototype controls do not close original findings.

## Pros and cons

Explicit source intent and owned engagement localize change and repeated work. Complete dependency and lifecycle ownership is essential; conservative fallback may retain work where a narrower premise is not established.
