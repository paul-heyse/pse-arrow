# ADR-0264: Qualify numerical execution products independently of symbolic recipes

## Context

Plan73b B05 requires a real numerical-only receiver. The existing process-local `ArtifactRequest`
already carries `PreparedSupport`, while canonical admitted-body acquisition reconstructs an
`AdmittedBody`. Neither can provide a cold execution hit without prior symbolic construction.
Interface evidence is the compiler's existing
[`BodyInput` and planned instances](../../crates/pse-compiler/src/workspace/modeling/executable/grouped.rs),
[`prepare_modeling_observations`](../../crates/pse-compiler/src/workspace/modeling/executable/solve.rs)
and the current [canonical recipe receiver](../../crates/pse-runtime/src/math/portable.rs).

## Scope

Amend blueprint §14.3 and §20.5 with a separately interpreted numerical product. Reuse the
existing canonical store, selected revisions/dependencies, qualified producer classes,
publication owner, leases, flights and retirement epochs. Existing identities and generic
framing/commit rules keep their meaning; this adds distinct execution request/payload domains.

## Drivers

Prepare mathematical structure once and bind new numerical points through private workers.
Keep compiler-issued scientific meaning separate from encoded instructions, allocation facts,
process cache signatures and mutable operation controls. Pure observation acquisition is the
initial caller; later derivative/class construction continues through symbolic acquisition.

## Options

Recipe replay retains source eligibility but repeats work an execution-only caller does not
need. Renaming a compiled body or blessing its local cache hash cannot establish durable source
custody. Select the supplier's checked f64 program plus original layouts/control schedule inside
a protected compiler-qualified execution description. Source-independent native compilation may
consume those numerical stages locally; native binaries and producer shared-object paths are not
transferred. Keep one selected acquisition route per required capability, without fallback after
corrupt/incompatible present products or refused construction.

## Outcome

### Consequences

The compiler seals passive execution request/custody at the existing `BodyInput`/planned-instance
boundary, before materializing `AdmittedBody`. Its semantic body identity, deployment physical
meaning, ordered output/coordinate/product request and original source/guard/instance role maps
remain distinct from the optional local build recipe. The controlled producer binds realized
layouts/pairs, coefficient realization, instruction/head semantics and exact payload to that
request after successful demanded preparation and a final stop check.

Use new separately named/versioned framed request and payload meanings. A canonical product key
also binds the distinct interpretation, qualified producer key/class and complete protected
payload under the existing canonical dependency selection. A process-local numerical key is
never promoted to durable receiving authority. Absent compiler semantic identity or complete
original custody refuses publication.

Acquire a checked immutable numerical template and validated passive execution binding. Fresh
workers receive current scope and independently admitted backing; no source-bearing wrapper,
`ScalarProducts`, producer cancellation, warmed scratch or implicit later-symbolic eligibility
is fabricated. The pure observation/property caller selects this capability before source
construction. A miss may complete the existing admitted source frontier; a present invalid hit
is a typed refusal. Retire the migrated caller's mandatory symbolic preparation/reader branch.

### Compensating controls

Supplier passive profile/dataflow inspection precedes materialization. Preserve original
scientific source/guard custody, ordered coordinates, selected symmetric pairs, physical affine
transport and per-instance failures. Allocation extents and serialized limits grant no resource
permission. Receiving uses actual current ownership and retains input/restored overlap through
drain. Later symbolic demand uses its separately qualified original recipe/custody route.

### Confirmation

Plan73b B05 and Plan73c C03 own production acceptance. The current math transfer prerequisites
are targeted evidence only: cold immutable workers, Second layouts and stable fixed-head
instruction transfer do not themselves establish canonical scientific receiving. Require the
actual observation caller, fresh receiver/retired producer and independent tiny/extreme
numerical controls before claiming that outcome.

## Pros and cons

The split removes symbolic reconstruction from successful execution-only acquisition and keeps
one mathematical execution implementation. It requires a genuine pre-materialization compiler
descriptor, scientific custody validation and an execution-only assembly path. These obligations
cannot be replaced by matching bytes or introducing a new type name.
