# Scientific packages and equipment delivery

Source: [Notion](https://app.notion.com/p/40b141b583698153afabc7c9f833ef79). Imported 2026-11-02.

[Implementation design and IDAES equivalence plan](index.md)

**Adopted scientific delivery contracts** · 2026-11-01 · Inspected repository basis: `19e7b49a67df24c1bf73005a6186e145e930d53a`. The contracts govern selected scientific increments. Source inspection establishes the starting point; no new numerical qualification is reported.

## 1. Extend the authored scientific library

The process domain should make the existing scientific contracts accessible and composable. Chemistry, property methods, parameter records, state definitions, reactions and equipment already live in authored `.pse` packages. Generic Rust owners provide admission, specialization, mathematics, execution and results. Keep that division: new process science belongs in packages unless a selected example demonstrates a missing generic mechanism.

The adopted [Materials, states, and physical models](../process/materials-states-and-physical-models.md) and [Cross-industry domain contracts](../process/cross-industry-contracts.md) govern the selected packages. Preserve material identity versus characterization and representation, exact chemistry versus empirical evidence, measurement/balance bases and population or polymer structure where the consumer requires them. Current molecular/molar liquid–vapor seeds must not become universal restrictions. Start with authored scientific records, axes, functions and relations; extend a generic owner only for a demonstrated missing meaning. This changes the implementation constraint, not the IDAES acceptance population.

The baseline already includes ideal and Peng–Robinson potentials, SRK family/kappa records, NRTL excess-Gibbs/activity expressions, nonassociating PC-SAFT, caloric correlations, FTPx/FPhx and dilute-liquid bindings, equilibrium variants and saponification reaction projection. These are starting implementations to extend and qualify, not abstractions to recreate. Scientific records and applicability are already admitted through `properties.selection_context`, existing record families and declared requirements. [Physical semantics](https://github.com/paul-heyse/pse-arrow/blob/19e7b49a67df24c1bf73005a6186e145e930d53a/docs/authoritative_design/sections/physical-semantics.md); [property selection](https://github.com/paul-heyse/pse-arrow/blob/19e7b49a67df24c1bf73005a6186e145e930d53a/packages/reference/domain/models/properties.pse).

## 2. Thermodynamic and property delivery contracts

### Material identity and scientific data

A selected material context must preserve component/phase identities, phase membership, composition and charge knowledge, and the physical bases used by its consumers. Elemental conservation requires an explicit complete composition claim; unknown pseudo-components remain usable where no such claim is required. Keep component identity distinct from display order, name and formula string.

Property selection must identify the actual method, parameterization, subject tuple, variant, reference conventions and admitted dependencies. Preserve ordered versus symmetric interactions, joint-fit groups and explicit predictive rules. Missing interaction data must not become zero by default. Applicability statements retain whether they are fitted, recommended, validated, reported or unknown; extrapolation permission does not upgrade evidence or override mathematical domains.

Retain the selected input records and their generating meaning: measured data, fitted parameters, predictions, correlations and applicability evidence are distinct. Where observations enter a case or fit, record quantity/basis, sampling or aggregation interval, uncertainty/error assumptions and missing-data treatment; an interval average is not automatically a pointwise state. Shared fitted parameters retain their identity across experiments, while fit data and independent prediction evidence retain separate uses. Use existing physical-context, scientific-record, case, fitting and result owners. A later plant-data adapter maps acquisition time to process time and external sensor/equipment references to model occurrences; this does not require a historian or new observation database now.

### Property methods and thermodynamic consistency

Extend the current cubic, activity, caloric and pure-property families through their existing interfaces and data selection. PR's potential-derived fugacity and its explicit IDAES ordered-row convention remain distinct variants. NRTL activity responses should continue to derive from the extensive excess-Gibbs potential. PC-SAFT association/polar extensions are separate scientific additions to the current nonassociating implementation.

Each method delivers demanded physical outputs, declared derivative availability and validity, plus independent checks appropriate to its claims: pressure and chemical-potential identities, heat-capacity/enthalpy/entropy consistency, Gibbs–Duhem or other applicable relations. A value comparison at supplied density does not establish a flash, phase envelope or stable equilibrium. [Thermodynamic source conventions](https://github.com/paul-heyse/pse-arrow/blob/19e7b49a67df24c1bf73005a6186e145e930d53a/packages/reference/thermodynamics/sources.md); [NRTL source contract](https://github.com/paul-heyse/pse-arrow/blob/19e7b49a67df24c1bf73005a6186e145e930d53a/packages/reference/methods/models/nrtl.pse).

### State definitions, equilibrium and transport

A state definition declares independent coordinates, composition reconstruction, supplied/receiving responsibility and demanded outputs. Extend the present FTPx/FPhx bindings to the additional coordinate families required by selected reference cases. The seed's single liquid/vapor split must not become a universal phase representation. New families need physical round-trip comparisons and transport correspondence, including zero-flow or disappearing-phase behavior where promised.

Separate state coordinates from phase-equilibrium equations and from numerical realization. SmoothVLE, complementarity and nested closure have different approximation and branch contracts. Require the intended phase/root selection and the appropriate stability evidence; neither a start nor local convergence supplies a global-equilibrium guarantee. Near branch coalescence, values may remain available while local derivatives are withheld.

Ports consume advected component and enthalpy flows, with explicit heat/work exchanges. Equal units alone do not establish compatible species, basis or datum. Mixers impose balance/mixing equations; translators impose an explicit scientific mapping. A direct property-expression consumer should demand only the relationship it needs, using existing lazy specialization rather than constructing an unnecessary full state. [Current state contracts](https://github.com/paul-heyse/pse-arrow/blob/19e7b49a67df24c1bf73005a6186e145e930d53a/packages/reference/thermodynamics/models/equilibrium.pse).

Substitution is qualified for the requested use: required observations, material representation, phase/root convention, validity region, reference datum, accuracy, derivative order, initialization and effects. Equal ports do not make a detailed model and a surrogate interchangeable for internal fields, optimization curvature or transient memory. Use existing demanded-property and native-capability mechanisms; value, first-derivative, second-order and global-certification eligibility remain separate. Changing EOS or constitutive science is an explicit model change. Replacing the implementation of the same model instead preserves its stated observations within the declared contract, without requiring identical internal variables or algorithms. [Physical contract owner](https://github.com/paul-heyse/pse-arrow/blob/19e7b49a67df24c1bf73005a6186e145e930d53a/docs/authoritative_design/sections/physical-semantics.md); [Provider capabilities](https://github.com/paul-heyse/pse-arrow/blob/19e7b49a67df24c1bf73005a6186e145e930d53a/crates/pse-kernels/src/provider.rs).

### Transport and external property implementations

Viscosity, conductivity, diffusion and other transport families are new scientific coverage. Each needs its source data, mixture/phase rules, applicability and a real consuming unit. General multiparameter Helmholtz/CoolProp capability also remains new work; the existing authored residual-Helmholtz framework is not that capability.

Use an authored formulation or the existing generic provider boundary according to the selected implementation. External implementations must supply typed coordinates, exact implementation/data identities, shapes, derivative and smoothness contracts, validity and typed failure behavior. Declare whether they expose equations, evaluate an input/output relation or advance state in time, using the lifecycle distinctions in [Architecture and process authoring](architecture-and-process-authoring.md). Do not infer time-advance, rollback or event synchronization from an ordinary provider call, or hide initialization, conversion and branch switching inside a “package works” claim. A missing binary, unavailable license or unsupported derivative remains a named unmet requirement. Inspect suitable native-library capabilities before adding numerical integration machinery; native libraries retain numerical iteration, globalization and factorization, with bounded scientific composition only for an actual consumed-contract gap. [Provider boundary](https://github.com/paul-heyse/pse-arrow/blob/19e7b49a67df24c1bf73005a6186e145e930d53a/crates/pse-kernels/src/provider.rs); [Current scope](https://github.com/paul-heyse/pse-arrow/blob/19e7b49a67df24c1bf73005a6186e145e930d53a/docs/authoritative_design/sections/scope-and-open-design.md#section-25).

## 3. Reaction and equipment contracts

A reaction package delivers authoritative stoichiometry, participating species/phases, extent normalization, rate or equilibrium law, parameter records and thermal convention. Extend the current checked `reactions.Projection`; reactors should not replace it with arbitrary per-component source callbacks. Compare elemental and charge closure independently where required. Distinguish kinetic, equilibrium and declared yield/lumped transformations. Reaction heat must be consistent with the property package's formation/sensible convention, preventing omitted or double-counted energy. Ionic speciation and true/apparent-component mappings require their own equations and evidence; generic charge/composition metadata is not an electrolyte engine. [Reaction projection](https://github.com/paul-heyse/pse-arrow/blob/19e7b49a67df24c1bf73005a6186e145e930d53a/packages/reference/thermodynamics/models/reactions.pse).

An equipment definition delivers ports and internal states, demanded property/reaction contracts, geometry and constitutive equations, selectable balance assumptions, specification freedoms, optional inventories and initialization recommendations. It also declares the configurations it supports. Compose balances and reusable submodels where appropriate; a boundary source, algebraic junction or other memoryless device need not contain a storage control volume.

Current 0D balances support multiple material bases, but dynamic stored transport restricts phase allocation and inventory projections. Add multiphase holdup, phase-transfer terms and elemental/total inventory projections explicitly when required. The 1D seed is spatial and refuses temporal dynamics without accumulation closure. Extend distributed balances with boundary conditions, geometry, local transfer laws and mesh-refinement evidence; an available discretization engine does not supply these physical equations. [Lumped volumes](https://github.com/paul-heyse/pse-arrow/blob/19e7b49a67df24c1bf73005a6186e145e930d53a/packages/reference/process/models/control-volumes.pse); [distributed volume](https://github.com/paul-heyse/pse-arrow/blob/19e7b49a67df24c1bf73005a6186e145e930d53a/packages/reference/process/models/control-volume-1d.pse).

Initialization recommendations should select existing staged, continuation and recycle mechanisms. Temporary fixes, simplified property laws and tear values remain procedure-local overlays; the accepted result must satisfy the restored original specification. Record the selected branch and actual start separately. A successful initialization stage is not acceptance of the complete unit or flowsheet.

Each equipment increment should expose at least its intended simulation and design specifications. For example, specifying exchanger area and solving duty differs from specifying an outlet temperature and solving area; changing one role should preserve the same physical definition. Check structural closure after composition, and retain diagnostics for inconsistent or redundant specifications. Degrees of freedom count alone does not prove solvability or identify a singular physical configuration. This provides a practical substitutability test: exchange a compatible state/property implementation, then verify the unit still consumes the promised flows, energy and derivative contracts under the same engineering problem. [Initialization and numerical ownership](https://github.com/paul-heyse/pse-arrow/blob/19e7b49a67df24c1bf73005a6186e145e930d53a/docs/authoritative_design/sections/numerical-execution.md#section-17).

### Engineering assessment scopes

When a selected case includes costing or environmental assessment, author that assessment over identified physical outputs and declare any coupling into objectives or constraints. Retain the equipment/flow boundary, period or campaign, utilization, currency and base date, factor provenance, allocation and functional unit where applicable. Capital, annual operating cost, physical emission and characterized environmental impact are different results. Internal transfers, shared utilities and intermediate coproducts must not be counted twice when the boundary changes. Keep prices and impact factors out of conserved material identity. The current exchanger cost correlation remains a scoped sourced model; its extrapolation permissions do not establish a qualified purchase-price estimate. Broader accounting packages enter with selected P3/P6 consumers, not as new P1 requirements or a comprehensive finance/life-cycle database. [Current costing owner](https://github.com/paul-heyse/pse-arrow/blob/19e7b49a67df24c1bf73005a6186e145e930d53a/packages/reference/process/models/costing.pse).

### Proposed sourcing and catalog workflow

[Scientific sourcing and catalog architecture — review and proposed detail](scientific-sourcing-and-catalog.md) records the user's direction for a targeted Rust scientific pipeline within pse-arrow, with canonical scientific content in its existing SurrealDB instance. It reuses native typing, admission, source revisions, publication, research and evidence owners, and specifies derived discovery views. Unit definitions are part of this domain-design exercise: reusable physical structures and typed constitutive closures support familiar engineering templates and explicit configurations. Database-backed typed definitions share the existing authored semantics. The chapter distinguishes retained sources, admitted native science and use-specific qualification; its detailed delivery plan remains proposed.

## 4. Scientific dependencies within the implementation sequence

The implementation sequence is P0–P7 in the companion roadmap. P1 uses existing coupled cases to establish authoring and evidence paths. P2 supplies property/state/reaction capabilities required by the case portfolio. P3 completes generic equipment; P4 supplies missing inventory and distributed physics. P5 delivers specialized families, and P6 completes analysis/application workflows before P7 closes the declared scope.

Scientific dependency, rather than a universal package-completion gate, determines the order. Steam and supercritical-fluid properties unlock pump/turbine and power cases. Electrolyte/speciation and transport methods unlock rate-based capture; gas/solid states and heterogeneous reactions unlock contactors and chemical looping. Temporal distributed accumulation unlocks the corresponding transient families. New surrogate artifacts require their own inputs, outputs, validity and derivative contracts before equation embedding or optimization.

Each selected case should bring its smallest complete scientific dependency closure. Independent families can proceed concurrently once shared contracts are stable. Costing and data selection accompany their consuming models. An installed external library or a method name in the catalog does not complete a dependency until the demanded values, derivatives, physical conventions and failure behavior are available and qualified.

## 5. Definition of a completed scientific increment

Each increment supplies authored definitions and source-attributed records; explicit supported configurations and refusals; one useful consuming process case; and selected independent value, derivative, conservation, branch and workflow evidence. Reuse existing conformance fixtures, native reference exchanges, completion assessments and retained lineage. Preserve original failed, unavailable and unattempted outcomes. No new evidence database or parallel qualification authority is required.

This makes separation of concerns operational: scientific packages own meaning, generic mathematics owns realization, native workflows own execution, and existing outcomes own evidence. Substitution is conditional on the actual physical and derivative contracts; composability does not erase reference conventions or validity. Historical or focused passing evidence remains scoped to its exercised cases. Broader acceptance and current Plan53/58 obligations stay with their existing owners. [Validation ownership](https://github.com/paul-heyse/pse-arrow/blob/19e7b49a67df24c1bf73005a6186e145e930d53a/docs/authoritative_design/sections/operations-and-validation.md#section-24-2).
