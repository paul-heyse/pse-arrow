# Solution: what comes back

Source: [Notion](https://app.notion.com/p/40a141b58369812d9b39cd86ab96540b). Imported 2026-11-02.

**Version 3** · 2026-11-01

A Solution records the outcome of one submitted SimulationProblem and one actual Run. Its primary values and diagnostics use model identities and readable engineering paths. Numerical artifacts remain available in provenance for inspection.

## Outcome and members

A successful solve may contain one or more roots, optima, or other requested members. A failed or infeasible run may contain no solution member, or clearly marked partial candidates. Status must not manufacture a trustworthy member simply to satisfy a fixed cardinality.

Each returned value, derivative, dual, statistic, or analysis carries its definition, coverage, and qualification. Connection multipliers are not intrinsically economic stream prices; that interpretation requires a relevant objective, conventions, and normalization.

## Representation and evidence

Trajectories and fields include their reconstruction basis, valid intervals/regions, discontinuities, boundary conventions, and any undefined portions. A partial trajectory is not claimed to cover the full requested horizon.

Structural checks, numerical diagnostics, statistical estimates, and certificates are distinct evidence. “Converged” does not by itself mean globally optimal, globally stable, uniquely identifiable, or physically accurate.

A failure diagnostic names the engineering origin as far as evidence allows and retains relevant numerical detail. Unknown causes remain explicitly unresolved.

## Provenance and reuse

Provenance identifies the problem, submission, actual run, selected definitions/data/evaluators, transformations, numerical settings, versions, seeds, and relevant environment. It supports the declared reproducibility level without promising bitwise equality on every equivalent machine.

Reconstruction may be partial. Values and sensitivities are returned only where their meaning survives the transformation chain. A Solution can feed a later Specification as a binding, guess, dataset, or uncertainty description through an explicit alignment contract.
