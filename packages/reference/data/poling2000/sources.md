# Poling 2000 parameter banks

The benzene critical bank transcribes Table A.1, row 187, in Appendix A of the fifth
edition of *The Properties of Gases and Liquids*: Tc 562.05 K, Pc 48.95 bar and omega
0.210. Stored pressure is normalized to 4895000 Pa. These are critical-property values;
the separate vapor-pressure correlation table has different fitted critical inputs.
[The table](https://www.eng.uc.edu/~beaucag/Classes/ChEThermoBeaucage/Quizzes2020/Books/Bruce%20E.%20Poling,%20John%20M.%20Prausnitz,%20John%20P.%20O'Connell%20-%20The%20properties%20of%20gases%20and%20liquids-McGraw-Hill%20Professional%20(2000).pdf)
retains the central CAS identity 71-43-2. The DM2 fixture selects this bank alongside
IDAES's independently attributed Reid 1987 values, without redefining benzene.
